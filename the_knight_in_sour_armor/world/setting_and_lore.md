# Setting and Lore: Mizunara Keep & The Eastern Marches

---

## 1. Geography & The Frontier Realm

### Mizunara Keep
Mizunara Keep stands as a massive fortress-city of grey slate, river stone, and black pine timber, perched above the rushing rapids of the Mizunara River in the Eastern Marches of the Kingdom of Ouka. Originally constructed two centuries ago as a military redoubt against subterranean incursions, it has swelled into an overcrowded boomtown powered by the Adventurer Guild economy. 

The city is tiered into three distinct strata:
1. **The Upper Bastion**: Where highborn guild arbiters, royal tax inquisitors, and nobility reside behind polished limestone parapets, dining on imported white rice, salted sea trout, and plum wine.
2. **The Mid-Tier Guild Row**: Wide flagstone avenues flanked by armories, runic forges, apothecary shops, and guild administrative halls. Here, silver and gold-ranked adventurers parade their gilded armor and enchanted blades before breathless commoners.
3. **The Lower Mire (The Gutters)**: A sprawling labyrinth of waterlogged timber shanties, muddy drainage trenches, and cheap tallow-lit taverns clinging to the outer ditch of the fortress walls. It is here that broken novices, discarded porters, crippled vanguards, and indebted laborers bleed out in the rain, forgotten the moment their bodies fail.

### The Surrounding Wilderness
- **The Karasuma Stone Quarries**: An abandoned, labyrinthine basin of jagged granite cuts, rotted derrick towers, and water-filled sinkholes five leagues north of the city. Once mined for fortress stone, it is now an isolated death trap inhabited by scavenger hounds and used by unscrupulous guild parties for clandestine executions.
- **The Iron Ravine**: A treacherous volcanic cleft four leagues east of Mizunara, rich in blood-iron ore and guarded by territorial obsidian-plated carapace beasts and subterranean rock-hewers.
- **The Tanizaki Valley**: A destitute agrarian basin five days south, where tenant farmers harvest coarse millet and barley under heavy guild grain quotas. This is the impoverished birthplace of Kazuki and Saki.

## 2. Guild Economics & The Exploitation of the Vanguard

### The Guild Ledger System
The Mizunara Guild operates not as a benevolent alliance of heroes, but as a ruthless commercial syndicate. Adventurers are ranked from Wood, Iron, Bronze, Silver, to Gold. 
- High-tier ranks grant tax exemptions, aristocratic dining rights, and access to lucrative royal subjugation bounties.
- Lower-tier ranks bear the full cost of guild overhead: filing fees, mandatory healing draught tariffs, equipment repair charges, and hazardous expedition insurance.

### The Vanguard Trap
In the party structure of the Eastern Marches, vanguard shield-bearers are systematically exploited:
- Offensive casters (like Reiko) and flashy sword-masters (like Takumi) require pristine gear and uninterrupted casting times to execute grandiose, high-scoring strikes.
- The vanguard must anchor the frontline, absorbing concussive blunt-force impacts, acid splatters, and beast venom.
- Because enchanted shields and heavy plate mail degrade rapidly under concussive stress, the vanguard bears exorbitant maintenance costs.
- When chronic trauma inevitably ruins the vanguard's knees, spine, and lungs, parties discard them to avoid splitting bounty shares with a "declining asset," falsely reporting them as "fallen heroically in battle" to claim death benefits and preserve party prestige.

## 3. The Crossover Intrusion: The Blue Box

### The TARDIS
To the denizens of Mizunara Keep, the TARDIS appears as a wooden sentry box of alien blue paint, marked with strange white glyphs (*POLICE PUBLIC CALL BOX*), smelling faintly of wet cedar, ozone, and old library parchment. Its physical dimensions defy Euclidean geometry, opening into a cavernous, roundel-studded cathedral of humming chronal engines, brass consoles, and glowing time rotors.

### The Twelfth Doctor
A solitary traveler across time and space, currently in his silver-haired, sharp-featured incarnation. He carries no weapons, only a sonic screwdriver and a chalkboard. His presence is an ontological anomaly in this high-fantasy realm: he views magical incantations as undiscovered physical frequencies, guild hierarchies as transparent feudal exploitation, and the casual cruelty of adventurer parties as a profound moral failure demanding his intervention.
