# Character Dossiers: *The Knight in Sour Armor*

---

## 1. Kazuki (一樹) — *The Discarded Bulwark / The Knight in Sour Armor*

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Kazuki of Tanizaki, formerly known as *The Iron Anvil*; later known across the Eastern Marches simply as *The Knight in Sour Armor*.
- **Archetype**: Frontline defensive bulwark / heavy tower shield master.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Born to tenant barley farmers in the famine-struck Tanizaki basin. Starvation and landlord bailiffs formed his earliest memories. When he and his childhood friend Saki fled to Mizunara Keep to become adventurers, he took an oath to never let her go hungry or suffer harm. For five grueling years, Kazuki served as the party's literal meat-shield, absorbing charging tusk-beasts, venomous wyrm stings, and concussive stone-hammer blows. His right knee was shattered and poorly set; two floating rib fractures never healed; chronic manticore venom blackened his forearms and restricted his breath.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want (Beginning)*: To be acknowledged, valued, and loved by Saki and the party he forged from the mud.
  - *Internal Need*: To understand that self-sacrifice without boundaries is self-destruction; to discover self-worth independent of being a tool for selfish narcissists.
- **Core Wound / Misbelief**: "My body is only worth the blows it stops. If I stop bleeding for them, I deserve to be discarded."
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Chronic self-erasure and martyrdom; mistaking parasitic exploitation for comradeship.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: Lying face-down in the freezing mud outside the tavern, hearing the woman he bled for laughing at his expense inside the warm light.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Subconsciously testing the weight on his right knee; rolling his scarred shoulder when sizing up terrain.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Sparse, measured, low-toned. Speaks with quiet frontier pragmatism; initially hesitant, growing into calm, unshakeable moral certainty under the Doctor's tutelage.
- **Internal Contradiction**: Deeply disillusioned with the greed and cruelty of mortals, yet fundamentally incapable of walking past a suffering person without offering his shield.
- **Evolution**: Guided by the Twelfth Doctor, Kazuki recovers his health and dignity. He rejects petty vengeance, instead embracing the grim, stubborn duty of a true protector: protecting others not for fame, gold, or gratitude, but simply because *if he doesn't, nobody else will*.

## 2. The Twelfth Doctor — *The Timeless Wanderer / The Oncoming Storm*

- **Full Name & Epithet**: The Doctor (Twelfth Incarnation).
- **Appearance**: Silver-haired, gaunt, severe facial contours, piercing blue-grey eyes beneath heavy, expressive brows ("attack eyebrows"). Dressed in a dark navy velvet frock coat with crimson silk lining, white linen shirt, dark trousers, and sturdy black leather boots.
- **Lived Background & Cosmic Grief**: Over two thousand years of travel, battle, loss, and regeneration. A survivor of the Time War and the siege of Trenzalore. Having spent four and a half billion years punching through a diamond wall out of sheer defiance of death, he carries an unimaginable depth of pain, cloaked behind dry, caustic cynicism, sharp impatience, and razor-sharp intellect.
- **Mannerisms & Tactile Tics**: Pacing with long strides; snapping his fingers; waving a chalkboard or scribbling formulas on walls; tapping his sonic screwdriver against his chin; glaring fiercely when anyone acts with performative sentimentality; retreating to tea and electric guitar when processing cosmic grief.
- **Speech Register**: Sharp, dry, rolling northern cadence with hard consonants that snap like shears, fast, professorial, scathing toward hypocrites and tyrants, yet infinitely gentle with the broken and dying. Highly allergic to hugs ("Never hug someone, it's just a way to hide your face").
- **Philosophical Anchor**: 
  > *"Never be cruel, never be cowardly. Hate is always foolish, and love is always wise. Always try to be nice, but never fail to be kind."*
- **Role in the Narrative**: Gruff, fiercely protective mentor. He nurses Kazuki's broken body, challenges his martyr delusions, and teaches him how systems of exploitation work. But when Kazuki's former companions attempt to murder him in cold blood, the Doctor drops his eccentric facade and demonstrates why the universe trembles before the Fury of the Time Lord.
- **Epistemic Status & Identity Isolation**: To Kazuki and the narrative voice, he is known only as *"the silver-haired stranger"* or *"the traveler"* throughout Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 until the tea scene at the end of Ch 2, where Kazuki explicitly asks his identity and the Doctor formally introduces himself (*"I am the Doctor"*). To the former party, the authorities, and the public, his true nature and name remain unknown; he is perceived merely as an eccentric foreign scholar, witness, or hedge-doctor.

## 3. Takumi (巧) — *The Gilded Swordsman*

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Takumi of House Kaneshiro, *The Golden Flash*.
- **Archetype**: High-speed duelist and offensive swordsman; usurper party leader.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Second son of an impoverished lesser noble house in the capital. Grew up in bitter status anxiety, terrified of being seen as a provincial failure. Joined the party late, bringing fine steel and aristocratic connections, quickly claiming credit for the party's victories.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Gold-Rank prestige, royal patronage, and undisputed adulation.
  - *Internal Need*: To bury his gnawing terror of inadequacy and cowardice.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Arrogant narcissism. Truly believes that his flashy sword-kata are the sole reason the party succeeds, blind to the fact that without a vanguard absorbing enemy aggression, he is fragile pot glass.
- **The "Fun-to-Hate" Bastard Traits**: Flamboyant vanity, insufferable smugness, performative chivalry that masks sniveling cowardice the moment a beast actually breaks through his guard.
- **Epistemic Perception of Kazuki's Defense**: Possesses zero knowledge of meteor iron or Gallifreyan metallurgy. Views Kazuki's shield as a crude, uncrested, heavy black iron slab scavenged from a dead mercenary in the ditch. Dismisses Kazuki's healer as a charlatan back-alley hedge-doctor selling temporary dead-root pain-draughts that will shatter under siege quarrels.
- **Cathartic Downfall**: Caught red-handed by Chief Auditor Ren and the High Inquest; stripped of rank, his star-silver rapier shattered on the blacksmith's anvil, and expelled as a disincorporated vagrant into the debtor's ditch.

## 4. Saki (咲良) — *The Osanajimi / Divine Acolyte*

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Saki of Tanizaki, *The Maiden of the White Dawn*.
- **Archetype**: Divine recovery caster / healer.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Kazuki's childhood companion from the destitute mud hovels of Tanizaki. Grew up barefoot, chewing bitter roots during winter famines. Kazuki took beatings from landlords to steal bread for her, and bled on the frontline to buy her first white acolyte robes.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Highborn social standing, silk gowns, aristocratic marriage, and total erasure of her peasant origins.
  - *Internal Need*: Absolution from the suffocating guilt of unpayable debt.
- **Core Wound & Psychology of Betrayal**: Resentful usurpation. Every time she looked at Kazuki's broken knee, twisted ribs, and venom-blackened hands, she didn't feel gratitude—she felt humiliated. His suffering was an unbearable mirror reflecting the starving peasant girl she desperately wanted to forget. By throwing him into the ditch and bedding Takumi, she convinced herself she was cutting away dead weight and ascending to her rightful nobility.
- **Epistemic Perception & Superstitious Dread**: As a trained acolyte of the White Dawn, she knows that necrotic wyvern venom in the bone marrow requires a 5th-circle high cathedral arch-prelate miracle costing hundreds of crowns. Seeing Kazuki whole terrifies her with superstitious dread that an impossible divine power has intervened.
- **Cathartic Downfall**: Publicly defrocked by the Elder Deacon on the guildhall steps; her white robes stripped, temple ordination annulled, and cast into the freezing rain to shiver in the same mud ditch where she left Kazuki.

## 5. Reiko (麗子) — *The Highborn Pyromancer*

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Lady Reiko of the Northern Spire, *The Crimson Comet*.
- **Archetype**: Heavy offensive fire caster.
- **Core Vice & Personality**: Entitled aristocratic cruelty. Treats commoners like draft horses. Views Kazuki's sacrifices as nothing more than a mechanical requirement for her three-minute incantations. When his broken body caused a half-second delay in his block, she was the first to demand he be cut loose.
- **Downfall**: Her high-tier grimoire turns to blank parchment; her incantations fizzle into harmless tallow smoke. Forgotten by her noble house, she ends up shivering in the mud beside the commoners she despised.

---

## 6. Kaede (楓) — *The Shadow Scout*

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Kaede of the Red Willow, *The Silent Fang*.
- **Archetype**: Rogue, scout, and poison striker.
- **Core Vice & Personality**: Cold utilitarian sociopathy. Has no loyalty to anyone, measuring every human interaction purely in copper coins and survival odds. Betrayed Kazuki simply because she calculated that Takumi's political connections offered a 14% higher net profit margin.
- **Downfall**: Stripped of her guild bounties, contacts, and stealth arts. Discovered lying in the ditch, exposed to the cold light of day with nowhere left to hide.
