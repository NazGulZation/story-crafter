# Character Dossiers: The Lion's Debt

---

### Lord Tywin Lannister (Incarnated in the Mortal Vessel of Lucan Holt)
- **Identity & Epithet**: Former Lord Paramount of Casterly Rock, Shield of Lannisport, Warden of the West, and Hand of the King; now styling himself Lucan Holt, High Factor of *The Golden Lion Syndicate*.
- **Physical Form**: Nineteen years of age, lean and wiry from years of marching with campaign baggage. Clear, cold hazel-green eyes with flecks of gold. Broad-shouldered, though lacking the bloated muscle mass of front-line berserkers. Tywin carries himself with rigid, imperial posture, wearing severe black wool, high stiff leather collars, and clean linen cuffs.
- **Core Wound / Transmigration Trauma**: Slain upon a garderobe privy in the Tower of the Hand by his dwarf son Tyrion. The indignity of dying in latrine filth with loosened bowels burns in him like molten copper. He holds deep contempt for weakness, sentimental whining, and romantic indulgence, viewing the boy Lucan's unrequited longing for Rosamund as the same fatal softness that ruined his father, Tytos Lannister.
- **Concrete Desire**: To establish absolute financial, logistical, and political dominion over the Grand Duchy of Dun-Carrow; to crush the vainglorious upstarts who dared dismiss him; and to build a sovereign dynasty of unquestioned authority where debts are paid in full.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Imperious pride and unyielding intolerance for emotional variables. Tywin tends to view men and women strictly as ledger entries, assets, or livestock, occasionally underestimating the desperate, suicidal unpredictability of zealots who care nothing for self-preservation or gold.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Steeping his long fingers together at chest height; tracing the edge of a signet ring or ledger rule; holding absolute, stone-still silence until lesser men scramble to fill the void with frantic explanations.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Low, dry, deliberate baritone. Terse, measured sentences devoid of flowery adornment or exclamation. He never raises his voice; his quietest tone commands total silence.
- **Guiding Philosophy**: *"A lion does not concern himself with the opinion of sheep."* / *"Power resides where men believe it resides."* / *"A Lannister always pays his debts."*

---

### Lysander Morvan (The "Sun-Gilded Lance")
- **Identity & Epithet**: Party Leader of the *Dawn-Seekers*, blessed by the Goddess of the Dawn; third son of an impoverished marcher baron.
- **Physical Form**: Strikingly handsome, with shoulder-length spun-gold hair, porcelain skin, aquiline nose, and brilliant sky-blue eyes. Towering stature, clad in mirror-polished gilded plate armor adorned with stylized sunbursts.
- **Core Trait**: Narcissistic, vainglorious, and shallow. Granted immense divine swordsmanship and a blinding holy aura purely because the Goddess favored his face, Lysander believes the cosmos exists to worship him. He knows nothing of logistics, supply trains, food reserves, or debt.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Delusions of absolute divine invulnerability. He believes every battle can be resolved by glorious single combat and that money magically manifests whenever a hero demands wine or silk.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Booming, performative, laced with self-congratulatory piety and condescending laughter.

---

### Rosamund Cleary
- **Identity & Epithet**: Former village companion of Lucan Holt; healer and herbalist of the *Dawn-Seekers*.
- **Physical Form**: Petite, with honey-brown curls, wide hazel eyes, and a deceptively innocent smile. Now dresses above her station in silk gowns with low-cut bodices and velvet slippers.
- **Core Trait**: Venal opportunism masked by soft femininity. Having grown up in the same poor rural parish as Lucan, she accepted his quiet devotion and protection while using him as a pack-beast. The moment Lysander showed her favor, she threw herself into the hero's bedroll, reveling in the luxury, status, and carnal thrill while sneering at Lucan's diligence.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Arrogance rooted in temporary physical favor. She fails to recognize that to a narcissist like Lysander, a common-born healer is merely a replaceable diversion until a noble bride appears.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Sweet and soft-spoken in public; venomous, vulgar, and mocking toward those she deems beneath her.

---

### Lady Vivienne Montclair
- **Identity & Epithet**: High Sorceress of the *Dawn-Seekers*; estranged daughter of an eastern magistrate.
- **Physical Form**: Tall, statuesque, with raven hair pinned with silver needles, sharp aristocratic features, and cold grey eyes. Clad in indigo silk robes threaded with gold wire.
- **Core Trait**: Intellectual snobbery and private desperation. Vivienne looks down upon martial fighters as brute oxen, yet she hides a crippling dependency on refined mana-crystals to sustain her high-tier incantations—crystals she procures through illegal smuggling.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Arrogance regarding arcane supremacy. She assumes magic overcomes basic supply lines, forgetting that when mana-crystals are cut off, an archmage is merely a woman in a silk dress.

---

### Kallista Kroll
- **Identity & Epithet**: Vanguard scout and skirmisher; former mercenary cutthroat from the Iron Foothills.
- **Physical Form**: Muscular, weather-beaten, with short cropped auburn hair, a notched brow, and callused hands. Wears supple boiled leather reinforced with blackened chain links.
- **Core Trait**: Brutal pragmatism and martial bravado. She despises anyone who does not draw blood in battle, considering Lucan a useless burden who only dragged around parchment and cooking kettles.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Disdain for organizational logistics. She believes iron and grit win wars, blind to the fact that mercenary retainers desert when wages and dry grain fail to arrive.

---

### Archon Althaus of Dun-Carrow
- **Identity & Epithet**: Sovereign Ruler of the Grand Duchy of Dun-Carrow.
- **Physical Form**: Middle-aged, gout-ridden, soft-handed, wearing ermine-trimmed crimson velvet robes that strain against his belly.
- **Core Trait**: Politically weak, vain, and financially desperate. He spends fortunes on tournaments and monuments to project power while the Grand Duchy's treasury is hollowed out by debt to foreign merchant factors.
- **Role**: The sovereign debtor whom Tywin systematically corners and subordinates into a royal puppet.
