# Character Profile: Nozomi

![Nozomi](../assets/nozomi_portrait.jpg)

### Overview
- **Name**: Nozomi
- **Role**: Step-sister, unrepentant NEET, domestic squatter, eroge fanatic
- **Age**: 21 (just shy above twenties)
- **Physical Build**: Slender, lean frame with long pale legs and modest bust. Conventionally attractive facial features completely betrayed by disheveled dark-brown hair, heavy sleep deprivation, and slouching posture. Could easily pass for a print model if she brushed her hair, showered consistently, and stepped into natural sunlight.
- **Attire**: Oversized, faded dark graphic t-shirts that swallow her frame and drop off one collarbone, worn over minimal or absent smallclothes; pink noise-canceling gaming headset perpetually clamped around her neck or ears.

### Psychological Architecture

- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**:
  Brought into the protagonist's household during late middle school following a whirlwind remarriage between their parents. When the parental union quietly disintegrated years later and the parents drifted to separate prefectures, Nozomi was left adrift. Lacking social anchoring or academic discipline, she dropped out of vocational college and retreated entirely into the sanctuary of 2D visual novels, finding safety in branching dialogue trees where outcomes are deterministic and every mistake can be reloaded from a previous save.

- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Unrestricted access to high-speed internet, gaming hardware, instant tonkotsu ramen, and unlimited permission to squat in her step-brother's apartment without paying rent or doing chores.
  - *Internal Need*: Unconditional belonging and absolute terror of abandonment. Her aggressive reliance on her step-brother is not calculated parasitism, but an instinctive panic response: if she makes herself an inseparable fixture of his daily life, he cannot discard her like the rest of her broken family did.

- **Core Wound & Misbelief**:
  Believes she is fundamentally defective for the real world—unemployable, socially incompetent, and incapable of surviving without a caretaker. She compensates by treating her domestic reality as an eroge route, leaning shamelessly into the archetype of the coddled little sister because that role has clear, predictable rules.

- **Genuine Flaw & Blind Spot**:
  Total absence of interpersonal boundaries and severe lack of executive functioning. She cannot plan beyond the next meal or game chapter. When confronted with adult consequences, she instinctively regresses into weaponized cuteness or absurd melodramatic play-acting.

- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**:
  Genuine emotional confrontation without anime irony. When the teasing drops and real separation or rejection looms, her brazen demeanor instantly shatters into raw, trembling panic.

- **Tactile Tics & Mannerisms**:
  - Tugging the hem of her oversized t-shirt between two fingers whenever feeling cornered or anxious, inadvertently pulling the fabric taut across her hips and bare thighs.
  - Sprawling diagonally across whatever surface is closest—especially the protagonist's freshly made bed—tucking her icy bare feet under his calves or shins for body heat.
  - Tilting her chin up and widening her amber eyes into an exaggerated, trembling "puppy-eyes" grimace whenever assigned a chore.

- **Dialogue Cadence**:
  Speaks in rapid, casual bursts laced with unironic visual novel terminology (*flag triggered*, *bad end*, *route branch*, *affection points*). Slips effortlessly into an exaggerated, honeyed *onii-chan* whine whenever asking for food or evading accountability, punctuated by dry, deadpan commentary when her games hit dramatic climaxes.

- **Internal Contradiction**:
  She spends fourteen hours a day absorbing romance stories and explicit adult visual novels, yet possesses zero real-world romantic experience. Her boldness is an armor of theoretical knowledge: she can describe intricate sensual scenarios with complete technical fluency, yet flushes bright crimson down to her collarbones the moment genuine physical skin-to-skin intimacy takes hold.
