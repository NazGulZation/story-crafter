# The Harvest & Trustee Teamwork Seminar

A 5-chapter comedic gaming fiction featuring **Nice Nature (POV)**, **Special Week**, **Kitasan Black**, and **Satono Diamond** playing *Payday 2* in Nice Nature's dorm room across a rainy Saturday afternoon.

---

## Story Overview

- **Protagonist / POV**: Nice Nature (Third-Person Limited)
- **Setting**: Room 204 (Nice Nature's Dorm Room), Ritto Dormitory, Tracen Academy
- **Target Heist**: *Bank Heist: Cash* (Harvest & Trustee Branch, Washington D.C.)
- **Contractor**: Bain (Crime.net)
- **Difficulty**: Normal / Hard (Classic beginner gauntlet)
- **Core Tonal Arc**: Wholesome corporate team-building seminar $\to$ Meticulous stealth infiltration $\to$ Accidental frag grenade calamity $\to$ High-voltage police assault survival $\to$ Exhausted dorm room tea and PTSD.
