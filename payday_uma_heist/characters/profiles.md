# Character Profiles: The Harvest & Trustee Teamwork Seminar

---

### 1. Nice Nature (POV Anchor)
- **Full Name & Epithet**: Nice Nature ("The Self-Deprecating Voice of Reason")
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Constant bronze finishes and perpetual third-place podiums have given her an ingrained modesty and an acute radar for disaster. She expects things to go sideways, which makes her the only person in the room equipped to handle actual chaos when it arrives. Deeply affectionate toward her juniors and peers, even when she sighs like a middle-aged salaryman.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Finish this bizarre foreign video game without waking the dorm supervisor, breaking Dia's expensive laptops, or embarrassing herself in front of Special Week.
  - *Internal Need*: Validation as a capable, reliable elder peer who can protect and anchor those around her without fading into the background.
- **Core Wound / Misbelief**: Convinced she is inherently unsuited for the spotlight, doomed to be an unremarkable supporting character who cleans up messes for flashier talents.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Chronic hesitation and over-apologizing; she often delays decisive action because she assumes someone else surely knows better.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: High-frequency auditory chaos (three girls shrieking at once while game sirens scream) causes her to freeze for three seconds before snapping into stern elder-sister mode.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Massaging the bridge of her nose with thumb and forefinger while exhaling through puffed cheeks; tapping her mouse wheel rhythmically when anxious.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Informal Kansai-tinted, self-deprecating, dryly observant, frequently punctuated with exasperated sighs and protective warnings (*"Wait, wait, don't press that—"*, *"Why does this game hate us?"*).
- **Internal Contradiction**: Claims she wants a quiet, boring, low-stakes afternoon, yet throws herself directly into harm's way the moment her teammates' health bars flash red.
- **In-Game Payday 2 Persona**:
  - *Avatar*: Dallas (Default unpainted clown mask; refuses to waste money on cosmetics).
  - *Build*: Mastermind / Combat Medic with CAR-4 assault rifle and Chimano 88 sidearm.
  - *Deployable*: Doctor Bag (2 charges).
  - *Playstyle*: Methodical corner-peeking, checking sightlines, yelling at teammates to stop eating bullets, and constantly reminding everyone that health does not regenerate automatically.

### 2. Special Week ("Spe-chan")
- **Full Name & Epithet**: Special Week ("The Country-Born Engine of Sincerity")
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Raised in the rural expanses of Hokkaido by her two mothers, Spe possesses boundless physical grit, unmatched appetite, and an endearing, naive sincerity. She treats every commitment—whether a Grade 1 turf race or a tutorial prompt from an anonymous handler named Bain—with life-or-death solemnity.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Carry out Bain's mission faithfully, make her squad proud, and avoid letting anyone down.
  - *Internal Need*: The burning drive to prove her competence and show that her rural background doesn't make her clumsy or slow on the uptake.
- **Core Wound / Misbelief**: Deep anxiety that her natural clumsiness and lack of technical familiarity will ruin things for her friends.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Panic-induced tunnel vision. When startled, her fingers tense up completely, leading to catastrophic key-mashing.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: Being yelled at through a headset while sirens blare causes her to stammer in thick Hokkaido dialect and lose all spatial awareness.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Squeezing her knees together, leaning within six inches of the screen, and letting out small squeaks (*"Fueh?!"*) whenever an enemy appears.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Earnest, polite, rural-tinged keigo mixed with desperate cries for guidance (*"Nature-san, Bain-san is shouting at me again!"*, *"Where is the vault key?!"*).
- **In-Game Payday 2 Persona**:
  - *Avatar*: Houston (Mask: The Happy Clown, customized with bright pink rosy cheeks and yellow star stickers).
  - *Build*: Enforcer / Ammo Specialist with starter AMCAR rifle and Compact-5 submachine gun.
  - *Deployable*: Ammo Bag (2 charges).
  - *Playstyle*: Shoots in wild, uncontrolled bursts; constantly forgets that reloading drops her movement speed; possesses an uncontrollable urge to interact with every desk drawer. Fat-fingers the `G` key when startled.

### 3. Kitasan Black ("Kita-chan")
- **Full Name & Epithet**: Kitasan Black ("The Festival Vanguard")
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Raised in the boisterous, open-hearted atmosphere of traditional working-class festivals, Kitasan is built of boundless vitality, honesty, and communal spirit. She tackles obstacles by charging head-on with an infectious grin, viewing cooperative games as digital bonfires where everyone pulls together.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Smash through police blockades, carry the heaviest loot, and protect her best friend Dia from harm.
  - *Internal Need*: Proving that her straightforward, unrefined strength is just as valuable and sophisticated as Dia's elite strategic intellect.
- **Core Wound / Misbelief**: Believes complex problems can always be solved by applying more raw vigor, courage, and shouting.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Reckless bloodlust. She forgets objective markers the second an armored SWAT team enters her peripheral vision, chasing enemies across open street lanes.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: Being physically restrained or immobilized (e.g., getting electrocuted by a Taser or pinned by a Shield) infuriates her into violent keyboard rattling.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Bouncing her heels on the carpet, throwing her shoulders back, and yelling *"Wasshoi!"* in real life every time a shotgun shell ejects on screen.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Boisterous, cheerful, direct, full of athletic metaphors and festival shouts (*"Leave the front doors to me!"*, *"One more blast and they'll scatter like festival sparklers!"*).
- **In-Game Payday 2 Persona**:
  - *Avatar*: Chains (Mask: Flaming Red Oni Kabuto, adorned with brass horns).
  - *Build*: Shotgun Enforcer / Iron Man with Combined Tactical Vest, Reinfeld 880 pump shotgun, and Locomotive 12G secondary.
  - *Deployable*: Cable Ties and First Aid Kits.
  - *Playstyle*: Heavy tanking, sprinting directly at riot shields to jump-shot over their rims, meleeing SWAT units with brass knuckles, and treating heavy cash bags like featherweight training sacks.

### 4. Satono Diamond ("Dia-chan")
- **Full Name & Epithet**: Satono Diamond ("The Aristocratic Architect of Crime")
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Scion of the prestigious Satono conglomerate, Dia has been groomed with world-class education, etiquette, and analytical discipline. She approaches every hobby with thorough scholarly research and unhesitating financial commitment. She believes in bad omens and jinxes, using meticulous preparation to banish them.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Execute a textbook, zero-detection stealth operation that validates her extensive wiki research and pre-planning investments.
  - *Internal Need*: Breaking out of her sheltered, polite cage by experiencing raw, unscripted camaraderie with friends who treat her as an equal partner rather than an heiress.
- **Core Wound / Misbelief**: Subconsciously worries that without her family's wealth, resources, and pedigree, she has no genuine practical value in an ad-hoc crisis.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Rigid adherence to theoretical models. When reality diverges from her printed guides and spreadsheet notes, she experiences a five-second freeze while recalculating probabilities.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: Losing control of the stealth state; having her calculated master plan dissolved by an unforced error makes her eyes widen in polite, aristocratic shock.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Straightening her back to absolute vertical perfection, tucking a lock of green-ribboned hair behind her ear, and smoothing her skirt with both palms before taking a sip of black tea.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Impeccable high-society polite Japanese (ojou-sama register), delivered with serene academic detachment even while discussing high-explosive charges and off-shore money laundering (*"According to my cross-referenced tactical dossier..."*, *"A minor systemic variance, Nature-san. Nothing we cannot rectify."*).
- **In-Game Payday 2 Persona**:
  - *Avatar*: Hoxton (Mask: Venetian Gold-Filigree Masquerade with diamond dust accents).
  - *Build*: Ghost / Hacker with Two-Piece Suit, silenced Bernetti 9 pistol, and Gruber Kurz. Maxed dodge and concealment.
  - *Deployable*: ECM Jammers and Body Bag Case.
  - *Playstyle*: Memorized all keycard spawn coordinates; purchased every pre-planning asset (Expert Driver, Camera Feed, Body Bags, Insider Keycard) using millions in offshore funds she accumulated during offline practice runs; glides silently behind guards like a lethal shadow until Spe blows up the lobby.
