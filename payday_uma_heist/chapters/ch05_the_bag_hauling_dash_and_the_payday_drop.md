# Chapter 5: The Bag-Hauling Dash and the Payday Drop

Golden incandescent light spilled from the ceiling of the Harvest & Trustee vault, illuminating three industrial steel worktables piled high with banded bricks of crisp United States currency. 

Behind them, the charred, smoking ruins of the bank lobby lay littered with spent shotgun hulls, shattered bulletproof glass, and the motionless, hulking carcass of the Green Bulldozer. In Room 204, the only immediate sound was the soft, synchronous clicking of four mouse buttons as the girls gathered around the treasure.

"Look at all these green papers," Spe whispered in pure, country-girl awe. Her avatar, Houston, stood before the first steel table with her arms dangling at her sides, her giant smiling clown mask catching the warm vault glare. "Nature-san, are these all real American dollars? There are thousands of them! Millions!"

"Four million two hundred thousand dollars in uncirculated municipal cash," Dia stated with clinical exactitude, her Hoxton avatar kneeling beside the second table. She pressed and held the `F` key. With a crisp, rhythmic zip of heavy canvas, a thick blue duffel bag swelled with green currency bundles before strapping itself shut. "According to Bain’s contractual distribution matrix, twenty percent will enter our liquid spending balances, while eighty percent is routed directly into offshore trust accounts to evade federal financial scrutiny."

"Offshore accounts," Nature muttered, shaking her head as she bagged the second stack. "We haven't even finished high school, and Dia-chan has us setting up international tax shelters in the Caribbean. Hurry up and bag the last two tables. The assault break won't last forever."

"Allow me!" Kitasan shouted, scooping up the third and fourth bags with two lightning-fast taps of her interact key. 

Instantly, Kitasan’s screen lurched forward. 

Her avatar’s posture slumped under the colossal, simulated mass of eighty pounds of bundled paper currency. The agile, athletic festival runner was suddenly reduced to a sluggish, encumbered crawl. Her field of view narrowed, and a heavy, labored panting sound echoed through her headset.

"Wait! Why am I moving like a turtle in winter?!" Kitasan complained, rattling her `W` key with her thumb. "My legs feel like they're buried in wet rice-paddy mud! Nature-san, my person is walking at a snail's pace!"

"Weight physics, Kita-chan," Dia explained, hefting her own duffel and experiencing the exact same deceleration. "Paper currency possesses substantial physical density. An operative carrying a full extraction sack suffers a forty-five percent reduction in locomotive velocity. To maximize transit efficiency across exposed municipal terrain, we must execute the relay throw mechanic."

"The relay throw?" Spe asked, her ears perking up with innocent curiosity. "How do we throw the money?"

"You aim your reticle upward at approximately thirty-five degrees," Dia instructed, "and you depress the primary equipment release key. The `G` key."

At the mention of that specific letter, Special Week froze. 

Her hands flew off her mouse and keyboard as if the plastic had suddenly heated to boiling point. She shrank back against her square floor cushion, her purple eyes wide with sheer psychological trauma.

"No!" Spe gasped, shaking her head until her brown ears whipped across her cheeks. "Not the `G` key! Nature-san, Dia-chan, please! I will walk across the street on foot! I will carry the bag with my bare teeth! If I touch that letter again, another pineapple will bounce across the floor and blow up the entire city of Washington!"

"Spe-chan," Nature said gently, setting her own mouse down and turning her body toward her junior classmate. She reached over, placed a steady, calming hand over Spe’s trembling fingers, and guided her index finger back to the mechanical keyboard. "Look at your inventory HUD in the bottom right corner. Do you see the grenade icon?"

Spe sniffled slightly and peered at her screen. "It... it has a little grey zero next to it."

"Exactly," Nature smiled, her voice rich with elder-sister patience. "You threw your only grenade into the teller counter three minutes ago. You don't have any left. The `G` key only throws whatever is currently in your hands. Right now, that’s a bag full of hundred-dollar bills. Money doesn't explode when it hits the floor. Just tap it once. Don't hold it down. A light, polite tap."

Spe swallowed hard. Her throat gave a distinct gulp. She stared at the letter `G` on the black keycap, took a deep breath of the warm, macaron-scented air in the room, and tapped the key with the tip of her fingernail.

*Thump.*

On screen, Houston cleanly launched the heavy blue duffel through the air in a high, tumbling arc. The bag cleared the vault threshold, soared over the floor grating, and landed with a soft, satisfying canvas slap in the middle of the hallway.

Spe’s face instantly lit up with radiant, incandescent triumph. 

"I threw the money!" Spe squealed, clapping her hands against her knees. "Look, Nature-san! The money flew through the air and nobody got hurt! It didn't explode at all!"

"Told you," Nature said, tossing her own bag right beside Spe’s. "Now pick it up and throw it again. We have to move all four bags through the lobby to the parking lot."

Bain’s voice cut through the comms with urgent, crackling clarity:

*"Crew! Twitch is pulling the van into the street parking lot right now! He can't sit there forever, the cops are closing off the perimeter! Get those bags to the van and get the hell out of there!"*

Outside the bank, through the shattered front windows, the faint wail of distant police sirens began rising once again into a frantic chorus. The grey afternoon sky flared with the reflected red and blue strobe lights of dozens of incoming tactical cruisers.

"The secondary assault wave is assembling," Dia warned, scooping up a bag and hurling it through the copy room door. "We possess an extraction window of approximately ninety seconds before municipal reinforcements establish a complete perimeter blockade."

"Bucket brigade formation!" Kitasan shouted, her festival instincts immediately taking command. 

Thanks to her Enforcer tier-two skills, Kitasan’s avatar possessed the *Transporter* ability, allowing her to heave heavy bags twice as far as an ordinary operative. She planted her boots on the linoleum, picked up a duffel, and threw it like a champion launching an iron kettle. The bag sailed thirty feet across the lobby, crashing down right beside the shattered glass front doors.

"Wasshoi relay!" Kitasan roared, picking up the next bag and hurling it in a matching arc. "Keep the rhythm going! One, two! One, two!"

Spe scrambled behind her, giggling with newfound confidence as she tapped her `G` key, launching bags through the corridor like a cheerful postal clerk sorting parcels. Nature covered the rear, keeping Dallas’s rifle trained on the back alley in case flanking units appeared.

Within forty-five seconds, all four bags were piled on the sidewalk just outside the front entrance.

Across the rain-soaked four-lane avenue sat an enclosed asphalt parking lot, surrounded by a chain-link fence. Idling beside a rusted red sedan was a boxy white Econoline van with tinted rear windows. Its rear double doors were flung wide open, revealing a dark, empty cargo bay with a pulsing green extraction zone glowing on the metal floor.

"There's the carriage!" Spe shouted, pointing Dallas's camera toward the vehicle. "Twitch-san is waiting for us!"

"Hold your advance!" Dia commanded sharply, her voice cutting through Spe’s excitement like a razor. "Observe the roofline of the commercial automotive dealership across the street. Twelve o'clock high."

Nature zoomed her CAR-4’s optic toward the flat roof of the dealership.

Through the falling drizzle, two crimson laser beams sliced down from the rooftop parapet, cutting bright, needle-thin lines through the mist. The beams swept back and forth across the asphalt crosswalk, coming to rest directly on the pile of money bags at their feet.

"Snipers," Nature grunted, her stomach dropping. "Two of them. High-caliber sniper rifles. If you step into that laser beam, one shot will strip your armor, and the second will put you on the pavement."

"And the crosswalk is sixty meters of completely flat, exposed pavement," Dia added, her brow furrowing in severe tactical calculation. "With our movement penalized by the duffel bags, a linear transit across the street guarantees an eighty percent casualty rate."

"Then we don't walk in a straight line," Nature declared, stepping Dallas to the front of the group. "Kita-chan. How much armor do you have left?"

Kitasan checked her HUD. Her Combined Tactical Vest had regenerated to full capacity, displaying a thick, reassuring white arc around her health bar. 

"Maximum defense, Nature-san! My armor is as solid as an iron festival bell!"

"Good," Nature said, her eyes narrowing with sharp, competitive resolve. "You take the first bag. You run diagonally toward the concrete planter box on the left, draw their fire, and heave the bag over the fence. Dia-chan and I will counter-snipe the roof. Spe-chan, you grab the second bag and trail three paces behind Kita-chan. When she draws the shot, you sprint for the van. Understood?"

"Understood!" Kitasan and Spe answered in chorus.

"Executing in three... two... one... GO!"

Kitasan lunged out from the bank vestibule, a heavy bag tucked under Chains’s arm. Her lacquered red oni kabuto gleamed beneath the streetlights as she charged across the wet asphalt.

*CRACK!*

A high-caliber sniper round tore from the dealership roof, shattering the brick pillar behind Kitasan’s head with a sound like a thunderclap. 

"Target spotted!" Nature yelled, snapping her crosshairs onto the silhouette on the rooftop. 

She squeezed her mouse button twice. *Crack-crack!* Two 5.56mm rounds caught the first sniper in the chest. The laser beam flickered and died as the rifle tumbled off the roof edge.

"One down!" Nature shouted. "Move, Spe-chan!"

Spe bolted from the bank doors, hauling her duffel across the asphalt in a desperate, wide-eyed sprint. Houston’s smiling clown mask bobbed frantically in the rain.

*CRACK!*

The second sniper fired. 

The heavy bullet missed Kitasan entirely, tracking the brighter, larger target: Special Week’s neon-pink-cheeked clown face. The round caught Houston square in the right shoulder. 

Spe’s armor bar shattered into glowing blue fragments with a sickening electronic crunch. Her health bar instantly dropped into the deep crimson danger zone, flashing an ominous *LOW HEALTH* warning. Houston stumbled, dropping the bag in the center of the crosswalk.

"I AM STRUCK!" Spe wailed through her headset, tears of panic brimming in her eyes. "MY CLOWN IS BLEEDING! THE RED BEAM IS CENTERING ON MY CHEST AGAIN!"

On the dealership roof, the second sniper racked his bolt. The red laser beam pinned itself directly between Houston’s virtual shoulder blades.

"SPE-CHAN, DUCK!" Nature screamed.

A massive, armored shadow blurred across the screen.

Kitasan Black did not duck. She did not take cover behind the concrete planter. 

She turned on her heel, leaped three yards across the slick asphalt, and planted Chains’s colossal, armor-plated bulk directly between the red laser beam and Special Week’s trembling avatar.

"FESTIVAL SHIELD!" Kitasan roared at the top of her lungs, throwing her chest out toward the rooftop. "AIM AT THE FIRE, YOU COWARDLY ROOFTOP PEST!"

*CRACK!*

The sniper’s second bullet struck Kitasan dead center in the breastplate. 

Sparks flew. The impact rocked Kitasan’s avatar back on her heels, stripping her armor down to five percent, but she held her ground like a granite seawall against a typhoon surge. 

"NOW, DIA-CHAN!" Kitasan yelled.

From the sidewalk, Satono Diamond knelt in the gutter, brought her silenced Bernetti 9 up to eye level, and fired a single, exquisite, long-range precision shot.

*Thwip.*

Across the street, eighty meters away, the second sniper threw his hands up and slumped backward across the air-conditioning unit on the roof. The second red laser beam vanished into the rain.

"Rooftop neutralized," Dia announced with icy, aristocratic satisfaction. "Street corridor clear!"

"GRAB THE BAGS AND MOVE!" Nature roared, throwing Dallas forward.

The final thirty seconds dissolved into an ecstatic, breathless blur. 

Kitasan scooped up the dropped bag, hurled it over the chain-link fence straight through the van's open doors, and scrambled over the gate. Spe tossed her third bag into the cargo bay with a joyful shriek. Nature hauled the final duffel, sliding across the wet pavement and pitching it over the bumper just as Twitch stomped the engine into a deafening, revving roar.

"All bags loaded!" Bain yelled through their headsets, his voice completely ecstatic. *"Get in! Get the hell in, crew! We're rolling!"*

One by one, the four avatars leaped into the back of the white van. 

Dallas dove in last, hitting the metal floor beside Hoxton. Twitch leaned out the driver's window, slammed the heavy twin rear doors shut with a booming, hollow *CLANG*, and floored the accelerator.

Tires screamed against the wet asphalt. The white van fishtailed around an incoming police roadblock, smashed through a wooden construction barrier, and shot down the highway into the grey horizon as six pursuit cruisers chased hopelessly in its wake.

The screen went black.

A triumphant, brass-heavy victory fanfare blared through the laptop speakers. In the center of the dark screen, golden text materialized in towering, gilded block letters:

**PAYDAY: MISSION ACCOMPLISHED**

- *Total Cash Secured: \$4,200,000*
- *Offshore Account Allocation: \$3,360,000*
- *Spending Cash Earned: \$840,000*
- *Civilians Harmed: 0 (Directly)*
- *Law Enforcement Neutralized: 47*

In Room 204, a collective, earth-shaking collapse occurred.

Special Week let out a long, shuddering squeak and collapsed flat onto her back against the tatami mats, staring at the white ceiling plaster with her arms splayed out like a starfish. Her chest heaved beneath her track jacket, and her brown horse ears twitched weakly against the floorboards.

"We... we survived," Spe whispered, her voice faint and raspy. "Nature-san... we stole four million dollars from America... and we didn't go to prison..."

"It was an extraction of liquid municipal reserves," Dia corrected gently, though she too was slumped back against her bolster, her shoulders dropped, a faint flush of exertion dusting her pale cheeks. She slowly raised her hands to inspect her fingertips, which were trembling faintly from twenty minutes of nonstop ninety-mile-per-hour mechanical mouse movements. "A... highly educational seminar. Though the municipal law enforcement displayed an unexpectedly aggressive response to our procedural presence."

"Unexpectedly aggressive?!" Nature groaned, burying her face in her hands and pressing the heels of her palms against her aching eyes. "They brought a bomb squad juggernaut with an automatic shotgun through the front door, Dia-chan! A ninja dropkicked Kita-chan in the face from under a desk! I have never had so many people try to murder me on a Saturday afternoon in my entire life!"

"THAT WAS THE GREATEST THING I HAVE EVER SEEN!" Kitasan shouted, springing up onto her knees with her fists clenched in the air, her face glowing with pure, unadulterated euphoria. "Did you see that shot, Dia-chan?! The Dozer's faceplate went *ping* and then *BOOM*! And then we threw the money bags like festival turnips! When do we rob the next one?! Can we rob a bigger bank tomorrow?! Bain-san has a contract for an art museum!"

"No," Nature said firmly, looking up through her fingers with a weary, affectionate scowl. "No art museums. No diamond vaults. Tomorrow, you two are doing forty laps around the turf track to burn off all this unhinged adrenaline."

*Chime.*

A soft chime from the laptops drew their attention back to the screens.

The post-mission loot screen had appeared: three face-down cards floating against a dark velvet background, labeled *PAYDAY LOOT DROP*.

"Pick a card, everyone," Dia smiled, regaining her aristocratic composure as she tapped her touchpad. "The algorithmic reward protocol."

Four mice clicked in unison.

Dia picked the center card. The card flipped over with a brilliant, blinding golden flash, accompanied by a heavenly chorus of angelic synthesized trumpets. 

**ITEM UNLOCKED: [INFAMOUS] MATERIAL: SOLID 24K GOLD**  
**ITEM UNLOCKED: [INFAMOUS] MASK: THE ANCIENT SKULL OF CTHULHU**

"Oh my," Dia murmured, blinking in pleasant surprise. "A zero-point-seven percent probability drop on our inaugural extraction. How remarkably auspicious."

"Yahoo! My turn!" Kitasan cheered, flipping the right card.

**ITEM UNLOCKED: 12G FLECHETTE SHOTGUN AMMUNITION**  
**ITEM UNLOCKED: COLOR: BLOOD SCARLET / OBSIDIAN BLACK**

"Armor-piercing darts for my festival boom-stick!" Kitasan pumped her fist. "Next time, the riot shield guys won't know what hit them!"

"Um... what did I get?" Spe asked, sitting up and peering timidly at her left card.

**ITEM UNLOCKED: GADGET: COMPACT NEON LIME-GREEN LASER SIGHT**  
*(Compatible with: Bernetti 9, Chimano 88)*

Spe tilted her head, her purple eyes examining the tiny, glowing green cylinder on her screen. A slow, radiant smile spread across her face. 

"Look, Nature-san! It’s neon green! It looks like a little firefly from the farm! It doesn't explode at all! I can put it on my polite pistol!"

Nature looked at her own screen. She had picked the final card.

The card flipped over with a dull, unenthusiastic *thwack*.

**ITEM UNLOCKED: WEAPON MOD: STANDARD CYLINDRICAL SUPPRESSOR (GREY)**  
*(Effect: Damage -4, Concealment +1)*

Nature stared at the drab, unpainted metal tube on her monitor. She looked at Dia’s gleaming solid gold skull, Kitasan’s incendiary shotgun darts, and Spe’s cheerful neon-green firefly laser.

A quiet, familiar chuckle bubbled up from Nature’s chest. 

"Of course," Nature said softly to herself, smiling with complete, peaceful resignation. "A sensible grey tube for third place. Perfectly on brand."

The rain outside the window had slowed to a gentle, quiet mist, whispering against the darkening glass. Outside in the hallway, the distant chime of the six o'clock dormitory dinner bell echoed down the corridor, signaling the start of the evening meal in the central dining hall.

Special Week’s stomach immediately let out an enormous, reverberating growl that rattled the empty tea mugs.

"Ah," Spe blushed, pressing both hands over her midriff. "All that bank running made my engine hungry..."

"Dinner is served in ten minutes," Nature said, reaching over to close her laptop lid. The cyan LEDs flickered out with a soft, satisfying click. "Kita-chan, help Spe-chan put her shoes back on. Dia-chan, leave the laptops on the table; we'll pack them after we eat."

"Understood, Nature-san," Dia said, gracefully rising from her cushion and smoothing her skirt. "Thank you for hosting our strategic leadership seminar. I believe our interpersonal trust index has increased by at least twelve percent."

"Just don't mention any of this to the dorm supervisor," Nature said dryly, gathering the empty macaron wrappers and the used tea cups onto a lacquer tray. 

She walked over to the small kitchenette sink, turned on the tap, and let the warm water rinse the roasted tea leaves down the drain. From the living room behind her came the cheerful, bickering laughter of three horse girls putting on their jackets, arguing about who was going to carry the cafeteria tray first.

Nice Nature wiped the porcelain cups with a dry cloth, set them neatly in the drying rack, and turned off the small desk lamp, letting the quiet twilight settle over Room 204.
