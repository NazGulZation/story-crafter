# Chapter 2: The Art of the Quiet Professional

Grey morning light washed over the cracked asphalt of a rain-slicked Washington street. On four synchronized displays, four figures clad in tailored dark business suits materialized on the sidewalk across from a brick-and-limestone municipal bank.

In Room 204, the only sounds were the quiet hum of cooling fans, the distant patter of rain against the window, and the low, tense rhythm of an electronic upright bass pulsing through their headsets. A small text prompt hovered in the upper corner of Nature’s screen: *Casing Mode. Do not draw weapons.*

"Everyone remain in your starting positions," Dia instructed through her headset microphone. Her tone was measured and cool, the cadence of an officer observing a chess board. She lifted her mouse slightly to reset its position on her black velvet pad. "Observe the interface. As long as our masks remain in our coat pockets, the municipal inhabitants perceive us as ordinary commercial patrons. Our detection meters are currently sitting at an optimal rating of three."

"I am walking!" Spe whispered excitedly. Her avatar took three jerky, stuttering steps forward, pivoting twenty degrees to the left with every twitch of her mouse hand. "Look, Nature-san! My person has very nice leather dress shoes! They make a little clicking sound on the pavement!"

"Yes, Spe-chan, very stylish," Nature murmured, using her own mouse to pan her camera left and right. "Now please stop spinning in circles in front of that mailbox. People are going to think you've lost your train pass."

"Nature-san," Kitasan said, her knee bouncing under the table. Her avatar—a mountain of muscle in a charcoal jacket—was practically vibrating on the curb. "Can I run across the street? The crosswalk light is red, but there are no cars coming. If I sprint, I can reach the front doors in two seconds!"

"Negative, Kita-chan," Dia interjected smoothly. "Running in the presence of municipal civilians increases our visual profile. If you depress the sprint key, the circular detection meter will accumulate suspicion. Please proceed at a dignified stroll. Follow my vector toward the rear service corridor."

Dia’s avatar, sleek and unencumbered, glided across the crosswalk with fluid grace. 

Nature guided Dallas behind her, keeping her eyes glued to the upper floors of the bank. The building was a classic Harvest & Trustee branch: two stories of soot-stained brick, large floor-to-ceiling plate glass windows in the front lobby, a fenced parking lot on the right flank, and an enclosed service alley tucked behind the rear wall.

"Alright," Nature muttered, checking the key binds she had scribbled onto a sticky note beside her trackpad. "`F` to shout and interact. `Space` to jump. `Left Shift` to run. `Left Control` to crouch. And whatever we do, nobody touch the number keys unless we're actually in a gunfight."

"Nature-san! Look!" Spe gasped, her avatar halting abruptly beside a concrete planter box. "There is a gentleman in a light grey suit pacing outside the glass doors! He has a yellow lanyard around his neck! Is he the principal of the bank?"

"That is the branch manager," Dia confirmed, a spark of satisfaction warming her voice. "According to the procedural algorithms, he carries the magnetic keycard granting administrative override to the high-security surveillance office. He is currently on a telephone call. Kita-chan, please pivot your camera toward the alley fence. Do you perceive the surveillance device above the delivery bay?"

"Found it!" Kitasan called out. "A black dome camera! It’s swiveling left and right like a mechanical owl!"

"The cone of detection sweeps across the asphalt every four seconds," Dia noted, clicking her tongue softly as she tapped her keyboard. "We shall bypass its blind spot. Gather behind the green sanitation container."

The four avatars filed into the narrow alleyway behind the bank. The brick walls on either side trapped the shadow, cutting off the ambient traffic rumble from the main avenue. Beside a rusted green dumpster lay a blue nylon duffel bag marked with a discreet white cross—the dead drop body bag case Dia had purchased during pre-planning.

"Asset located," Dia whispered. "Now, observe the protocol. We must don our disguises to manipulate the lock on the service entrance. Remember, once disguised, any civilian or guard who establishes line of sight will initiate an immediate alarm cycle. Prepare to execute."

"Putting on the mask," Nature said.

She pressed and held the `G` key. 

On screen, two gloved hands rose from the bottom of the display, pulling down Dallas’s cracked porcelain clown mask with a heavy, rubbery snap. Simultaneously, Kitasan masked up, her lacquered red oni kabuto gleaming with digital sheen. Spe donned her smiling clown visage with its enormous painted rosy cheeks, and Dia settled into her golden Venetian masquerade.

The game audio shifted instantly. The ambient street sounds muted, replaced by a tight, muffled filter, accompanied by the cold, metallic slide of a round chambering into an assault rifle.

"Masks on," Bain’s voice crackled crisply into their headsets, sounding sharp and professional. *"Alright crew, keep it clean. Don't let the cameras catch you, and keep the civilians quiet."*

"He spoke!" Spe squeaked, pressing her hands against her cheeks. "Bain-san said our masks look clean!"

"He meant the operation, Spe-chan," Nature sighed. "Keep your voice down."

At that exact second, the heavy steel door at the end of the alley clicked. A private security guard clad in a white shirt and black necktie stepped out into the drizzle, holding a plastic clipboard. He took two steps onto the pavement, paused, and looked directly toward the dumpster.

A bright blue exclamation mark flashed above the guard's head with a sharp, high-pitched *ping!*

"Hostile visual contact!" Kitasan shouted, slamming her hand down onto the mouse. "Wasshoi breach!"

"Kita-chan, do not fire!" Dia commanded sharply.

Kitasan’s heavy avatar lunged forward, closing the distance in three massive strides. Instead of firing her twelve-gauge shotgun, she hammered the melee key. Chains’s gloved fist drove straight into the guard's jaw with a sickening, concussive crack. 

The security guard crumpled backward onto the wet asphalt like a sack of damp grain, completely immobilized.

"Target pacified!" Kitasan reported proudly, her chest heaving as she grinned across the table. "One clean strike! Just like tamping the festival mochi!"

"Good heavens," Nature muttered, her heart thumping against her ribs as she stared at the virtual body. "Is he...?"

"He is merely concussed, Nature-san," Dia said calmly, stepping forward with her silenced Bernetti 9 drawn. "However, observe his belt. His handheld radio is flashing yellow. That is the municipal dispatch pager. If it is not acknowledged within ten seconds, the police monitoring station will treat the silence as a catastrophic breach."

"The radio is buzzing!" Spe cried, pointing frantically at her monitor. "It’s making a little bee sound! *Bzzz! Bzzz!*"

"I shall handle the communication," Dia said. Her avatar knelt beside the fallen officer. Dia held the interact key. 

Over the radio, Hoxton’s sarcastic British drawl floated across the channel: *"Sorry mate, dropped my radio in my tea. Everything's lovely down here. Pip pip."*

A small green checkmark appeared over the guard's radio. The dispatch operator bought the excuse.

"Communication verified," Dia sighed with aristocratic poise. "Kita-chan, please apply plastic zip-ties to secure his limbs. Nature-san, retrieve a blue body bag from the pre-planning container and conceal his person behind the refuse bin."

"Right, right, sanitation duty," Nature grumbled, clicking her interact key on the dumpster. Her avatar scooped up a heavy blue vinyl bag, walked over to the tied guard, and packed him away with a clean zip sound. She dragged the bundle into the dark recess behind the dumpster. "Done. He's tucked away next to the cardboard boxes. Nobody can see him from the alley entrance."

"Flawless execution," Dia praised, turning toward the reinforced service door. She retrieved the magnetic keycard from her inventory. "Now, we infiltrate the surveillance hub. According to the blueprint, the administrative security office is located directly through this vestibule on the left. Once I neutralize the camera operator, every surveillance node in the facility will deactivate, granting us unrestricted tactical dominance."

Dia swiped the keycard. The security lock gave a soft, pneumatic *beep*, and the heavy steel door swung inward.

The interior of the bank smelled of dry office toner, lemon wax, and cold conditioned air. Fluorescent tubes hummed softly along the ceiling panels. Through the frosted glass partition of the adjacent hallway, Nature could see the broad expanse of the main teller lobby: plush red carpet, four tellers standing behind a waist-high wooden counter with brass grilles, and two elderly civilians sitting on a leather sofa reading magazines.

Dia crept along the carpet in a low crouch, her silenced pistol raised. She reached the camera room door, picked the latch in four seconds with her Ghost skills, and slipped inside.

*Thwip.*

A single suppressed shot echoed through the office. On all four HUDs, a notification popped up: *Security Cameras Deactivated.*

"The surveillance network is offline," Dia announced, stepping out of the small room with a serene, triumphant smile. She reached for her teacup and took a delicate sip of roasted barley tea. "The external and internal monitors are dark. The rear corridor is secure. We have achieved ninety percent of our tactical prerequisites without alerting a single municipal authority."

"We did it!" Spe whispered, her purple eyes shining with admiration. "Dia-chan, you're amazing! You're like a phantom master thief from the storybooks!"

"It is merely a matter of methodical calculation," Dia replied graciously, inclining her head. "Now, the final phase. We must enter the central lobby simultaneously. Nature-san and Kita-chan will intimidate the two civilians on the sofa using the vocal compliance command—the `F` key. Spe-chan, you will approach the teller counter and direct the two tellers to lie upon the carpet before they reach the panic button under the wooden desk. Once they are subdued, we shall tie their wrists and deploy the thermal drill in complete tranquility."

"Vocal compliance," Spe repeated, nodding her head vigorously. Her hands trembled against her mouse. "Hold the `F` key. Shout politely. Make sure they lay down so nobody gets hurt."

"Correct," Nature said, stepping up beside Spe’s avatar in the doorway. "Just take it slow, Spe-chan. Walk up to the wooden partition, look at the teller, and tap `F`. Don't look at anything else. Just `F`."

"Yes! Understood, Nature-san! Class representative of bank robbery, Special Week, moving out!"

"Don't call yourself that—"

The four avatars stepped out of the office corridor and onto the plush burgundy carpet of the main bank lobby.

The lighting was bright and peaceful. Gentle acoustic elevator jazz drifted from ceiling speakers. One of the elderly civilians looked up from his newspaper, his eyes widening as he noticed four figures in two-piece suits wearing an unpainted clown, a horned oni, a golden Venetian mask, and a giant smiling clown with bright pink cheeks.

A question mark appeared over the civilian's head.

"Down! Everyone on the floor!" Nature shouted, hammering her `F` key. Dallas's gruff American voice barked through the speakers: *"Get down on the ground! Do it now!"*

The two civilians immediately threw their hands over their heads and dropped flat onto the carpet. Kitasan dashed over, zipping their wrists with rapid plastic clicks.

"Tellers next!" Dia called out, gliding toward the manager's office. "Spe-chan, secure the counter!"

"Right now!" Spe cried out in real life. 

Her avatar broke into a panicked, frantic sprint toward the teller counter. A young female teller in a blue blazer gasped, her hand darting toward the red emergency button mounted beneath the counter ledge.

"Please don't push the button!" Spe shrieked through her microphone, her voice climbing an entire octave into pure rural panic. "Lie down! Please lie down, miss teller! It’s dangerous! Aaaaah, which key was it?!"

In the cramped quarters of Room 204, Spe’s index finger spasmed over the keyboard. 

Her finger slipped off the `F` key. Her knuckles tensed. In a blind surge of adrenaline, her fingertip slammed down with crushing force onto the key directly adjacent to it.

The `G` key.

*Ping.*

A sharp, metallic clatter cut through the lobby jazz.

Nature’s screen froze. She slowly turned her camera toward Spe’s avatar.

Houston was standing three feet from the teller’s bulletproof glass window. His arm was cocked back in a clean, textbook baseball pitcher's follow-through. Flying through the air in a graceful, tumbling arc was a heavy, ribbed, olive-drab steel canister.

The safety spoon fluttered off the fuse with a dry, mechanical *clack*.

"Spe-chan," Nature whispered, her voice dropping into a flat, hollow void. "What did you just throw?"

"Fueh?" Spe blinked, looking down at her empty virtual hands. "I... I pressed the polite shouting button..."

The fragmentation grenade hit the polished linoleum floor behind the teller counter.

*Clink.*

It bounced once off the mahogany wood.

*Clank.*

It rolled directly under the teller’s swivel chair and came to a gentle, rocking halt against the base of the computer tower.

*Sssssssss.*

"That's a grenade," Kitasan said, her voice filled with childlike wonder. "Spe-chan threw a pineapple."

"GRENADE!" Nature screamed, throwing herself backward against her floor cushion. "TAKE COVER!"

*BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!*

A blinding orange fireball erupted through the teller counter. The explosion blew the heavy wooden partition into a thousand splintered toothpicks. The reinforced bulletproof glass shattered with a deafening, ear-splitting crash that rattled the laptop speakers. Plaster dust, shreds of deposit slips, and glowing embers rained down across the lobby carpet.

The teller alarm shrieked like a severed steam valve.

A shrill, pulsing siren began howling from the ceiling, echoing down the street in a frantic, rising wail: *WURRR-WURRR-WURRR-WURRR!*

Bain’s voice exploded into their headsets, completely devoid of his previous professional calm, howling with sheer, unadulterated disbelief: 

*"WHAT IN GOD'S NAME WAS THAT?! THE WHOLE DAMN BLOCK HEARD THAT! THE ALARM'S TRIPPED! 911 CALLS ARE FLOODING IN! GET READY, THEY'RE COMING IN HARD!"*

On all four monitors, the quiet casing interface shattered. A massive, pulsing banner of bold crimson text stamped itself across the top of their screens in letters five inches high:

**POLICE ASSAULT IN PROGRESS /// ESCAPE UNAVAILABLE**

In Room 204, complete silence descended for two entire seconds, broken only by the whistling kettle on the stove and the wailing game sirens.

Special Week slowly removed her hands from the keyboard, her face turning the exact shade of her avatar's painted pink clown cheeks. Her purple eyes filled with horrified tears as her ears flattened entirely against her skull.

"I... I blew up the bank," Spe whimpered, her voice trembling like a reed in a storm. "Nature-san... I blew up the polite lady's desk..."

Dia sat frozen, her teacup hovering two inches from her lips. Her emerald eyes stared at the smoking crater in the teller booth, her aristocratic composure completely dissolved into sheer, uncomprehending shock.

"The... the structural integrity of the lobby," Dia whispered, blinking rapidly. "The stealth parameters... have experienced a ninety-nine percent variance."

Beside them, Kitasan’s eyes widened to the size of saucers. A massive, incandescent grin spread across her face, showing every single tooth.

"WASSHOI!" Kitasan roared, slamming both heels into the carpet and cocking her shotgun with a deafening pump. "IT’S LOUD TIME, BABY!"

Nice Nature closed her eyes. She inhaled the scent of burning toner and hot laptop plastic, slowly reached up, and massaged the bridge of her nose until it hurt.

"May God forgive us," Nature said quietly, opening her eyes and racking the bolt on her CAR-4 assault rifle. "Spe-chan. Pick up your gun."
