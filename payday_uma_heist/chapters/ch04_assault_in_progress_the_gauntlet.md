# Chapter 4: Assault in Progress: The Gauntlet

The timer above the smoking thermal drill ticked down with agonizing, glacial defiance: *0:28... 0:27... 0:26...*

Inside Room 204, the air had grown thick, warm, and heavy with the dry stench of four overtaxed laptop fans blowing heat across the tea table. Spe was breathing through her teeth in rapid, shallow pants, her nose barely five inches from the screen. Kitasan’s knees were drumming a continuous, frantic tattoo against the floorboards, while Dia sat with her spine rigid, her emerald eyes tracking the minimap with mathematical ferocity.

Nice Nature gripped her wireless mouse until the plastic seams creaked under her palm. 

"Twenty-five seconds," Nature announced, her voice strained and hoarse from shouting over the music. "Do not get greedy. Do not leave the vault corridor. The drill is almost done. Just hold the doorways!"

"I need ammunition!" Spe cried out in sheer, unfiltered panic. She clicked her left mouse button, producing only the dull, hollow *click-click* of an empty firing pin. "My polite carbine has zero bullets left! Zero! The number is red!"

"There's an ammo box on the floor by the customer water cooler!" Kitasan shouted, leaning out from behind her concrete pillar to blast a gas-masked SWAT officer who was trying to vault the counter. "I saw two blue guys drop little grey packages when I introduced them to the festival spirit!"

"I will fetch the sustenance!" Spe yelled.

"Spe-chan, wait! Do not break the defensive perimeter—" Dia’s warning came two seconds too late.

Houston broke from the safety of the corridor, sprinting into the devastated lobby. Her avatar’s smiling clown face, complete with its bright painted rosy cheeks, bobbed frantically as she dashed toward the overturned water cooler.

From behind the velvet executive couch, a figure in a heavy yellow-striped tactical suit stepped into the open. The operative carried a bulky, modified bullpup carbine outfitted with thick copper coils and pulsing blue capacitors.

A sharp, high-voltage crackle split the air: *BZZZZZZZZT!*

Twin barbed electrical darts slammed into Houston’s chestplate. Fifty thousand volts of pulsing blue current surged across Spe’s screen. 

"FUEEEEEEEH?!" Spe shrieked at the top of her lungs, throwing her hands straight into the air in the real world.

The game mechanics forcibly seized control of Spe’s avatar. Houston’s arms jerked violently upward. Her AMCAR carbine—having automatically chambered a single scavenged bullet—began firing in an uncontrollable, full-auto frenzy straight into the ceiling acoustic tiles. 

*BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG!*

Plaster dust, fiberglass insulation, and fluorescent light shards rained down in a blinding white cloud as Houston danced a grotesque, jittering jig of pure electrical agony.

"I AM BEING ELECTROCUTED!" Spe wailed, her ears flattening back so hard they practically vanished into her hair. "MY FINGERS ARE NUMB! THE CONTROLLER IS VIBRATING! NATURE-SAN, I CANNOT STOP SHOOTING THE CEILING!"

"LIGHTNING OPERATIVE LOCATED!" Kitasan roared, her eyes blazing with furious festival vengeance. "UNHAND THE SINCERE COUNTRY GIRL!"

Kitasan abandoned her defensive barricade. Her heavy-armored Enforcer lunged over the shattered teller counter, her Reinfeld 880 pump shotgun racked and ready to blow the yellow-suited Taser into the next prefecture.

She never reached him.

From underneath the bank manager’s solid mahogany desk, where the darkness gathered behind an overturned swivel chair, two emerald-green circles of light ignited with a demonic, phosphor glare.

Then came the sound.

It was a high-frequency, electronic banshee shriek that rose from a low whine to a deafening, piercing mechanical crescendo:

*WULULULULU-WULULULULU-WULULULULU!*

"WHAT IS THAT SOUND?!" Kitasan yelled, her avatar halting mid-stride.

A shadow clad in skin-tight black Kevlar, combat webbing, and night-vision goggles launched out from beneath the desk with the velocity of an artillery shell. The operative hit the carpet, slid on his knees, and leaped into a horizontal, flying bicycle dropkick that defied all known laws of Newtonian physics.

His heavy combat boots slammed square into Kitasan’s lacquered crimson oni kabuto.

*CRACK!*

Kitasan’s avatar, possessing three hundred points of heavy tactical armor and full health, was instantly hurled backward across the lobby, crashing into the reception wall with a violent thud before collapsing flat onto the carpet. 

The Cloaker landed in a perfect three-point crouch, spun his combat baton between gloved fingers, and leaned over Kitasan’s downed body. 

His synthesized, mocking voice cut through the comms with devastating toxicity:

*"Now go to the forums and cry like the little baby you are!"*

In Room 204, Kitasan’s jaw dropped so wide it nearly touched the floorboards. She stared at her greyed-out screen, where her character lay on the carpet with a pulsing skull icon over her chest.

"He... he kicked me," Kitasan whispered in stunned, sacred disbelief. "He slid out from under a wooden desk... and kicked me in the face with both feet... and then told me to visit an online discussion board to weep..."

"CLOAKER!" Nature screamed, her voice cracking as the full horror of Payday 2’s special unit roster dawned upon her. "HE'S BEATING HER WITH A STICK!"

"Nature-san!" Spe whimpered, still trapped in the Taser's electrical lock, her gun clicking uselessly on empty as sparks cascaded down her vest. "The yellow man is cooking my person like a salted mackerel!"

At that exact second, the heavy front double doors of the bank exploded inward off their brass hinges, splintering into flying chunks of painted timber.

Bain’s panicked warning cut through the deafening alarm: *"Holy shit, team! You got a Dozer on the floor! Keep your distance and aim for the visor!"*

Through the billow of white smoke and pulverized masonry stepped a colossal nightmare.

The operative was encased in three hundred pounds of olive-green, reinforced explosive-ordnance-disposal armor. His torso was as wide as a municipal tractor. Over his helmet sat a blast-shield visor crafted from three layers of bulletproof laminate glass, glowing dull green beneath the lobby lights. In his colossal, armored gauntlets, he hefted a drum-fed twelve-gauge automatic combat shotgun.

A deep, synthesized roar shook the headphones:

*"MAKE WAY FOR THE DOZER!"*

"A TANK!" Spe shrieked, tears springing to the corners of her eyes. "A WHOLE TANK JUST WALKED THROUGH THE DOOR!"

"A Bulldozer," Dia declared, though for the first time since the seminar began, a microscopic tremor of apprehension ruffled her polite aristocratic voice. Her fingers hovered over her keys. "Class four heavy shock assault unit. He possesses five thousand hit points and absolute resistance to bodily kinetic trauma. Only sustained cranial concussions through his facial visor plate can register critical damage."

The Bulldozer racked the drum-fed shotgun with a sound like a portcullis dropping: *KA-CHUNK*. 

He took one heavy, earth-shaking step into the lobby. His boots left scuff marks in the carpet.

*"I'M GONNA DROP ON THESE SHITHEADS LIKE A FUCKING PIANO!"* the Bulldozer bellowed, leveling the barrel straight down the corridor toward Dallas and Hoxton.

In Room 204, Nice Nature stared at the screen. 

Spe was convulsing in the customer lounge under high-voltage arcs. Kitasan was bleeding out against the reception wall, taunted by a ninja in night-vision goggles. Dia was pinned behind the final vault pillar with zero armor. And a walking bomb shelter was marching toward them, racking an automatic twelve-gauge.

Something snapped inside Nice Nature’s brain.

For months, she had been the sensible one. The girl who took third place. The girl who smiled politely on the podium, accepted her bronze sash, stepped aside for the flashier runners, and quietly packed away the communal water bottles. She was the one who avoided trouble, followed the rules, and kept her head down.

And now, in her own dormitory room, on a peaceful Saturday afternoon, a man in a green bomb suit was threatening to drop a musical instrument on her friends.

"Not in my room," Nature snarled through clenched teeth.

She hit the `3` key. 

Dallas hurled her final tactical smoke grenade directly into the center of the lobby. A thick, opaque cloud of grey chemical fog blossomed instantly, swallowing the Taser, the Cloaker, and the advancing Bulldozer in a blind, swirling haze.

"Dia-chan! Deploy your electronic jammers!" Nature commanded, her voice dropping into the cold, authoritative steel of a lead pacemaker at the fourth turn. "Right now!"

"Deploying ECM!" Dia answered instantly. 

Hoxton slapped a small black electronic module against the vault doorframe. An ear-splitting electronic feedback tone shrieked through the bank audio—a high-pitched electromagnetic pulse that disrupted municipal radio bands.

Inside the smoke cloud, the Taser dropped his weapon, clutching his helmet with both hands as the feedback fried his circuitry. The high-voltage lock broke. Spe’s avatar collapsed onto her knees, free of the current.

"Spe-chan! Crawl to the counter!" Nature yelled as she charged directly into the smoke cloud.

Dallas did not hesitate. Nature sprinted blind through the fog, using her auditory senses to track the Cloaker’s mockery. She rounded the reception desk, saw the black-clad operative raising his baton to finish Kitasan, and leveled her CAR-4.

*CRACK-CRACK-CRACK-CRACK!*

Four rounds of 5.56mm punched through the Cloaker’s night-vision goggles. The green lenses shattered. The Cloaker collapsed in a heap of Kevlar, dissolving into digital pixels.

Nature slid to her knees beside Kitasan, slamming the `F` key.

Dallas barked an Inspire command: *"Get the fuck up, Chains! You're not dying today!"*

Kitasan’s avatar surged to its feet with a burst of revived health, armor snapping back into place.

"I AM RESURRECTED!" Kitasan roared in real life, throwing her arms back so hard her chair nearly tipped over. "THE FIRE IS NOT EXTINGUISHED!"

"The Dozer!" Nature shouted, pointing her barrel into the thinning smoke.

The Green Bulldozer emerged from the haze, unfazed by the smoke, his heavy boots crushing porcelain shards beneath his soles. He raised his drum-fed shotgun, aiming directly at Dallas’s chest.

"ALLOW ME!" a crystalline, aristocratic voice rang through the comms.

Satono Diamond leaned forward over her mechanical keyboard, her knuckles gleaming white under the cyan LED strips, her emerald eyes ablaze with the razor-sharp predatory focus of an apex bloodline bearing down on the final furlong pole. 

"Tactical diversion initiated," Dia announced with absolute, icy calm.

Hoxton broke into a dead sprint. 

Without a single plate of ballistic armor, relying entirely on her Ghost perk deck’s seventy-five percent dodge probability, Dia dashed straight into the teeth of the Bulldozer’s fire.

*BOOM!* 

The Bulldozer fired his automatic shotgun. A devastating spray of heavy lead pellets ripped through the air where Hoxton had been half a second prior. The pellets pulverized a plaster column into flying powder, gouging deep craters into the brickwork. A small blue notification flashed on Dia’s HUD: *DODGE!*

Dia didn't flinch. She slid across the pulverized linoleum tiles, popped up directly behind the Bulldozer’s right shoulder, and raised her silenced Bernetti 9 pistol with two-handed tournament discipline.

"Target coordinates locked," Dia said, her voice dropping to a whisper that sent chills down Nature’s spine. "Facial laminate integrity: compromised."

Dia hammered her mouse button with blistering, inhuman speed.

*Thwip-thwip-thwip-thwip-thwip!*

Five hollow-point rounds slammed into the Bulldozer’s green glass visor in less than two seconds. The first two cracked the outer glaze. The third shattered the secondary laminate into spiderwebs. The fourth and fifth punched clean through the glass, blowing the heavy steel faceplate off the helmet with a violent, ringing *CLANG*.

Beneath the armor, the Bulldozer’s exposed face recoiled in shock, his eyes widening in pure disbelief at the sight of a suited robber wearing a gold Venetian mask standing on his heel.

Spe, scrambling on her knees, scooped up the fallen Taser's weapon pack, slammed a fresh magazine into her AMCAR, and fired three rounds into the Dozer's knee joint. "Take that, rude municipal officer!"

"FESTIVAL CLIMAX!" Kitasan screamed, stepping up beside Dia.

She shoved the twin barrels of her Reinfeld 880 directly into the Bulldozer’s unarmored collarbone.

*BOOOOOOOOOOM!*

The muzzle blast was a miniature sun inside the ruined lobby. 

The three-hundred-pound Green Bulldozer lifted off the floor, his shotgun flying from his hands, and crashed onto his back across the customer sofa with a sickening, definitive crunch. The heavy armor plates settled into the dust, completely motionless.

*Ping.*

A gentle, pneumatic chime echoed from the rear hallway.

The deafening roar of the thermal drill died away. The diamond bits dropped into the floor grating with a heavy, oily splash. 

The colossal four-ton circular steel vault door gave a soft, hydraulic hiss: *ssssssssss*. 

The twelve deadbolts retracted with a heavy, synchronized *CLACK-CLACK-CLACK*. The massive slab of bank alloy swung outward on its oiled hinges, revealing the warm, golden interior of the Harvest & Trustee vault.

Inside, stacked high upon three industrial steel tables, rested towering pyramids of banded green hundred-dollar bills.

In Room 204, the chaotic electronic synthesizers abruptly lowered into a soft, pulsing rhythm. 

Nice Nature dropped her hands from the keyboard onto the edge of the low table. Her chest rose and fell in ragged, trembling gasps. Sweat clung to her temples, dampening her auburn bangs, while her cardigan hung loose from one shoulder. 

She slowly turned her head to look around the room.

Special Week was sitting cross-legged on the tatami, clutching her mug of cold tea with both hands, staring at the open vault door with wide, reverent purple eyes. Kitasan was panting, her face flushed crimson, grinning from ear to ear like she had just won the Arima Kinen by ten lengths. And Satono Diamond was delicately smoothing her pleated skirt, her breathing slightly rapid, but her back once again as straight and elegant as a marble column.

Nature exhaled a long, shuddering breath through her teeth, slowly lifted her sleeve, and wiped the cold sweat from her forehead.

She looked at her screen, where Dallas stood surrounded by spent brass casings and smoking rubble, and pointed Dallas’s rifle toward the glowing green dollar icons inside the vault.

"Spe-chan," Nature said quietly, her voice trembling only slightly. "Go get the duffel bags."
