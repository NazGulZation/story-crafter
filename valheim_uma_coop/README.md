# Valheim Co-Op: The First-Rate Viking Odyssey

A 17-chapter comedic survival epic featuring King Halo, Gold Ship, Sakura Bakushin O, and Haru Urara playing *Valheim* in King Halo's dorm room across four LAN gaming sessions.

---

## Story Overview
- **Protagonist / POV**: King Halo (Third-Person Limited)
- **Setting**: King Halo's dorm room (Room 304), Tracen Academy
- **Game Biomes**: The Meadows, The Black Forest, The Ocean, The Plains (brief, traumatic encounter), The Swamp
- **Bosses Defeated**: Eikthyr (The Meadows), The Elder (The Black Forest), Bonemass (The Swamp)
- **Major Architecture**: King's Outpost (Stone-paved granite castle & workshop), The Aerial Citadel (Swamp treehouse fortress), The Leveled Skull Arena
- **Fleet**: The Karve, The Longship (18 cargo slots)
