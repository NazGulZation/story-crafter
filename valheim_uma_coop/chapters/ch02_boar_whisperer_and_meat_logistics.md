# Chapter 2: The Boar Whisperer and the Meat Logistics

Outside the dormitory windows, twilight settled over the Tracen Academy grounds in shades of bruised slate. The autumn rain had deepened from a noisy rattle into a steady, rhythmic drumming that insulated Room 304 from the outside world.

King Halo reached across the table, picked up an opened ceramic jar, and spooned a fresh measure of roasted barley into the teapot. The dry, nutty aroma mingled with the briny tang of soy-glazed seaweed crackers that Gold Ship had scattered across an empty paper plate.

"Pay attention to the interface," King commanded, tapping the glass of her secondary display where a fan-made game wiki lay open in three neatly arranged browser tabs. "Our avatars are currently operating at a maximum health pool of twenty-five points. A stiff breeze or an angry squirrel could dispatch us. Why? Because you three have been treating our dietary regimen like a roadside foraging picnic."

"Hey! Raspberries are delicious!" Urara said, her eyes shining in the blue monitor glow. She clicked her inventory, where four neat stacks of ruby-red berries occupied the top row. "And look, King-chan, I found yellow mushrooms in a hollow log! They glow in the dark like little lamps!"

"And they provide almost no sustained constitution," King countered smoothly. "Valheim operates on a multi-tier caloric index. To face significant challenges, one must consume three distinct nutritional profiles simultaneously. One fruit or vegetable, and two varieties of cooked protein."

"Protein!" Bakushin sat upright, her eyes flashing with intense academic focus. "Meat builds muscle! Muscle produces explosive acceleration! Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O volunteers to secure the highest-grade wild game in this province!"

"First, we require cooking facilities," King said. She guided her avatar to the stone hearth, selected her building hammer, and snapped a wooden Cooking Station directly over the open flames. Two iron hanging spits dropped into place above the crackling embers. "Observe. Raw meat is placed upon the iron spit. When it crackles and changes color, you must remove it immediately. If you leave it too long, it turns to carbonized ash."

"Oho! A culinary speedrun!" Gold Ship shoved two strips of raw neck tail and a boar flank onto the rack.

A satisfying, wet sizzle echoed through their headsets. White steam drifted from the hanging skewers. Three seconds passed. 

*Hiss-pop.*

The meat darkened into a rich, roasted golden-brown.

"Wait for it—" King began.

"Not on my watch!" Gold Ship shouted, reaching forward with her avatar. But instead of clicking the meat, she accidentally tapped the jump key, bounced her viking’s skull off the chimney mantel, and landed square in the campfire. 

Small flames engulfed Gold Ship’s rag pants. Her avatar shrieked in game audio, running in circles around the cabin.

*Crack.*

The two skewers let out a puff of black soot, transforming instantly into two black lumps of solid Coal.

"My roast!" Bakushin cried in despair. "The fuel of my sprint has been reduced to soot!"

"Gold Ship!" King groaned, rubbing her temples with both thumbs. "Step out of the hearth! You are setting the floorboards on fire!"

"A temporary setback!" Gold Ship dove headfirst through the front door, extinguishing her trousers in the river with a loud hiss. "Charcoal builds character, King! It cleanses the digestive tract!"

King sighed, pulled two fresh cuts of boar meat from her personal pouch, placed them onto the hooks, and waited with measured precision. The moment the meat hissed and popped, she clicked. Two steaming cuts of Cooked Meat dropped into her inventory. She placed them beside roasted neck tails and a handful of berries.

When she consumed all three, her health bar stretched across the bottom of the screen, expanding from a meager twenty-five to an impressive eighty-five hit points, accompanied by a thick, golden stamina bar.

"There," King said, her composure restored. "Now we possess the constitution of true warriors. However, our next hurdle is material. To face the lord of this realm, we require leather armor and crude bows. That means deer hides."

"Leave it to me!" Bakushin declared, grabbing her mouse. "Deer are swift! Therefore, they must be pursued with superior swiftness! I shall run them down on the open plains!"

"Bakushin, wait—"

Bakushin was already gone. Her avatar bounded across the river shallows, water splashing in white sheets beneath her sandals. On the opposite bank, a peaceful clearing opened beneath three towering oak trees, where a majestic stag and two does grazed in the tall grass.

Bakushin held down Shift. 

"Bakushin! Bakushin! Surrender your hides to the Class Representative—!"

Before her avatar covered ten yards, the stag’s head snapped up. An exclamation mark flashed above its antlers. With a sharp, terrified bark, the entire herd bounded into the air, leaping across the meadow at blinding speed and vanishing deep into the trackless hills.

Bakushin’s avatar skidded to a halt in the empty clearing, her stamina bar flashing gray.

"They... they fled!" Bakushin gasped, staring at her monitor in utter bewilderment. "They did not contest the straightaway at all!"

"Of course they fled!" King walked her avatar across the river, stopping beside Bakushin. "Deer possess exceptional olfactory and acoustic awareness. If you charge at them screaming like a freight train with the wind at your back, they will detect you from half a kilometer away. Crouch."

"Crouch?"

"Press the Control key," King instructed, demonstrating with her own avatar. King’s viking sank low into the clover, moving with slow, silent tread. A small eye icon appeared in the center of the crosshairs, half-closed. "This is stealth. When you break their line of sight and advance from downwind, they cannot hear your approach."

King drew a newly crafted Crude Bow, nocking a wood arrow. The bowstring groaned under tension. She aimed slightly above the shoulder of a doe that had emerged seventy paces away, accounting for the gentle gravitational drop of the shaft.

She released.

The arrow hissed through the autumn air and struck true. The doe collapsed in a shower of golden sparks, leaving behind two deer hides and three cuts of venison.

Bakushin watched the display, her eyes wide. She pressed the Control key on her laptop. Her avatar sank low into the turf. 

"Patience before the acceleration..." Bakushin murmured, her fingers steadying on the mouse. "A tactical pacing run. I understand, King-san! Even a sprinter must reserve her final burst for the opportune furlong!"

Meanwhile, three hundred yards to the north, a high-pitched giggle echoed through their voice comms.

"King-chan! Bakushin-san! Come look! Look what I found!"

King switched her camera view, panning across the rolling knolls. 

At the edge of a dense hazelnut grove, Haru Urara’s avatar was trotting in cheerful circles. Following closely behind her were two stout, dark-bristled wild boars, their red eyes flashing with blind animal fury as they snorted and charged.

"Urara!" King yelled into her microphone, her aristocratic poise shattering instantly. "Run! You are being pursued by hostile beasts!"

"They aren't hostile, King-chan! They're just energetic!" Urara laughed, deftly leading the two charging boars through a narrow gap in a circular wooden enclosure she had constructed out of roundpole fencing. "See? I built them a playpen!"

The moment both boars stormed inside, Urara slapped down a final wooden fence post, sealing the pen shut. 

The boars slammed their snouts into the pine timber, grunting in agitation. Urara stood on an adjacent wood pile, opened her inventory, and tossed down handfuls of bright yellow dandelions.

"Here you go, little piggies! Pretty flowers for lunch! Don't be mad anymore!"

In the mud below, the two boars snorted, nudged the yellow petals with their snouts, and turned away in complete disinterest, slamming their tusks against the logs.

"Eeeh?!" Urara gasped, her shoulders drooping beneath her fleece blanket. "They don't like my flowers, King-chan! Why won't Melon eat?!"

King glanced at her secondary monitor, skimming the survival entries. "Urara, wild boars do not consume floral decorations. The compendium states they require sustenance: forest berries, red mushrooms, or root vegetables."

"Oh!" Urara quickly opened her pouches, tossing down two generous handfuls of sweet raspberries and three red mushrooms.

The boars paused. They sniffed the berries and fungi. One by one, they bent their bristled heads and began to chew contentedly.

Small, glowing pink pixelated hearts floated upward from their backs into the evening air. A gentle status alert appeared above their heads:

`Boar is becoming acclimated (48%)`

King lowered her spear, exhaling a quiet breath of relief. "They... they are actually taming. Just keep wild predators away from the fence."

"Hehehe!" Urara beamed, hugging her knees inside her fleece blanket. "This one is named Carrot, and that one is named Melon! When they're happy, we can pet them!"

"Gyahaha! Nice work, Urara!" Gold Ship’s avatar trotted up, wielding a massive two-handed club. "If they act up, we just turn 'em into sausages! Maximum livestock efficiency!"

"Don't you dare touch Melon, Golshi-senpai!" Urara pouted, puffing her cheeks.

Before Gold Ship could reply, the amber twilight on their monitors abruptly vanished.

The sun dipped beneath the horizon. A deep, cold blue washed over the meadow hills. A somber brass chord droned through the audio, accompanied by the howl of a rising night wind.

In the top right corner of their screens, an icon of an icicle appeared: *Cold.* Health and stamina regeneration plummeted by half.

"The sun has set," King warned, drawing her circular shield. "Return to the homestead immediately. Darkness in this realm brings—"

A sharp, guttering hiss rose from the tall grass outside the fence.

Through the mist, six pairs of glowing, malevolent yellow eyes flared to life. Small, hunched wooden creatures with twisted root limbs and glowing moss on their shoulders emerged from the shadows, chattering in discordant wooden clicks.

`Greydwarf`

`Greydwarf Thrower`

And towering behind them in the gloom stood a massive wild boar with two jagged white stars floating above its snarling snout.

"Intruders!" Bakushin shouted, leaping onto a fence rail. "They seek to vandalize the Class Representative’s supply lines! All units, charge!"

"Maintain formation!" King ordered. "Do not scatter into the dark—"

"Party time, goblins!" Gold Ship roared. 

Her avatar charged into the pack, swinging her heavy wooden club in wide, sweeping arcs. But in her haste, Gold Ship had left the PvP toggle active in her inventory menu.

Her club whistled through the air, completely missed the lead Greydwarf, and caught Bakushin’s avatar squarely across the ribs with a deafening wooden *THWACK*.

"Gwaaaah!" Bakushin’s screen spun violently. Her avatar was propelled forty feet through the night sky, soaring over the wooden palisade and splashing headfirst into the freezing river. 

"Friendly fire! Friendly fire!" Bakushin shrieked, her voice cracking in the dorm room. "Gold Ship! You struck down your own vanguard!"

"Tactical repositioning, Bakushin! I granted you the high ground!" Gold Ship hollered, ducking as a volley of jagged river stones pelted her shoulders.

Two Greydwarves lunged toward the boar pen, raising crude wooden clubs to smash Urara’s fences.

"Melon! Carrot!" Urara cried, waving a burning resin torch to ward them off. "Shoo! Go away, bad root-men!"

King Halo stepped forward.

Her avatar planted its boots in the mud directly before the broken gate. She raised her circular Wood Shield, painted in the emerald green and brilliant white of the Halo racing silks. Unlike an unwieldy tower shield, the wooden round shield carried a swift 1.5x parry multiplier.

The two-star boar charged out of the shadows like a battering ram, its razor tusks lowered to tear her apart.

King timed the impact with razor focus. At the exact millisecond the boar’s snout struck the bronze boss, she tapped right-click.

*CLAAAANG!*

The parry sound rang out like a bronze bell. A white shockwave erupted from King’s shield, stumbling the massive boar backward into the dirt, staggered and completely vulnerable.

King lunged. Her Flint Spear flashed forward in a crisp, deadly three-hit combination.

*Thrust. Thrust. Pierce.*

The two-star boar disintegrated into a burst of golden smoke, showering the ground with six cuts of prime raw meat, four thick leather scraps, and a magnificent horned trophy.

Behind her, Bakushin charged out of the river like a vengeful water spirit, spearing the remaining Greydwarf Thrower through the chest, while Gold Ship’s wild club swings finally connected with the last root-fiend, smashing it into kindling.

Silence returned to the riverbank. The river gurgled peacefully in the darkness. Inside the pen, Melon and Carrot snorted softly, chewing their raspberries and mushrooms undisturbed.

In Room 304, King Halo lowered her hands from the keyboard and blew a gentle breath upward to shift a stray hair from her forehead.

She looked at her wristwatch. The hands pointed to 10:15 PM.

"A satisfactory defense," King said smoothly, picking up her teacup and taking a measured sip. The warm tea soothed her parched throat.

She guided her avatar back into the cabin, pulled out her building hammer, and constructed three sturdy wooden storage chests along the back wall. Using six wood planks and the lumps of charcoal salvaged from Gold Ship’s incinerated roast, she meticulously mounted a carved wooden sign above each chest, typing out their contents in crisp capital letters:

`[WOOD & STONE]`  
`[PROVISIONS & SEEDS]`  
`[LEATHER & TROPHIES]`

She sorted the hides, the cooked meats, and the flint arrowheads into their proper receptacles, leaving the inventory in immaculate order.

"We possess thirty arrows each, full leather tunics, and three cuts of cooked meat per person," King declared, looking across the table at her three companions. "Get some water. Clear your plates. When we log back in, we march upon the storm altar."
