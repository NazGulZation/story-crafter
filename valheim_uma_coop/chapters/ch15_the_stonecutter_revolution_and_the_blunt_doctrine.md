# Chapter 15: The Stonecutter Revolution and the Blunt Doctrine

By eight-forty in the evening, the air in Room 304 carried the warm, savory aroma of toasted seaweed rice crackers and fresh rainwater. Rain beat a steady tattoo against the glass, while the dorm radiator hissed steam into the cozy room.

Around the low cherry-wood table, four laptops hummed in unison. At the head of the table, King Halo reviewed her metallurgical ledger under the warm circle of her brass desk lamp. On her screen, twin smelters belched dense black clouds of soot into the virtual sky above King’s Outpost.

*WHOOSH. CLINK. THUD.*

Every few seconds, a glowing bar of black metal slid down the stone troughs:

`Smelted: Iron (x1)`  
`Smelted: Iron (x1)`  
`Smelted: Iron (x1)`

"Maintain fuel cadence, Bakushin," King commanded, her voice steady and crisp. "Keep both kilns packed with birch logs. We require thirty bars of charcoal for every twenty bars of raw iron scrap."

"Understood, Class Representative!" Bakushin shouted, feeding fresh timber into the smoldering kilns with rapid clicks. "Charcoal replenishment at one hundred percent efficiency! The furnaces shall never starve!"

A soft, hesitant knock sounded at the door.

*Tap, tap, tap.*

The handle clicked downward, and the door creaked open an inch. A pair of chestnut horse ears poked through the gap, twitching curiously, followed by the round, earnest face of Special Week. In her hands, Spe cradled a large earthenware bowl draped in a clean cotton kitchen towel, radiating fragrant, sugary steam.

"Um... excuse me?" Special Week called out softly, sniffing the air. "Is Golshi-san in here? Trainer-san was looking for her because she vanished right after track practice without sweeping the equipment shed—"

Spe froze mid-sentence. Her purple eyes widened as she took in the scene.

On the floor, Gold Ship was hunched intensely over her laptop screen, chewing on a rice cracker while muttering under her breath. On Golshi's display, her avatar was diligently gripping the handles of a heavy wooden cart, hauling fifty granite boulders up a grassy slope toward the outpost gate.

Special Week’s jaw dropped. The towel slipped slightly off the warm bowl.

"Ehhhh?!" Spe gasped, stepping into the room with wide eyes. "Golshi-san is... doing chores?! You skipped sweeping the real shed to haul heavy rocks in a video game?!"

Gold Ship didn't even lift her eyes from the monitor. "Silence, Hokkaido country pumpkin! This isn't chores, this is imperial expansion! I'm the chief logistical freight hauler for King’s Outpost! Show some respect to the working class!"

"Special Week," King Halo addressed her with composed dignity, smoothing her skirt. "Wipe your feet at the genkan. Do not loiter in the doorway; you are letting the autumn draft into the room."

"Ah, right! Pardon the intrusion!" Special Week quickly kicked off her slippers, placed them neatly on the mat, and padded over to the low table on her socks. She set the earthenware bowl down in the center of the table, pulling back the cloth with a proud, beaming smile. "My mom sent a huge box of autumn sweet potatoes from Hokkaido! I steamed a whole batch in the dormitory kitchen! Please have some, everyone!"

"Sweet potatoes!" Urara gasped, her eyes turning into sparkling stars. "They're purple and yellow and super warm!"

"An exceptional caloric supplement!" Bakushin declared, immediately taking a steaming golden half. "Straight-line sprinters require complex carbohydrates for muscle recovery! Thank you, Special Week!"

"Mmph, acceptable quality," King said, taking a delicate slice onto a porcelain saucer. The potato was piping hot, sweet as chestnut puree, and fragrant with earthy warmth. "You have my gratitude, Special Week."

Gold Ship snatched two whole potatoes at once, stuffing one directly into her cheek like a squirrel while continuing to steer her stone cart with one hand. "Gyahaha! Hokkaido starch power! The cart moves twenty percent faster now!"

Spe giggled, kneeling comfortably between King and Urara. She leaned forward, resting her chin on her hands as she peered at the glowing screens. 

"So this is that Viking game everyone is talking about," Spe murmured, watching Urara's avatar happily chop wood. Then Spe's gaze drifted to the lower-left corner of King’s display. She tilted her head, pointing a finger at three small icons pulsing beside the health bar.

"Um, King-chan? What are those three little picture boxes next to your health bar? One looks like a sausage, one is a bowl of green soup, and one is a roasted drumstick."

"Those," King said, adopting her most dignified pedagogical posture, "are our dietary allocations. In Valheim, nutrition is the foundational pillar of physical competence. An unbuffed avatar possesses an utterly pathetic base pool of twenty-five maximum health points. Without food, a single stray stone from a woodland creature or a bump from a wild beast will kill you instantly."

Spe gasped, clutching her own stomach. "Twenty-five health?! That's so tiny! Can't you just eat three giant steaks to make your health super big?"

"An intuitive inquiry, yet fundamentally flawed," King explained, tapping the screen with her pen. "The system enforces three ironclad nutritional laws. First: a character may only consume three items of sustenance simultaneously. Second: identical foods cannot be stacked. Consuming three cuts of meat is mechanically barred. Every single meal slot must be filled by a distinct culinary item."

Bakushin chimed in with passionate class-president fervor: "And third, Special Week! Every food provides a distinct physiological distribution! Sausages, crafted from entrail casings and wild boar meat, provide fifty-five maximum health points and eighteen stamina! Turnip Stew, simmered with garden vegetables, provides fifty-five maximum stamina and eighteen health! And Deer Stew contributes forty-five health and fifteen stamina!"

King nodded approvingly at Bakushin's recital. "Precisely. Starting from our base pools of twenty-five health and fifty stamina, combining these three specific dishes elevates our combat avatars to one hundred and forty-three maximum health and one hundred and thirty-eight maximum stamina. If we ate sweet potatoes or simple berries, our stamina bar would deplete after two swings of a weapon, leaving us paralyzed in the swamp mud."

"Whoa..." Special Week breathed, her eyes shining with genuine admiration. "So you have to plan your meals carefully, just like our sports nutrition classes! That's so cool!"

"Indeed," King said, a pleased smirk gracing her lips. "Now, observe the metallurgical fruits of our labor."

King directed her avatar to the central workbench. With over four hundred raw scrap iron bars in queue, their production bottlenecks had shattered. She opened the building menu, scrolling down to the newly unlocked craftsmanship tab.

`Craft: Stonecutter (Iron x2, Wood x10, Stone x4)`

She clicked the mouse.

A heavy wooden worktable topped with a spinning circular whetstone and an iron-toothed chisel snapped into place beside the main hearth. The moment the stonecutter materialized, an entire library of masonry blueprints illuminated King’s interface:

`Unlocked: Stone Wall 2x1`  
`Unlocked: Stone Wall 4x2`  
`Unlocked: Stone Arch`  
`Unlocked: Stone Floor 2x2`  
`Unlocked: Paved Road`

King Halo’s eyes narrowed with fierce aesthetic determination. 

"Look at this," King muttered, her fingers dancing across the WASD keys as she surveyed the perimeter of King’s Outpost. "Look at these crude, splintered wooden palisades. Pine logs shoved into the dirt like toothpicks. It is an eyesore. An indignity to any settlement bearing the Halo family crest."

Gold Ship swallowed her sweet potato with a loud gulp. "Uh-oh. The ojou-sama has that look in her eye. The look she gets when the cafeteria runs out of French butter."

"We are modernizing," King declared, slamming a small fist onto her ergonomic gel rest. "Gold Ship! Empty that cart into the masonry stockpile! We are erecting a fortress worthy of civilized society!"

For the next twenty minutes, Special Week watched in complete fascination.

King dismantled the wooden stakes with crisp strokes of her hammer. In their place, she laid thick, interlocking foundations of dressed grey granite. Massive two-by-four stone blocks snapped into plumb alignment. High defensive ramparts rose along the cliffside, complete with crenelated battlements, sweeping stone archways, and a grand paved cobblestone boulevard running from the Longship pier straight to the great hall's double doors.

"Sugoi..." Spe whispered, her mouth slightly open. "It turned into a real castle! King-chan, this looks like an aristocratic palace in Europe!"

"Naturally," King said, tilting her chin up with proud satisfaction. "A first-rate leader accepts nothing less than architectural perfection."

"Now then," King continued, her tone snapping back to cool, calculated pragmatism. "Admire the architecture later. We must address our primary operational target."

King opened her world map. Far to the south, deep within the tangled expanse of the southern marshlands, a crimson boss marker pulsed gently against the fog of war.

`Bonemass`

"During our sweep of the Drowned Crypts," King explained, tapping her fountain pen against the open binder, "we discovered the Vegvisir runestone that registered this location. To challenge it, we must offer ten Withered Bones at its skull altar."

"Ten big bones!" Urara reported proudly. "I kept them safe in my backpack!"

"Now, pay attention to the tactical audit," King said, flipping to her sketched resistance matrix. "Bonemass possesses unique elemental and physical immunities. First: Spirit damage. Completely immune. Second: Stagger. Completely immune. He cannot be stunned. Furthermore, because we are operating in a four-player cooperative server, the boss's health pool scales by forty percent per additional player. From a base of five thousand, his health is elevated to eleven thousand points!"

Special Week gasped, her hands flying to her cheeks. "Eleven thousand health points?! That's huge!"

"Indeed," King said. "And look at the physical resistances. Pierce damage suffers a seventy-five percent penalty. Against Bonemass, pierce attacks deal only twenty-five percent damage."

Spe tilted her head. "Wait... so if you shoot him with arrows or poke him with a spear...?"

"Arrows and spears will strike him for two to four damage per volley," King stated coldly. "You would exhaust two hundred arrows and break three bows before carving away a tenth of his health pool."

"And slashing weapons," King continued, glaring pointedly at Gold Ship's sword icon, "suffer a fifty percent damage penalty. Blades get stuck in the gelatinous grease."

Special Week looked worried. "Then how are you supposed to beat an eleven-thousand health monster?!"

King smiled. It was a sharp, predatory smile of absolute tactical superiority.

"Blunt force," King pronounced. "The runic texts are unequivocal. Bonemass is weak to concussive impact. When struck with a heavy hammer or mace, his internal bone lattice shatters. Blunt strikes trigger a one-hundred-and-fifty percent yellow critical damage multiplier!"

"Six times more damage than a spear!" Bakushin barked. "The All-Mace Doctrine reigns supreme!"

King walked her avatar to the glowing iron forge. With over three hundred refined iron bars resting in the chests, she began drafting the wargear:

`Craft: Iron Mace (Iron x20, Wood x4, Leather Scraps x3)`  
`Upgraded: Iron Mace (Quality 2)`  
`Upgraded: Iron Mace (Quality 3)`

Heavy, brutal weapons materialized from the anvil: flanged heads of tempered black iron mounted on reinforced ash-wood shafts, spiked with cruel studs designed to pulverize armor. King forged three identical maces, dropping two into the trade chest for Bakushin and Urara.

Meanwhile, Gold Ship was rummaging through the specialized armory tabs, her eyes glittering with manic delight.

"Hold on, hold on, hold on!" Golshi yelled, grabbing King’s shoulder and pointing frantically at the lower forge interface. "Look at this beauty! Look at this absolute unit! The Iron Sledge!"

`Iron Sledge`  
`Requirements: Iron x30, Ancient Bark x10, Ymir Flesh x4, Draugr Elite Trophy x1`  
`Damage: Blunt 55`  
`Effect: Massive Area Concussive Shockwave`

Special Week leaned in, reading the recipe. "Y-Ymir Flesh? What is that?! That sounds terrifying!"

"We bought it from a creepy little blue dwarf merchant named Haldor deep in the Black Forest!" Gold Ship grinned, clicking the craft button. "One hundred and twenty gold coins a chunk! And now, I forge the ultimate weapon of mass destruction!"

*CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!*

A colossal two-handed siege hammer appeared in Gold Ship’s inventory: a massive block of riveted black iron bound to a thick oak beam.

Gold Ship equipped it, stepped out onto the paved courtyard, and unleashed an overhead smash against the stone ground.

*KABOOM!*

A concussive shockwave rippled out in a ten-meter circle, kicking up dust and sending wooden practice dummies flying backward.

"GYAHAHAHA!" Gold Ship roared into her headset. "FEEL THE POWER! I AM THE GOD OF THUNDER! I AM AN EARTHQUAKE IN RUNNING SHORTS!"

Special Week jumped a foot into the air, clutching her ears before bursting into amazed laughter. "W-Wah! That was incredible! The whole screen shook!"

"Do not crack my newly paved courtyard, Gold Ship!" King scolded, though she could not hide the approving nod in her voice.

She walked her avatar over to the armor rack, donning her upgraded Iron Scale Mail. The polished black plates clinked with quiet menace. Beside her, Bakushin gripped her new spiked iron mace, swinging it with relentless sprint cadence. Urara hefted her own mace with both hands, giving it an earnest, determined nod.

Inside Room 304, the wall clock chimed a single soft note. Nine-fifty-five PM.

Special Week blinked at the clock, then gasped, scrambling to her feet.

"Oh no! It's almost ten o'clock!" Spe squeaked, gathering the empty earthenware bowl and the clean towel. "If I don't get back to Room 201 right now, Suzuka-san will think I got lost on the way back from the kitchen!"

"A sensible precaution," King said, standing up with graceful poise. "Thank you for the sweet potatoes, Special Week. They were exceptionally nourishing."

"Good luck against the big slime monster!" Spe beamed, bowing energetically at the door. "Make sure Golshi-san doesn't get squished, King-chan!"

"Hey! I do the squishing around here!" Gold Ship yelled after her.

Special Week giggled, waved goodbye, and quietly shut the door behind her, her soft footsteps hurrying down the carpeted hallway.

King Halo reached across the table, taking the empty plates and setting them neatly on the side tray. She poured fresh cold water into four glasses, sliding one toward each of her companions.

"Hydrate," King said calmly, checking the durability meters on her hotbar. "Our weapons are forged. The doctrine is set. Next, we prepare the ground for war."
