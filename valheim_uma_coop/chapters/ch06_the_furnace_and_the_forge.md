# Chapter 6: The Furnace, The Forge, and The Golden Carrot

By one o'clock in the afternoon, the autumn sun stood high over the academy grounds, casting golden rectangles of light across the parquet floor of Room 304.

King Halo adjusted her white laptop on its cooling stand, cleared an empty tea saucer from her workspace, and unrolled a sheet of grid paper beside her mousepad. On it, she had drafted a miniature industrial blueprint with pencil and ruler.

"We have returned with ten Surtling Cores and forty units of raw stone," King said, her voice brimming with executive authority. "Now, we establish our metallurgical precinct. Gold Ship, Bakushin, unload the timber from your inventories."

"Timber arriving at maximum velocity!" Bakushin clicked rapidly, dropping four neat bundles of fifty beech wood logs onto the riverbank.

King selected her building hammer. 

Outside the north wall of the longhouse, where the river breeze carried away stray soot, she laid the foundations for their heavy industry.

She snapped down the first structure: a massive, beehive-shaped dome of dark river stone.

`Charcoal Kiln`  
`Cost: 20 Stone, 5 Surtling Cores`

Beside it, she constructed a towering, cylindrical furnace fitted with two iron chutes and a glowing stone crucible at its base:

`Smelter`  
`Cost: 20 Stone, 5 Surtling Cores`

The moment the structures materialized, their red core runes pulsed with fiery inner heat.

"I know how this works!" Gold Ship shoved twenty beech logs into the feeder hatch of the Charcoal Kiln.

The stone kiln let out a deep, hollow roar. Thick black smoke billowed into the sunny blue sky from its rounded chimney. Ten seconds later, the lower chute spat out a heavy, crackling pile of solid black Coal with a satisfying clatter.

"Coal secured!" Gold Ship shouted. "Now feed the big boy!"

Bakushin loaded twenty chunks of coal into the left hopper of the Smelter, while King fed green-veined copper ore into the right chute.

The furnace roared to life. 

Within the belly of the stone tower, the coal flared white-hot. The stone hissed. From the lower crucible, a glowing, viscous stream of orange liquid metal spilled into a sand mold, solidifying into a sparkling, mint-green ingot with a sharp hiss of cooling steam:

`Copper (x1)`

"It works!" Urara clapped her hands in the dorm room, her eyes sparkling. "King-chan, it makes shiny metal bricks!"

"Indeed," King smiled, a flush of pride coloring her cheeks. "Now, load the tin ore. We require a two-to-one ratio: two parts copper to one part tin to produce bronze."

For thirty minutes, the industrial rhythm settled over their camp. The kiln turned fresh wood into coal; the smelter consumed raw ore, filling their wooden storage chests with stacks of polished copper and gleaming silver tin.

With six copper ingots and four pieces of coal in her pouch, King entered the newly built covered workshop wing beside the cabin. She selected her hammer and placed a heavy iron-and-stone anvil upon a solid wooden stump:

`Forge`  
`Requirements: 10 Wood, 6 Copper, 4 Stone, 4 Coal`

A golden crafting menu unfolded before King’s eyes.

At the very top of the list sat the cornerstone of the new era:

`Bronze`  
`Requirements: 2 Copper, 1 Tin`

King clicked *Craft*.

Her avatar raised a blacksmith's hammer. Sparks showered across the dark workshop as hammer met anvil with three ringing, musical chimes: *Clang! Clang! Clang!*

A gleaming, golden-brown ingot dropped into King’s hands, polished and radiant beneath the workshop lanterns:

`Bronze (x1)`

"The Bronze Age," King whispered, her chest swelling. "Our true foundation begins."

"Hey! King!" Gold Ship leaned across the table, tapping her screen frantically. "Check the forge menu! Check the nails!"

King opened the forge tabs. 

`Bronze Nails (x20)`  
`Cost: 1 Bronze`

King smithed one bronze ingot into twenty sturdy, squared bronze nails. She deposited them into Gold Ship’s inventory.

Gold Ship let out an unhinged cackle that startled the crows outside King's dorm window. She sprinted her avatar out the front door, pulled out her building hammer, and clicked the misc tab.

*THWACK.*

Standing on the riverbank, framed in sturdy pine timbers and two spoked wooden wheels, was a pristine, fully functional two-wheeled Cart.

`Cart`  
`Requirements: 20 Wood, 10 Bronze Nails`

"IT LIVES!" Gold Ship screamed. She backed her avatar into the leather pull-harness, grabbed the wooden handles, and began tearing across the meadow turf in sweeping zig-zags. "OUTTA THE WAY! GOLSHI EXPRESS LOGISTICS IS OPEN FOR BUSINESS!"

"Gold Ship, do not overload it on the incline!" King warned, consulting her wiki tab. "The physics engine accounts for cargo weight and slope resistance!"

Naturally, Gold Ship ignored the warning. She loaded five hundred units of quarry stone into the cart and attempted to haul it up a thirty-degree hillside. Her avatar’s stamina bar drained to zero in two seconds, the wheels seized on the grass, and the heavy cart began rolling backward downhill, dragging Golshi’s screaming avatar into a thicket of gorse bushes with a violent wooden rattle.

"Gyah! The laws of physics are harassing me!" Gold Ship hollered, scrambling out from under the axle.

King shook her head with a wry smile and spent four wood, eight bronze, and two leather scraps at the forge to craft a weapon of forestry:

`Bronze Axe`  
`Requirements: 4 Wood, 8 Bronze, 2 Leather Scraps`

Bakushin eagerly accepted the gleaming bronze axe. She sprinted across the river to an ancient, white-barked Birch tree that had previously defied their flint tools with the mocking prompt *Too hard*.

Bakushin swung the bronze axe.

*CHOP. CHOP. CRACK.*

The white birch snapped at the base, toppling in a shower of golden autumn leaves. When the logs split, a rare, pale hardwood dropped onto the turf:

`Fine Wood (x20)`

"A breakthrough!" Bakushin shouted. "The timber of royalty! Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O has harvested the finest lumber in the province!"

Bakushin then dashed to the dark forest border, felling a straight, towering pine tree whose heavy resinous trunk yielded dense cylinders of **Core Wood**.

"And look what I made for Urara," King said softly, guiding her avatar to the garden plot beside the south wall.

She combined five bronze bars and five core wood logs at the forge to craft a long-handled agricultural tool:

`Cultivator`  
`Requirements: 5 Core Wood, 5 Bronze`

King handed the implement to Haru Urara.

"For me?" Urara gasped, clutching her wireless mouse with both hands.

"You gathered three bags of wild carrot seeds from the forest underbrush," King said, her voice softening into genuine warmth. "A first-rate estate requires a proper agricultural supply. The land is yours, Urara."

"Waai!" Urara squealed. 

Her avatar trotted to the meadow plot beside Melon and Carrot’s pen. She equipped the Cultivator. With gentle, rhythmic clicks, she tilled the wild grass into dark, rich, aerated loam. In straight, careful rows, Urara planted each delicate white carrot seed into the soil, being mindful of spacing so the prompts read *Healthy* rather than *Need more space*.

"And remember, Urara," King added, pointing at the botanical guide. "Once these carrots mature, we do not consume them all. You can plant mature carrots back into the cultivated soil as Seed Carrots. Each one blossoms into white flowers that yield three seeds each. It is a compounding agricultural harvest."

"Like magic flowers that make more carrots!" Urara beamed, hugging her knees inside her fleece blanket. "Melon and Carrot will have endless snacks!"

To complete the domestic revolution, King smelted ten bars of tin and mounted a broad, dark kettle above the cabin's stone hearth:

`Cauldron`  
`Requirements: 10 Tin`

The meager diet of raw berries and neck tails became a relic of the past. Using fresh venison, boar flanks, wild honey, and yellow mushrooms, King prepared their first batch of true gourmet trail rations:

`Deer Stew (Health: +45, Stamina: +15)`  
`Minced Meat Sauce (Health: +40, Stamina: +13)`

Finally, King returned to the forge to forge her personal defensive masterpiece. 

Using ten bars of bronze and four pieces of wood, King forged a circular, bronze-rimmed parry shield:

`Bronze Buckler (Quality 1)`  
`Requirements: 10 Bronze, 4 Wood`  
`Block Armor: 16 (Parry Block Armor: 40) - Parry Bonus: 2.5x`

King spent additional bronze bars to reinforce the rivets and polish the surface, upgrading it at the forge to Quality 3, elevating its base block armor to twenty-eight. Polished to a mirror shine, the buckler bore the formidable **2.5x parry multiplier** that would turn incoming brute strikes into staggered openings.

King Halo equipped the shield. The polished bronze caught the afternoon sun, gleaming with lethal elegance.

She closed the forge interface, pushed back her ergonomic chair, and stood up to stretch her back.

The kitchen timer on King’s phone sounded a cheerful chime, signaling the end of their initial shift.

King walked to the tea counter, set four steaming ceramic bowls of hot vegetable broth and seasoned rice upon a wooden tray, and brought them to the table. Beside each bowl, she placed a clean pair of lacquered chopsticks and a fresh paper napkin.

"Our industry is established," King said, handing a bowl to each girl with graceful hospitality. "Eat your lunch. We have balanced our copper and tin tallies, established our crop reserves, and mastered the basic physics of the cart. This afternoon, we venture north to harvest the Ancient Seeds and secure our passage across the sea."
