# Chapter 8: The Maiden Voyage of the Karve and the Golden Shore

Night fell over Tracen Academy at half-past seven. The autumn chill tapped against the double-glazed French doors of Room 304, but inside, the air was warm, smelling of savory nori rice crackers and freshly steeped roasted hojicha tea.

King Halo sat before her glowing laptop screen, double-checking the maritime manifest on her digital ledger.

"Eighty bronze nails, thirty fine wood planks from Bakushin's birch harvest, ten cured deer hides, and twenty units of sticky pine resin," King read aloud, her voice crisp. "Our vessel is prepared for launch. Everyone report to the river estuary."

At the mouth of the river where freshwater met the rolling gray tides of the open ocean, King placed a temporary shoreline workbench beneath a thatch awning.

She selected her building hammer and clicked the shipbuilding icon.

*SPLAAAASH!*

A magnificent wooden vessel materialized three paces out in the surf, bobbing gracefully on the ocean swells. 

`Karve`  
`Hull Health: 500 - Cargo Hold: 4 Slots`

Crafted from pale, curved ribs of fine birch wood and bound in gleaming square bronze nails, the sleek, single-masted longboat possessed an elegant, sweeping bow, a single rectangular woolen sail, and four central rowing benches.

"Behold," King said, her chest swelling with naval pride. "The flagship of our armada. A first-rate vessel worthy of the Halo house."

"YAAAHOOO! WE’RE PIRATES NOW, BABIES!" Gold Ship’s blue-hooded avatar scrambled over the gunwale, seized the wooden steering oar at the stern, and began furiously spinning the rudder in circles. "Call me Captain Dread-Golshi of the Seven Seas! Hoist the skull and crossbones! Prepare the broadside cannons!"

"There are no cannons in the tenth realm, Gold Ship, and do not beach the hull on the sandbar!" King scolded, climbing aboard with her Bronze Buckler strapped securely to her shoulder. 

Bakushin hopped onto the triangular deck at the very tip of the prow, standing ramrod straight into the sea breeze. "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O shall serve as chief lookout! I will detect any navigational hazards at maximum range!"

Urara curled up happily on the forward cargo bench beside four wooden storage crates, hugging her knees. "The boat rocks like a cradle, King-chan! It feels so nice!"

"Gold Ship, observe the navigation widget," King instructed, pointing to the circular dial in the top right corner of their screens. "The golden longship marks our hull. The golden circle with the blowing cloud indicates wind direction. If the wind blows into the black arc of our sail, we are in the dead zone. You must lower the sail and use the oar to paddle, or tack at forty-five degrees."

"Aye-aye, Admiral! Full steam ahead!" Gold Ship slammed the movement key forward.

The woolen sail dropped from the yardarm, catching a stiff easterly gust with a loud, resounding *SNAP*. 

The Karve surged forward into the open sea, its sharp birch prow slicing through white-capped ocean swells with exhilarating speed. Salty spray hissed against the wooden hull. Behind them, their starting meadows and the smoky chimney of King’s Outpost shrank into a tiny green smudge on the horizon.

For fifteen minutes, the voyage was pure, glorious adventure. Gulls wheeled through the dusk sky. Gentle waves rocked the timbers, and the rhythmic creak of the mast provided a soothing cadence to their journey south.

Then the ocean currents carried them past an unfamiliar landmass.

Ahead, the gloomy wall of dark fir trees and gray mist abruptly receded. The shoreline opened into a breathtaking, sun-drenched coastal savanna. 

Rolling hills of brilliant, golden amber grass undulated beneath a cloudless, cerulean blue sky. A warm, balmy breeze ruffled the surface of the shallow coastal waters. Dotting the golden slopes were lush green bushes bearing clusters of plump, glowing orange-yellow berries that looked like ripe, oversized raspberries.

The acoustic audio transformed into a serene, pastoral melody—warm acoustic strings and the gentle rustling of wind through wheat.

Haru Urara’s eyes went wide. She gasped, leaning toward her screen. "King-chan! Look! Look over there!"

"Urara, what is it?" King asked, checking the map.

"The grass is gold! And look at those bushes! There are giant golden strawberries everywhere!" Urara clapped her hands in pure delight. "They look so sweet and juicy! Can I pick some? Please, King-chan, just five little berries!"

"Wait," King said, leaning closer to her monitor. "The biome boundary along our vector should be Black Forest. That terrain does not match the geographic profile of—"

Before King could finish her sentence, Gold Ship steered the Karve into the sandy shallows. "Shore leave granted! Bring back a basket for the captain!"

Urara leaped joyfully off the birch prow, her boots splashing through the crystal-clear knee-deep surf. She bounded onto the warm golden sand, running straight toward a lush bush of glowing yellow berries.

The moment her boots crossed the threshold of the golden turf, an ancient, blood-red title card faded across all four screens:

`Plains`

In Room 304, King Halo’s blood ran colder than river ice.

Her secondary monitor flashed with the survival compendium database. Her eyes scanned the biome threat tier: *Meadows -> Black Forest -> Swamps -> Mountains -> PLAINS (TIER 5 ENDGAME).*

"URARA, GET BACK ON THE BOAT!" King screamed, jumping out of her chair with such force her tea saucer rattled. "GET BACK! THAT IS NOT A MEADOW! THAT IS AN ENDGAME EXTINCTION ZONE!"

Urara froze on the sand, blinking at her screen. "Eh? But King-chan, the golden berries are right—"

*BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZT.*

From the tall golden grass twenty paces ahead, a high-pitched, metallic drone drilled through the air like an industrial punch spinning at ten thousand revolutions per minute.

A creature the size of a raven erupted from the grass. It possessed black-and-yellow chitinous stripes, four translucent wings beating in a blur, and a needle-sharp proboscis eight inches long.

`Deathsquito`

The insect accelerated across the golden sand at one hundred miles per hour—faster than an arrow, faster than any sprint speed in the realm.

*STAB.*

A single, sickening puncture sound echoed through their headsets.

A spray of catastrophic red damage numbers exploded across Urara’s screen: *[-98 Pierce Damage!]*

Haru Urara’s avatar folded instantly in mid-stride. Her health bar vanished into nothingness. She dissolved into a small wooden tombstone resting on the warm golden sand, a single dandelion blossom floating beside it.

The server alert flashed across the top of all four monitors in stark white text:

`Urara_RunRun was killed by a Deathsquito.`

Complete, stunned silence fell over Room 304.

Haru Urara stared at her grayed-out screen, her wireless mouse slipping from her trembling fingers. Her lower lip wobbled beneath her fleece blanket.

"W-Waaaah!" Urara cried out, clutching her cheeks in utter shock. "A bug! A little mosquito poked me and I exploded! King-chan, I exploded!"

"Gyahaha! She got assassinated by a flying toothpick!" Gold Ship hollered, though she frantically jammed her keys to reverse the steering oar. "Reverse! Back up! Mayday! Mayday!"

"The tombstone!" Bakushin shouted, her fists slamming onto the table. "Urara’s cultivator! Her leather clothes! Her stack of carrot seeds! We cannot abandon the Class Representative’s agricultural supply line!"

The Deathsquito circled above Urara’s tombstone, its needle proboscis twitching as its compound eyes locked onto the Karve sitting fifty yards offshore in the shallows.

"Bakushin, execute emergency extraction!" King commanded, her voice dropping into icy, surgical resolve. "Activate Eikthyr! Recover the inventory! I will provide parry suppression!"

"CLASS REPRESENTATIVE SAKURA BAKUSHIN O ENGAGING MAXIMUM VELOCITY!"

Bakushin hammered her hotkey. The golden crest of Eikthyr flared to life across her screen. 

Wearing her lightweight Troll Leather tunic, Bakushin leaped from the Karve’s prow into the water. She hit the sand at full sprint, tearing across the beach at blinding speed. She slid onto her knees beside Urara’s wooden tombstone, mashed the interact key, and vacuumed every item into her inventory in half a second.

The Deathsquito wheeled in midair. 

*BZZZZZZT!*

The insect dove toward Bakushin’s back like a guided missile, its needle stinger poised to pierce her spine.

King Halo vaulted over the gunwale of the Karve, landing in the shallow surf with a concussive splash. She lunged forward, planting her boots in the wet sand directly behind Bakushin.

King Halo raised her circular **Bronze Buckler**. 

She tracked the blurred trajectory of the diving needle with steady concentration, timing the impact with razor precision.

At the exact millisecond the needle struck the bronze boss, King right-clicked.

*CLAAAAAAAAANG!*

A blinding concussive shockwave of golden parry sparks erupted from King’s shield. 

The Deathsquito’s high-speed dive met an immovable wall. The 2.5x parry multiplier shattered the insect's momentum, throwing the bug backward into the sand, completely stunned and spinning on its back.

King stepped forward with ruthless aristocratic authority. She drew her Bronze Spear and drove the heavy metal tip downward, skewering the insect squarely through its chitinous thorax.

*SPLAT.*

The Deathsquito possessed exactly ten health points. It popped like a soap bubble, disintegrating into two gray feathers and a single needle.

"EVERYONE ON BOARD! ROW! ROW FOR YOUR LIVES!" King roared, grabbing Bakushin by the collar and shoving her over the boat's gunwale.

Gold Ship threw her avatar against the oars, rowing with frantic, splashing strokes until the Karve slid off the sandbar and hit the deep ocean swell. Gold Ship caught a fresh westerly gust, billowed the sail to maximum, and the Karve tore away into the open sea, leaving the golden, murderous shores of the Plains far behind them.

In Room 304, King Halo collapsed back into her ergonomic chair, panting as if she had run a mile on the turf track herself. Her face was flushed, her knuckles white around her mouse.

Urara respawned at the longhouse bed, her avatar walking out to receive her recovered gear from Bakushin.

King reached out with trembling fingers, picked up her glass of hojicha tea, and took a long, stabilizing sip.

She set the glass down on its coaster, looked sternly across the table at Gold Ship and Urara, and issued an ironclad decree:

"Listen to me carefully. We do not look at yellow grass. We do not step on yellow grass. We do not pick yellow berries. If you see a single blade of golden wheat, you turn the ship around and sail until your arms fall off. Is that understood?"

"Understood, King-chan!" Urara sniffled, nodding vigorously inside her blanket.

Forty minutes of cautious coastal navigation later, the Karve approached the true destination.

Ahead, shrouded in thick, gloomy gray sea mist, loomed the rocky northern headland of the southern continent. Towering black firs and pines stretched endlessly into the fog, where the red tree marker pulsed atop a high mountain plateau.

They brought the Karve into a sheltered cove, beached the hull on the pebbles, and erected a sturdy palisade perimeter with a lean-to shelter and a blazing campfire.

King walked to the shore, tied the Karve’s mooring line securely to a carved wooden post, and logged their coordinates on the map.

"The beachhead is established," King said softly, looking up into the dark, silent forest where the ancient roots awaited them. "Rest. Tomorrow... we fell The Elder."
