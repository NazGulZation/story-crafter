# Chapter 11: The Bitter Lessons of Alchemy and the Corpse Run Chain

By eight o’clock, the cardboard pizza boxes had been stacked neatly in the hallway recycling bin. 

Inside Room 304, King Halo switched on the brass desk lamp, its pool of sharp white light illuminating an open leather-bound notebook and a fresh pot of hot sencha green tea. The bitter, grassy aroma filled the room, cutting through the heavy tension that lingered like swamp mist over the table.

On their screens, four avatars stood in the central longhouse of King’s Outpost, stripped down to ragged linen loincloths, their health pools reset to a pathetic base of twenty-five hit points.

Their bronze armor, their fine wood bows, fifty bowls of venison stew, and the irreplaceable brass Swamp Key lay submerged fifty miles to the south, guarded by an army of undead archers, foul green blobs, and blood-drinking worms.

"Listen to me," King said, her voice steady, cool, and surgically precise. "We were not defeated by superior intellect. We were defeated by systemic negligence. I have audited the compendium entries. The Swamp operates under three absolute environmental laws."

She tapped her fountain pen against the notebook page, where three bullet points had been drawn in crisp black ink.

"Law one: The *Wet* debuff is permanent. You cannot dry your boots in the swamp rain. Your stamina recovery is compromised by fifteen percent at all times, and health regeneration drops by a quarter. Law two: Deep water is a fatal trap. If your boots leave the mud and you begin to paddle, you cannot swing a weapon, you cannot jump, and the leeches will devour you. Law three: Poison is non-negotiable. Without medical countermeasures, a single sting from a leech or a blob is a delayed death sentence."

"So how do we counter the poison, King-chan?" Urara asked, her brown ears drooping slightly as she clicked her empty inventory grid. "Melon and Carrot can't eat poison flowers either!"

"Alchemy," King replied, guiding her naked avatar to the workshop. "The compendium outlines a pharmaceutical brewing chain. We craft a specialized fermenting vessel, brew a concentrated syrup in the cauldron, and ferment it into restorative mead."

Beside the stone hearth, King selected her building hammer and constructed an imposing, barrel-shaped wooden apparatus bound in bronze bands and fitted with a brass spigot:

`Fermenter`  
`Requirements: 30 Fine Wood, 5 Bronze, 10 Resin`

Next, she approached the iron Cauldron hanging over the hearth fire. She drew four jars of wild honey, five glowing blue thistle flowers that Urara had gathered from the Black Forest underbrush, ten lumps of coal, and one raw neck tail from the pantry chest:

`Mead Base: Poison Resistance`  
`Requirements: 10 Honey, 10 Coal, 5 Thistle, 1 Neck Tail`

With a bubbling hiss, the thick, dark green syrup materialized in King’s pouch. She carried the cauldron base to the Fermenter and poured the sweet, herbal mixture through the top hopper.

A deep, rhythmic bubbling sound echoed through the wooden cask. The status prompt appeared:

`Fermenting (Estimated time: 2 in-game days)`

"The fermentation cycle requires time," King said, turning to look across the table. "Roughly twenty minutes in real time. We cannot sit idle. Our primary wargear and the Swamp Key remain in the mire. We must execute a recovery operation."

"An unarmed recovery sprint!" Bakushin stood up from her chair, her competitive spirit blazing back to life. "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O shall lead the vanguard! With zero equipment, my sprint velocity will reach theoretical maximums!"

"Gyahaha! The legendary naked run!" Gold Ship cheered, cracking her knuckles. "Nothing to lose, everything to gain! If a leech bites my toes, I'll bite it back!"

King distributed backup crude wooden clubs, four spare torches, and three bowls of honey to provide a baseline constitution of sixty health points. 

"Review the recovery mechanics," King instructed. "When you reach your headstone with an empty inventory, pressing the interaction key instantly recovers your entire gear payload and equips your armor. Furthermore, reclaiming your spirit activates the ancestral *Corpse Run* buff: a fifty-second surge that grants seventy-five percent damage reduction, seventy-five percent less sprint stamina consumption, and one hundred and fifty units of bonus carrying capacity. Once you touch your stone, you run like a god."

They set out on foot along the southern coastline, crossing the narrows where the meadow hills met the dark, weeping tree line of the Swamp.

Ahead lay the scene of their slaughter.

Through the murky gray rain, two hundred yards into the stagnant marsh, the four wooden tombstones bobbed among the wreckage of their shattered Karve. Patrolling the muddy hummocks around the graves were two Draugr warriors and a green, gelatinous Blob hopping through the reeds.

"Bakushin," King whispered into her headset, crouched low in the gorse bushes at the swamp border. "Activate Eikthyr. Do not engage any hostile. Jump from fallen log to fallen log. Avoid the deep water channels. Touch your tombstone, grab your belongings, and sprint straight back."

"CLASS REPRESENTATIVE SAKURA BAKUSHIN O EXECUTING RECOVERY DASH!"

Bakushin hammered her key. The golden crest of the stag flared across her screen. 

Her naked avatar shot forward like a released arrow, boots tearing through the dark peat. 

She vaulted over a mossy mudbank, cleared a four-foot puddle of black water, and landed on a half-submerged fir trunk just as a leech snapped its jaws through the foam inches beneath her heels.

*THWIP.*

A Draugr arrow whistled through the rain, grazing her shoulder: *[-18]*. 

Bakushin kept her momentum. She slid on her knees through the slick marsh grass, slammed into her wooden gravestone, and pressed the interaction key.

Her full inventory—Troll Leather tunic, bronze axe, and bow—flooded back into her slots in a single fraction of a second.

Instantly, a roaring golden buff icon flared across Bakushin’s screen, accompanied by a triumphant war horn chord:

`Corpse Run (50s)`  
`Damage Taken: -75% - Jump/Run Stamina: -75% - Carry Weight: +150`

"I HAVE SECURED THE EMBLEM!" Bakushin yelled. "THE POWER OF THE FALLEN ANCESTORS COURSES THROUGH MY STRIDE!"

With the Corpse Run buff active, Bakushin sprinted like a mythical demigod. She grabbed Urara’s adjacent tombstone, hoovering up her friend's gear, and bounded back across the mudbank with double-speed strides.

Seeing Bakushin's success, King and Gold Ship charged in tandem, sliding onto their respective headstones. With their gear recovered, the double Corpse Run buff ignited on both their screens, cloaking their avatars in golden ancestral armor.

Then the swamp erupted.

*CRACK-SPLINTER!*

From a deep, bubbling pool of black mire twenty yards to their left, the water parted with a sickening suction sound.

Rising out of the mud stood a towering, four-legged monstrosity formed of petrified, weeping swamp roots and twisted ironwood. It was twenty feet across, its massive root-limbs moving like the legs of a giant wooden spider, a hollow knot in its chest glowing with sickly green bog light:

`Abomination`

The beast let out an ear-splitting, hollow wooden screech that rattled their laptop speakers. It raised one colossal, tree-trunk foreleg and slammed it downward with the force of a pile driver.

*BOOOOM!*

The impact tore a five-foot crater in the peat, splashing black mud across Bakushin's screen.

"A TREE MONSTER! A GIANT CRAB MADE OF FIREWOOD!" Gold Ship shrieked. "RUN! RUN FOR THE MEADOWS!"

"Form up on the boundary!" King Halo charged into the shallow water, brandishing her newly reclaimed circular Wood Shield. 

A Draugr warrior lunged from the reeds with a rusted iron axe, swinging for Bakushin’s back. 

King timed the parry with surgical precision: *CLAAANG!* The parry shockwave staggered the Draugr into the reeds, allowing Bakushin to blow past, carrying Urara’s gear across the stone border.

Behind them, the colossal Abomination stomped through the shallows, snapping dead fir branches in its wake. But as its wooden legs touched the edge of the sunny meadow clover, the beast halted. It let out a frustrated, hollow groan, turned its massive root-shoulders, and submerged slowly back into the murky depths of the swamp.

All four avatars collapsed onto the bright green grass of the Meadows, panting as the *Wet* debuff finally evaporated under the afternoon sun.

Bakushin dropped Urara’s gear onto the turf. Urara immediately re-equipped her orange dress and flower crown, tears of relief shining in her eyes. "My clothes! My seeds! Bakushin-san, you're a hero!"

"Class Representative duty... successfully fulfilled!" Bakushin gasped, her avatar panting on the grass.

Right on cue, a loud, wooden *THUNK-HISS* echoed from their longhouse across the river.

Inside the workshop, the Fermenter’s brass tap opened automatically. Six round glass flasks, filled with glowing, luminescent green nectar, popped out onto the wooden floorboards with a pleasant clatter:

`Poison Resistance Mead (x6)`  
`Duration: 10m - Poison damage taken reduced by 75%`

King Halo walked her avatar into the cabin, collected the six warm flasks, and walked back out onto the riverbank.

She traded two flasks to each girl.

"Drink," King commanded, her aristocratic composure fully restored, sharpened by hard-won battlefield experience. "We hold immunity against the poison of the mire. We have recovered our wargear and the Swamp Key."

She raised her Bronze Buckler, pointing the polished rim toward a towering, massive ancient tree whose gnarled black trunk dominated the swamp canopy a half-mile away.

"We do not build upon their mud," King declared. "Tomorrow, we conquer the canopy."
