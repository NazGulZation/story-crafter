# Chapter 4: Into the Pine Mist

The bedside clock in Room 304 read 1:15 AM.

The torrential downpour had finally exhausted itself, tapering into a quiet, clinging autumn mist that blurred the distant streetlamps of the academy campus. Inside the darkened dorm room, the air had grown thick and warm, heavy with the dry exhaust of four hard-worked graphics processors, the sweet aroma of strawberry gummy wrappers, and the quiet fatigue of four pairs of burning eyes.

King Halo clicked her mouse with deliberate precision.

At the level-two workbench inside their timber cabin, three hard-carved implements lay completed on the wooden counter:

`Antler Pickaxe (x3)`  
`Durability: 100/100 - Piercing: 18`

"Take them," King said in a hushed, gravelly whisper, rubbing the tension from the back of her neck. "One each for Bakushin, Gold Ship, and Urara. Check your inventory weights. Leave all non-essential dandelion blossoms and raw meat in the designated chests."

"A tool of industry!" Bakushin declared, keeping her voice down to an intense stage-whisper as she equipped the pickaxe onto her avatar's back. "With this, Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O shall carve roads through any mountain pass!"

Outside the cabin door, Gold Ship was rapidly clicking through her building hammer menu. "Alright, where's the wagon? I'm building us a two-wheeled Ferrari for the loot!"

She hovered her cursor over the Cart icon in the misc tab. A sharp, red missing-material alert flashed across her interface: *Missing requirement: Bronze Nails (x10).*

"Hey! What kind of rip-off is this?!" Gold Ship yelled, kicking King's desk leg. "Why do I need shiny bronze nails to build a wooden cart?! It’s just planks and wheels!"

"Because you cannot assemble a heavy cargo axle out of loose twigs, you delinquent!" King said, adjusting her headset. "Bronze requires smelting copper and tin together in an industrial kiln. We do not possess a single ingot yet. We haul our ore on foot."

"Barbarians!" Gold Ship groaned. "Actual manual labor! My delicate wrists!"

"Pick up your pickaxe and move out," King commanded, strapping on her circular Wood Shield and Flint Spear. "Urara, do you have your torch?"

"Right here, King-chan!" Urara’s avatar held up a flickering resin torch, casting a warm orange circle of light across the grass. "I packed ten cooked meats and twenty yellow mushrooms for snacks!"

"Then let us proceed," King said. "We march north toward the ridge."

The expedition set out on foot under the pale light of the northern moon. 

They crossed the shallow river ford, Bakushin scouting ahead in long, loping strides while King guarded the rear. With the Eikthyr blessing active, their stamina recovered with astonishing rapidity, allowing them to traverse the rolling meadows without stalling.

After ten minutes of steady marching, the gentle hills began to steepen. 

Ahead, the landscape changed with unsettling abruptness.

The bright, sun-dappled meadow turf ended at a jagged line of dark gray rock. Rising beyond it stood a dense, impenetrable wall of ancient vegetation. Towering black pines and ancient firs stretched eighty feet into the gray sky, their massive interlocking boughs choking out the moonlight. A heavy, greenish-gray mist clung to the ground like swamp water, swirling around moss-covered roots the size of wagon wheels.

The cheerful meadow flute cut out. 

In its place, a low, ominous cello chord droned through their headsets—a slow, mournful bow across gut strings, punctuated by the unsettling scuttle of unseen claws through dry pine needles and the distant, guttural groan of rotting wood.

A cold gray runic banner faded onto their screens:

`Black Forest`

In the quiet dorm room, Haru Urara pulled her pink fleece blanket tight up to her chin, her eyes wide as she leaned toward her screen. "It's... so dark in there, King-chan. The trees look like giant spears."

"Stay close," King whispered, her throat dry. "We stay in a tight diamond formation. Gold Ship, leave the cart near the tree line so we are not weighed down if we must maneuver."

They crossed the stone border, stepping under the canopy of the ancient firs.

The temperature debuff hit immediately: *Cold.* Even beneath the pine needles, the damp chill seeped through their leather boots. 

*Crack.*

Twenty yards ahead, the mist parted. Three figures stepped out from behind a colossal mossy pine trunk. 

Unlike the weak, brittle creatures of the meadows, these forest denizens were built of thick, petrified ironwood. One was a hulking brute with knotted roots for shoulders and glowing crimson eyes; the other two carried gnarled wooden staves topped with smoking animal skulls.

`Greydwarf Brute`  
`Greydwarf Shaman (x2)`

"Hostiles detected!" Bakushin whispered urgently, drawing her flint knife. "Class Representative initiating preemptive strike!"

"Bakushin, wait for the—"

One of the Shamans raised its staff. A foul, sulfurous yellow cloud of poisonous spores erupted from its jaw, rolling across the moss. 

Bakushin leaped sideways, her boots clearing the poison cloud by inches thanks to her enhanced stamina. She darted behind the Shaman, driving her flint blade into its exposed root-spine with three lightning-fast stabs.

The second Shaman lunged toward Urara, but King Halo was already there. 

King stepped into the lane, raised her green-and-white circular shield, and met the Shaman's staff with a crushing shield-bash that broke its guard. King drove her flint spear through its wooden throat, shattering it into a pile of ancient resin and gray wood.

"Outta my forest, ugly!" Gold Ship roared, bringing her heavy club down onto the Brute's skull with a two-handed overhead smash, turning it into kindling.

The skirmish ended as quickly as it had begun, leaving three piles of wood, stone, and glowing blue eyes in the damp clover.

"A clean victory," King exhaled, wiping her brow. "Now, search the hillside. Look for rock formations with a dull, greenish sheen."

They fanned out through the pine fog, their boots crunching softly over dead needles and fallen cones. 

"King-chan!" Urara’s voice called out from fifty yards up a rocky ravine. "Over here! There's a giant rock with shiny lines on it!"

King, Bakushin, and Gold Ship scrambled up the slope.

Embedded in the hillside, half-buried under moss and thick pine roots, lay an enormous, rounded boulder forty paces wide. Veins of rich, lustrous green and coppery orange twisted through the gray granite like frozen lightning.

King brought her crosshairs over the stone. A prompt appeared:

`Copper Deposit`

"We found it," King said, a surge of genuine triumph warming her chest. "Copper ore! With this and the tin deposits along the coastline, we can construct a smelter. We can forge true bronze armor, bronze axes, and proper swords!"

"Yahoo!" Bakushin pulled the Antler Pickaxe from her belt, stepping up to the rock face. "Let the industrial sprint begin!"

*CLINK!*

Bakushin swung. The sharp, hardened point of Eikthyr's antler struck the metallic vein. A shower of brilliant orange sparks burst into the gloomy forest, accompanied by a sharp, metallic ringing tone that echoed far across the ravines.

*CLANK! CLINK!*

Urara joined in, her little pickaxe striking the opposite edge. Chunks of gray stone chipped away, revealing glittering lumps of raw mineral:

`Copper Ore (x4)`  
`Stone (x38)`

Bakushin eagerly scooped up every piece of rock and ore, stuffing her inventory slots to the brim. 

Suddenly, a flashing red anchor icon appeared in the bottom corner of her screen: *Encumbered (322/300).*

Her stamina regeneration halted completely. Her avatar dropped its posture, slowing to an agonizing, leaden waddle.

"King-san!" Bakushin cried out in dismay, her keyboard clattering in panic. "My maximum velocity... has been curtailed! My boots feel as though they are set in wet concrete!"

"Because copper ore weighs ten kilograms per chunk and you have thirty pounds of loose quarry stone in your pockets!" King snapped. "Only take what you can carry!"

"Gyahaha! We're rich anyway!" Gold Ship laughed, hammering her pickaxe into the rock. "We're gonna buy out the entire Tracen cafeteria with bronze bullion!"

King opened her mouth to warn them about the noise—about how sound travels in the stillness of the deep woods—when the audio abruptly cut out.

The ambient cello music stopped dead.

The screen shuddered. The virtual camera jolted vertically by two inches, rattling the display. From the high branches of the surrounding pines, showers of brown needles and loose cones rained down upon their avatars' helmets.

*THUD.*

A deep, bone-rattling bass shockwave reverberated through their laptop speakers.

*THUD. THUD.*

"Uh... King?" Gold Ship’s fingers hovered over her keys. "Is the mountain having an earthquake?"

"No," King whispered, her blood running cold as she looked at the mini-map. 

Fifty yards up the ravine, between two massive, hundred-foot pines, the gray fog tore apart.

Stepping over a fallen redwood trunk as if it were a toothpick came a nightmare.

It stood eighteen feet tall, its skin a mottled, leathery blue the color of deep river ice. A thick ruff of black moss grew across its colossal shoulders, and two hollow, terrifying black eyes burned from beneath a heavy brow ridge. 

Clutched in its massive, four-fingered blue fist was an entire uprooted, thirty-foot pine tree, its broken branches stripped like barbed wire, wielded effortlessly like a crude war club.

A monstrous red boss bar stretched across the top of all four monitors:

`Troll`

"W-Waaaah!" Urara shrieked, pressing herself flat against King's floor cushions. "A blue giant! A giant with a tree!"

"Gyahaha! Look at the size of that guy!" Gold Ship yelled, her survival instinct completely overridden by absolute gamer mania. "Batter up, blueberry! Let's see your fastball!"

Gold Ship sprinted her avatar forward, attempting a sliding tackle at the beast's colossal blue kneecap.

The Troll swung.

Gripping the thirty-foot pine trunk with both hands, the beast leaned back and swept the entire tree in a thunderous, horizontal 180-degree arc at knee height.

*KABOOOOM!*

The log connected with the physics of a catastrophic train derailment.

Two full-grown fir trees sheared at the trunk, exploding into flying timber and splintered branches. 

Gold Ship’s avatar was swatted out of the air like a fly, propelled eighty yards through the pine canopy in a spinning parabola of limbs and rag cloth before smashing violently into the granite face of a distant cliff.

A spray of red damage numbers erupted across her screen: *[-104]*.

Gold Ship’s health bar dropped from 110 down to a hair-thin sliver of exactly 6 HP. A sickening, wet bone-crunching sound echoed through her headset.

In Room 304, Gold Ship threw herself backward off her cushion, clutching her ribs and howling in theatrical agony. "HOLY SMOKES! MY SPINE! HE HIT A FIVE-HUNDRED-FOOT HOMERUN! CALL AN AMBULANCE!"

The Troll let out an earth-shattering, bass-heavy roar that rattled the dorm room windowpanes. It raised the pine trunk vertically above its head with both hands, locking its terrifying black eyes onto Haru Urara and Bakushin, stepping forward to crush them into the granite.

King Halo looked at the sheer, colossal mass of the creature. She looked at their tiny flint spears and antler pickaxes.

She recognized instant, catastrophic annihilation in the arc of that pine trunk.

King leaned straight into her microphone and screamed with every decibel of authority she possessed:

"RETREAT! ALL UNITS, FULL TACTICAL RETREAT! RUN FOR THE MEADOWS!"

Chaos erupted in Room 304.

Bakushin slammed her thumb onto Shift, but her avatar merely took one heavy, dragging step forward. Her stamina bar remained dead at zero.

"King-san! I cannot sprint! The Class Representative is anchored to the moss!"

"Waaah! Me too, King-chan! I have too many rocks!" Urara whimpered in terror as the shadow of the colossal pine club fell across her screen.

"DROP THE STONE!" King shrieked, her voice cracking across the room. "OPEN YOUR INVENTORY AND DUMP THE QUARRY STONES! DUMP THE EXTRA WEIGHT!"

Bakushin and Urara frantically dragged stacks of heavy granite from their bags. Piles of gray rocks tumbled onto the moss.

The red anchor icon vanished. Their green stamina bars flared back to life!

"BAKUSHIN! TACTICAL SPRINT RESTORED!"

Bakushin’s avatar grabbed Urara’s avatar by the shoulder, dragging her through the moss at blinding speed. Behind them, the Troll brought the pine club down with apocalyptic force: *BOOOOM!* A ten-foot crater exploded in the earth where they had stood a split-second prior, launching dirt and shattered stone across the forest.

"Waaah! It threw a boulder! King-chan, it's throwing rocks!" Urara squealed, her hands trembling as she navigated through the maze of fir trunks.

King brought up the rear, keeping her eyes locked on Bakushin and Urara while deafening, splintering crashes shook the woods behind her. The Troll charged through the timber, snapping full-grown fir trees like dry matchsticks in its single-minded pursuit. 

Leaves and shattered branches rained across her camera. Her stamina bar dipped, but the Eikthyr blessing kept her legs pumping.

Ten yards. Five yards.

Ahead, the gloomy gray mist began to thin. The dark, claustrophobic canopy parted.

With a final, desperate leap, all four avatars burst through the tree line, tumbling over the gray stone border and sprawling face-first into the bright, sunlit clover of the Meadows.

Behind them, at the very edge of the dark forest, the colossal blue giant came to a halt.

Its massive head turned, sniffing the sunny air of the open plains. It let out a low, frustrated rumble that shook the pine boughs, turned its massive shoulders, and lumbered slowly back into the shadowed depths of the firs, vanishing into the gray mist.

The heavy, terrifying bass audio faded away.

The cheerful acoustic flute of the Meadows swelled back to life, accompanied by the gentle chirping of birds and the soft gurgle of the nearby river.

In Room 304, the silence returned with stunning finality.

Gold Ship lay sprawled flat on her back across the floor, staring up at the darkened chandelier, panting with breathless, giddy laughter. "Gyahaha... he swatted me... into the next postal code..."

Bakushin leaned back in her desk chair, her chest heaving, her forehead damp with sweat, staring at her monitor with wide, exhilarated eyes. "We... we outran it! A tactical withdrawal executed with zero casualties!"

"Hehehe..." Urara peeked out from beneath her fleece blanket, her face flushed red with excitement. "That was so scary! But we got four shiny copper rocks, King-chan! Look!"

King Halo sat at her desk, her hands resting on the edge of the mahogany table. Her shoulders were stiff, her fingers ached from six continuous hours of intense gaming, and her hair was slightly disheveled.

She looked at her inventory screen.

Sitting in the top row of her pouch, glowing with a soft, promising luster, were four chunks of raw Copper Ore.

A quiet, triumphant smile touched the corners of her lips.

"Yes," King said softly, her aristocratic poise slipping back into place like a well-worn silk glove. "A first-rate escape. And a first-rate start."

She guided her avatar back inside the warm, smoke-ventilated cabin, closed the wooden door, and brought up the system menu. She clicked *Save and Exit*.

One by one, the four monitors went dark.

King Halo stood up from her chair, stepped across the tangled web of extension cords, and walked to the window. She pulled the velvet curtain aside. Outside, the rain had completely stopped. Along the eastern horizon, the first faint, cool violet light of dawn was beginning to break over the Tracen Academy track.

King turned back to the room. 

Gold Ship was already fast asleep on the rug, using King’s velvet bolster as a pillow. Urara was curled into a small, warm ball inside her pink blanket, her breathing soft and rhythmic. Bakushin was sitting slumped in her chair, her chin resting on her chest, snoring softly.

King Halo smiled, picked up a spare cashmere duvet from the wardrobe, and gently draped it over Urara and Bakushin.

Then she set her empty teacup beside the kettle, closed her laptop lid with a quiet, satisfying click, and slipped beneath her own covers, resting her eyes for the battles that awaited them in the Bronze Age.
