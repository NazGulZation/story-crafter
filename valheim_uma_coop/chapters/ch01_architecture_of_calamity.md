# Chapter 1: The Architecture of Calamity

Rain hammered the French windowpanes of Room 304 with the steady clatter of gravel against glass. Inside, the heavy mahogany tea table—usually reserved for Noritake porcelain cups and lavender-scented study planners—disappeared beneath a snarl of black power cables, three multi-outlet surge protectors, and four humming gaming laptops.

King Halo adjusted the cuffs of her silk cardigan, counted to ten in her head, and picked up her ceramic teapot. 

"I agreed to this arrangement under one explicit condition," King said, her voice crisp as she poured steaming roasted barley tea into a pristine cup. The nutty, toasted scent of the grain drifted up through the dry plastic heat radiating from four exhaust fans. "This is a tactical leadership seminar. A demonstration of poise, communication, and first-rate coordination. It is not an excuse to turn my living quarters into a convenience-store stockroom."

"Gyahaha! Relax, King! You talk too much before the race even starts!" Gold Ship leaned back against King’s velvet bolster, her bare heels drumming an irreverent rhythm against the carpet. A bag of sour pickled-plum rice crackers rested precariously on her knee. She reached over with salty fingers, grabbed a wireless mouse, and clicked the glowing start prompt. "Look at this title screen! Viking valkyries! Axes! Mead! Today, we conquer the tenth realm, baby!"

"Gold Ship, please use the wet wipe before you touch that mouse," King snapped, her left eye twitching. "That is an ergonomic laser sensor."

"I am ready!" Sakura Bakushin O sat ramrod straight on an imported leather desk chair she had dragged across the hallway, her posture so rigid her spine resembled an iron ruler. Her fingers hovered over her laptop’s keyboard like sprinters poised in the starting blocks. "As Class Representative, I have thoroughly reviewed the primary objective! We shall conquer this wilderness by advancing straight forward at maximum velocity! Speed is the ultimate foundation of leadership!"

"U-Um, King-chan?" A soft, hesitant voice spoke from the corner of the low table. Haru Urara peeked out from beneath a pink fleece blanket, her hands clutching a pastel notebook mouse. On her screen, the character creation menu displayed a female viking with wild brown hair and a bright orange tunic. "Can we pick flowers in this game? Look, the little green man on the menu has pretty grass around his boots!"

King exhaled through her nose, set the teapot onto its cork coaster, and sat down before her own sleek white notebook. "Yes, Urara. You may gather flora. Provided it serves an orderly purpose in our settlement. Now, everyone enter the server password. It is *FirstRateKing01*—with capital letters on the proper nouns."

Four keys clattered in unison. 

The screens flickered black, replaced by a roaring gray gale. A colossal, feathered valkyrie soared between scudding storm clouds, clutching their four avatars in its talons before dropping them unceremoniously onto a grassy knoll.

Surrounding them stood five towering megaliths carved with crude runes. The ambient music swelled—a gentle, lilting acoustic flute paired with the rhythmic chirping of meadow birds and the rustle of wind through oak boughs.

"We have arrived!" Bakushin announced to the ceiling. "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O has made landfall!"

"Quiet down, the dorm monitor is on this floor today," King hissed, slipping on her headset. "Now, look around. The stone in the center contains an inscription. Let us approach it with dignity and—"

"Hey, look, a giant crow!" Gold Ship yelled.

A massive black raven with glowing runes along its wings fluttered down atop a rock, squawking an introductory greeting from the Allfather. Gold Ship’s avatar—a towering, barefoot viking stripped to ragged undergarments—immediately lunged forward with swinging fists.

"Hand over the feathers, you overgrown pigeon! Dinner is served!" Gold Ship’s avatar punched the raven squarely in the beak.

The raven let out an indignant screech and vanished in a puff of blue feathers.

"Gold Ship!" King shrieked, slamming a palm onto her desk. "That was the tutorial guide! The Allfather's messenger!"

"He looked shifty, King! Real warriors don't take advice from birds with bad haircuts!"

"King-chan, look!" Urara’s avatar wandered toward a cluster of white birch trunks near the tree line. "There are yellow dandelions here! And red berries! Can I eat them?"

"Pick them, Urara, but do not consume them yet," King commanded, opening her inventory menu to inspect the empty grid. "Our immediate priority is tools. The tutorial stone indicates we require wood and stone to craft a basic stone axe. Search the ground for loose branches and river pebbles."

"Understood!" Bakushin shouted. "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O shall secure all available timber in the region immediately!"

Before King could utter a syllable of warning, Bakushin slammed her pinky finger down onto the Shift key. 

Her avatar broke into a furious, headlong sprint across the rolling meadows, boots tearing up clods of green turf. She dashed forty meters in three seconds, cleared a small ditch, and skidded to a halt in front of a thick beech tree.

"Take this! Bakushin! Bakushin! Bakushin!" 

Bakushin hammered her mouse button. Her avatar began pummeling the trunk of the massive beech with bare fists. Dull, wet thuds echoed through the game audio. 

*Thud. Thud. Thud.*

"Bakushin, stop! You cannot fell a full-grown beech tree with bare knuckles!" King said, clicking her tongue. "Look at your interface!"

On Bakushin’s screen, the slender yellow-green stamina bar in the lower left corner flashed red, turned dull gray, and emptied entirely. Her avatar dropped its hands, slumped forward, and began panting with heavy, labored gasps. Each punch had dealt exactly 1.2 points of blunt damage to a tree with eighty health points, while draining thirty stamina. A small red alert popped up: *Low Stamina.*

"Guh... my sprint... has stalled!" Bakushin gasped, her own shoulders dropping in sympathy with her avatar. "The ground here possesses unusual resistance!"

"You exhausted your entire energy pool in five seconds without eating any breakfast!" King collected five fallen branches and four loose stones into her crafting tab, clicking the axe icon. A crude Stone Axe materialized in her avatar’s grip with a satisfying wooden click. "Observe. A lady gathers the proper implements first."

"Hey, hey, King! Check this out!" Gold Ship called out.

King swung her camera around. 

Fifty yards up the hillside, Gold Ship stood beside a towering, forty-foot beech tree, brandishing a freshly crafted stone axe. She had already chopped a deep wedge into the pale wood. The trunk trembled.

"Gold Ship, wait," King said, a cold premonition tightening the muscles in her neck. "Look at the slope. Which way is that trunk leaning?"

"Gravity is an illusion invented by the Tracen curriculum committee!" Gold Ship cackled, driving her axe into the wood with a final, echoing crack. "Timber, losers!"

The great beech snapped at its base.

The sound resembled the crack of a hunting rifle. The massive green canopy tilted, accelerated, and smashed downward with thunderous force. The impact struck an adjacent birch tree, severing it cleanly at the roots.

Two colossal, two-ton hardwood trunks were now rolling down the forty-degree grassy slope like runaway artillery shells.

"Out of the way!" King yelled, hammering her keyboard to dive sideways. Her avatar rolled into a ditch just as the birch log tore past, gouging a brown furrow through the wildflowers and missing her shoulder by two inches.

Downhill, Bakushin was still recovering her stamina. Her avatar stood frozen, hands on its knees, panting helplessly before the beech tree she had been punching.

"Bakushin, dive!" King screamed.

Bakushin looked up. On her monitor, an enormous cylinder of solid beech bark filled the entire screen.

"A Class Representative never retreats from incoming—"

*CRUNCH.*

The log plowed through Bakushin at twenty miles per hour with the concussive clatter of a bowling ball obliterating a single wooden pin. A spray of red damage numbers erupted across the grass: *[-64]*. 

Bakushin’s avatar folded in half, spun twice in the air, and dissolved into a small wooden chest marked with a crude headstone.

A server-wide message flashed across the top of all four screens in stark white lettering:

`Bakushin_Pres_1200 was crushed by Falling Tree.`

The dorm room went dead silent, save for the hum of laptop fans and the patter of rain outside.

Bakushin stared at her grayed-out monitor, her mouth open in a perfect 'O'. 

Then Gold Ship collapsed sideways onto King’s velvet floor cushion, clutching her stomach and roaring with laughter so hard she kicked the table leg, rattling the porcelain teapot. "Gyahaha! Did you see that?! Strike! Perfect ten-pin strike! She got flattened like a piece of dried kelp!"

"Gold Ship! You absolute menace!" King stood up from her chair, her face flushing crimson. "You nearly wiped out the entire expedition before we even established a camp! And look at my cushion! You got plum dust on the seam!"

"W-Waaah!" Urara dropped her mouse and covered her eyes. "Bakushin-san turned into a box! King-chan, Bakushin-san died!"

"She did not die permanently, Urara, she will respawn at the stones," King said, pressing two fingers firmly against her temple to stave off an impending migraine. She sat back down, grasped her mouse, and drew a slow, stabilizing breath. "Bakushin. Click the respawn button. Walk—do not run—back to your headstone and retrieve your belongings."

"Understood!" Bakushin shouted, her competitive fire instantly rekindled. "I shall retrieve my honor with an even faster dash!"

"I specifically said *do not run*!"

Ten minutes of agonizing coordination later, Bakushin’s avatar had retrieved her loincloth and flint stones, Urara had amassed a hoard of forty raspberries and fifty dandelions, and King had located a defensible elevated knoll beside a gentle river bend.

"Here," King declared, planting her avatar on the level ridge. "The river provides freshwater and an open line of sight. We shall construct our base of operations. An orderly, symmetrical longhouse."

King crafted a wooden Building Hammer, but when she attempted to snap a foundation beam, a red message flashed across her crosshairs: *Missing requirement: Workbench.*

"Tch. Bureaucracy even in the tenth realm," King muttered. 

She spent ten wood planks to erect a crude Workbench upon the grass, then threw up two temporary thatch roof tiles over it to satisfy the game's strict shelter requirement: *Workbench is exposed / sheltered.* With the crafting radius established, wood snapping sounds echoed through her headset as she laid down four square foundation beams, snapping 2x2 wooden floorboards into place with architectural precision. She raised sturdy pine walls, framing a wide doorway facing the water.

"Urara, place the wooden storage chests along the east wall," King instructed. "Bakushin, fell small fir saplings for additional timber. Gold Ship... do not touch any tree on high ground."

"Got it, Chief! I'm on interior decorating duty!" Gold Ship volunteered cheerfully.

King stepped outside to align the front steps. When she rotated her camera back toward the interior, she froze.

Gold Ship’s avatar was wildly clicking her hammer in the middle of the room. A shrill, buzzing error sound chimed repeatedly, accompanied by a bright red warning: *Must be placed on terrain.*

"Hey! What kind of broken rip-off game is this?!" Gold Ship yelled, kicking King's table leg. "It won't let me build the fireplace! The stupid ghost campfire stays red!"

"Because you are trying to ignite an open bonfire on dry wooden floorboards, you absolute delinquent!" King shouted. She switched to her hammer, right-clicked, and dismantled a single 2x2 floorboard in the center of the hall. The wood shattered into salvageable planks, exposing a square of dark, bare riverbank loam. "There. Build it upon the bare soil."

"Aha! Compromise!" Gold Ship slapped the stone campfire squarely into the dirt depression. Above it, Gold Ship immediately began slapping down 26-degree thatch roof tiles, sealing the ceiling completely from wall to wall like a wooden coffin.

"Gold Ship, what are you doing to the roof?" King asked, a knot of dread forming in her stomach.

"Keeping the rain out, obviously! What kind of amateur leaves a hole in their ceiling? It’s called insulation, King! Five-star luxury suite!"

"Wait. If you seal the roof completely, where does the smoke—"

*Puff.*

A thick, dark gray plume billowed from the lit campfire. Blocked by the tight thatch tiles, the smoke hit the ceiling, spread outward in a dense, rolling blanket, and began descending. Within ten seconds, the entire cabin filled with an impenetrable black fog.

Inside the game, a loud, wet choking sound erupted.

A red icon appeared in the top right corner of King’s screen: *Smoke Inhalation.*

Her health bar turned crimson, ticking down with alarming speed. *[-2]... [-2]... [-2]...*

"Cough! Hack! What is this?!" Bakushin cried out, her avatar stumbling blindly through the black room, bumping into the wooden walls. "The air! The air has turned into solid poison! Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O cannot breathe!"

"Gyah! My eyes! My digital eyes!" Gold Ship shouted, jumping her avatar up and down on the burning campfire, which set her pants on fire and triggered another damage alert. "The stove is fighting back! It's an inside job!"

"Waaah! King-chan, everything is gray! I can't find the door!" Urara whimpered, her health bar already dropping past half.

"Everyone out! Out of the house immediately!" King shrieked, hammering her directional keys. Her avatar bumped against a misplaced storage chest, choked on another mouthful of thick soot, and tumbled through the doorframe into the pouring rain with barely seven health points remaining.

Bakushin and Urara stumbled out behind her, their avatars collapsing onto the muddy riverbank, gasping as the fresh rain washed the debuff away.

Gold Ship’s avatar emerged last, trailing black soot and small licking flames from her trousers before rolling into the river with a loud hiss.

King Halo sat at her tea table, her knuckles white around her mouse. She looked at her screen. Through the front door of their newly built, prideful wooden home, thick black clouds of carbon monoxide were pouring out like the exhaust of an industrial incinerator.

She closed her eyes, took a three-second breath, and opened them again.

"Right," King said, her voice dropping into a terrifyingly calm register.

She selected her stone axe, walked her avatar back up the wooden steps, jumped onto the side wall, and smashed three thatch roof tiles above the hearth with violent, measured swings.

The thatch shattered into loose straw. The trapped smoke caught the draft, swirling upward through the newly created opening and dispersing harmlessly into the stormy gray sky. The air inside the cabin cleared instantly, revealing the warm, golden glow of the hearth.

King added two angled wooden beams to create a raised, louvered chimney gable—functional, ventilated, and structurally sound. 

A golden notification flashed across their screens:

`Rested (10m) - Comfort: 3`

Their health bars began ticking upward, recovering steadily in the warmth of the sheltered hearth.

In the quiet dorm room, the tension finally drained away, replaced by the rhythmic drumming of rain against the window and the steady hum of four cooled laptops.

King Halo reached out, picked up a monogrammed linen napkin, and gently wiped away a stray splash of barley tea that had landed beside her mousepad. She set her porcelain cup back onto its saucer with a soft, decisive chime.

Then she opened her hammer menu, placed four wooden beds along the rear wall, and pinned a neat wooden marker labeled *King's Outpost* onto the shared parchment map.

"The shelter is secure," King said evenly, folding the napkin with immaculate care. "Drink your tea. Tomorrow, we secure our provisions."
