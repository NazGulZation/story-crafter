# Chapter 16: The Civil Engineering of the Skull Altar

Ten o'clock arrived with the slow, resonant toll of the Tracen clock tower echoing through the rain. 

Inside Room 304, King Halo switched off the overhead fluorescent lights, leaving only the warm amber glow of her brass desk lamp and the soft luminescence of four laptop screens. The room felt snug against the autumn chill, smelling of peppermint tea, warm laptop exhausts, and clean linen.

On the desk, King’s whiteboard sat beside her keyboard. Under the heading *Operation: Toxic Sovereign*, five discrete phases were listed in green marker:

1. *Bone Extraction & Verification (10/10)*
2. *Topographical Terraforming (Hoe Operations)*
3. *Sub-Base & Evacuation Portal (`BONEMASS_01`)*
4. *Biochemical Neutralization (Poison Resistance Cadence)*
5. *Execution via All-Mace Doctrine*

"We have two hours before the dorm warden conducts midnight rounds," King said, resting her chin on her steepled fingers. "Any sloppy mistakes will result in our immediate disqualification. Is everyone properly fed?"

"Sausages, turnip stew, and deer stew consumed!" Bakushin announced, flexing her fingers over her mechanical keyboard. "Stamina bar resting at one hundred and thirty-eight points! Health pool steady at one hundred and forty-three!"

"I ate my deer stew and turnip soup too!" Urara chimed, pointing proudly at her screen. "My tummy is full and my health bar has three nice colored slices!"

"I’m ready to shatter reality," Gold Ship declared, twirling her mouse cord with one finger. "The Iron Sledge is itching for a skull-cracking good time."

"Then we deploy," King said.

They stepped through the central portal from King’s Outpost, materializing inside the elevated treehouse fortress in the heart of the Swamp. Outside the high wooden shutters, the marshland was drowned in midnight darkness. Green will-o'-the-wisps floated over black puddles, while distant draugr moans mingled with the steady drum of rain.

King led the party down the spiral ramp. In her pack rested the ten Withered Bones recovered from the Drowned Crypts, wrapped in linen like sacred relics. In Bakushin’s and Gold Ship’s inventories lay three hundred stones each, salvaged from their quarrying run. Both girls had Megingjord belts equipped around their avatars' waists, expanding their carrying capacity to four hundred and fifty pounds so the dense weight of the masonry would not trigger encumbrance.

Following the crimson beacon on King’s minimap, they marched deeper into the interior. They navigated along natural ridge lines, avoiding deep pools where leeches lurked in the black water.

Five minutes of cautious trekking brought them to a clearing that made all four girls pause.

Rising from the center of a wide, fetid pool stood the summoning altar. It was a gargantuan skull of petrified stone, thirty feet tall, carved in the likeness of an ancient titan. Pale green fires roared from its cavernous eye sockets and gaping maw, illuminating the surrounding marsh in an unearthly, emerald glare. Beneath the stone jaw sat an enormous iron cauldron, bubbling with boiling, iridescent sludge that gave off thick plumes of toxic steam.

Surrounding the skull was a disastrous landscape: jagged waterlogged logs, deep murky pits where the water reached chest-high, and treacherous puddles where four large leeches circled hungrily.

"Look at that horrifying abomination..." Urara whimpered, her avatar peeking from behind King's shoulder. "The big face is on fire!"

"A dramatic presentation," King remarked coolly. "Ignore the stone prop. Our primary hazard lies in the terrain."

Bakushin leaned closer to her screen. "The terrain, Class Representative?"

"Observe the water depth," King pointed with her cursor. "Those black pools are deep enough to trigger swimming animations. In Valheim, the moment your character begins swimming, your weapon is sheathed, your shield is disabled, and your stamina depletes at a catastrophic rate. If Bonemass attacks while we are treading water, we cannot dodge, parry, or counter-strike. We would drown or be crushed before we could reach dry shore."

"Plus leeches biting our boots!" Gold Ship added helpfully. "Leech bites mean poison! Poison means death! Death means running around naked in the dark looking for our gravestones!"

"Precisely," King said. "We dictate the battlefield. What is the foundational rule of Valheim civil engineering, Bakushin?"

"A covered workbench!" Bakushin recited instantly, puffing out her chest. "Without a sheltered workbench, the hammer and hoe remain inactive!"

"Correct," King nodded approvingly. "We establish our perimeter anchor first."

King jogged her avatar toward the edge of the clearing, finding a sturdy ancient tree ten meters from the altar pool. She drove four wooden stakes into the muck, placed a workbench on a dry patch of moss, and built a slanted thatch roof over it to satisfy the shelter requirement:

`Workbench (Level 1) active.`

The white building circle expanded outward, enveloping the entire circumference of the skull altar.

"Now, equip your Hoes," King ordered. "Gold Ship and Bakushin, select 'Raise Earth'. Use our stone reserves to fill in every submerged depression within a thirty-meter radius of the cauldron. Urara, follow directly behind them with 'Level Ground'. Flatten the raised dirt into a dry, elevated plateau."

The construction began.

*THWACK. THWACK. THWACK.*

Using the Hoe’s terraforming power, Bakushin aimed at the black pool. Columns of solid soil erupted from the seabed, swallowing the stagnant water in bursts of packed peat:

`Stone (x4) consumed.`  
`Stone (x4) consumed.`  
`Stone (x4) consumed.`

Leeches were stranded high and dry on the newly formed mounds, squirming helplessly on the soil where Gold Ship crushed them with swift overhead swings of her iron mace. 

Behind them, Urara swung her wooden hoe with rhythmic enthusiasm:

*SWISH. TAP. SWISH. TAP.*

`Level Ground` smoothed the jagged mounds of raised earth into a uniform, level plane. The Hoe adjusted the grade to the exact altitude of Urara's boots, knitting the loose dirt into an unbroken terrace three feet above the natural water line. They cleared away rotting roots, buried the muddy channels, and compressed the mire into packed gravel.

Within twenty minutes, three hundred stones had been transformed into an expansive earthen amphitheater. Surrounding the boiling skull altar now sat a massive, level circular arena, fifty meters wide, completely free of standing water, free of leeches, and perfectly suited for rapid footwork, clean sprint pivots, and evasive rolls.

"Behold," King said, surveying the dry dirt plaza. "Civilization brought to the wilderness."

"It's like an all-weather dirt track!" Bakushin cheered, sprinting complete laps around the green skull altar with flawless form. "Zero puddles! Superior footing! My straight-line sprints will suffer no speed reduction!"

"Now for our fallback position," King said.

She jogged her avatar to an enormous ancient willow bordering the western edge of the arena. Like all primordial swamp trees, its thick, twisted trunk was completely indestructible, impervious to monster blows, tree-felling axes, and boss damage.

King equipped her hammer. Using core wood beams and wooden floorboards, she constructed an elevated wooden staging platform nestled safely eight feet up within the tree's massive roots. In Valheim's building engine, contact with an ancient tree counted as a blue foundation piece, providing maximum structural integrity to every plank attached to it.

She installed a secondary covered workbench, built a defensive half-wall railing, and placed an unlinked portal on the planks.

*FZZZZT.*

The oval portal ignited with blue arcane fire. King typed the destination tag into the console:

`BONEMASS_01`

A direct, instantaneous supply line linked their boss arena straight back to the main hearth of King’s Outpost. If anyone fell in combat, they could respawn at the main base, eat food from the kitchen chests, grab spare weapons, and step right back onto the staging platform within fifteen seconds.

"Sub-base established," King declared. "Gather around the supply chest."

She opened the wooden crate on the staging platform, withdrawing four glass flasks glowing with amber liquid:

`Poison Resistance Mead (Duration: 10m - Poison Damage -75%)`

"Listen carefully," King commanded, her voice dropping into the solemn register of a racing tactician before a Grade I stakes race. "Bonemass’s primary offensive weapon is an expansive cloud of concentrated toxic vomit. Without pharmacological protection, that cloud will strip away your health pool in four seconds flat. Drink your mead the instant before I offer the bones. In Valheim, poison resistance must be active before poison application to mitigate damage properly. When the buff timer hits two minutes, drink your second flask. Under no circumstances may your resistance buff lapse."

Urara took her flask, placing it into quick-slot three. "I'll keep the buff up, King-chan! I promise!"

"Here are your secondary reserves," King continued, distributing red potions. "Five flasks of Medium Healing Mead per person. If your health drops below fifty percent, disengage to the outer perimeter, drink a healing flask, and allow your natural regeneration to cycle. Bakushin, you provide flanking pressure. Gold Ship, your sole objective is crowd control with the Iron Sledge whenever minions spawn. Urara and I will maintain primary blunt DPS on the boss's flank."

Gold Ship grinned, resting the colossal head of her Iron Sledge against her shoulder. "I'm gonna turn those swamp zombies into compost. Let's make this slimeball pay for my dry-cleaning bills!"

King checked the clock on her taskbar. Ten-forty PM.

She closed the inventory menu. She stood up from her chair in Room 304, taking a long, deep breath to steady her pulse. She walked to the window, verifying that the corridor outside remained dark and silent, then returned to her desk.

She placed her hands upon the keyboard, centering her mouse on the mousepad.

"Take your positions," King Halo said, her voice clear, calm, and utterly resolute. "The seminar reaches its final examination."
