# Chapter 9: The Wrath of the Ancient Roots

The hands of the wall clock in Room 304 crept past 10:30 PM.

The academy dormitory had long since descended into total curfew stillness. In the darkened room, the glow of four laptop monitors cast long, dancing silhouettes against the drawn velvet curtains. The air smelled of burnt wick from an extinguished lavender candle, the dregs of cold hojicha tea, and the sweet, lingering scent of strawberry gummy drops.

King Halo adjusted her headset, her voice dropping into a razor-sharp whisper.

"Confirm inventory status," King ordered, reviewing the digital manifest on her second display. "Food timers at maximum. Deer Stew, Minced Meat Sauce, and Urara's fresh Carrot Soup active. Confirm active durations."

"Deer Stew at twenty-two minutes! Minced Meat Sauce at twenty! Carrot Soup fully active!" Bakushin clicked, her fingers steady on her keyboard. "Total health pool registers at one hundred and twenty-eight units! Stamina pool exceeds one hundred and forty! Eikthyr stamina blessing primed and ready for immediate deployment! Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O is at peak combat readiness!"

"One hundred and twenty Flaming Arrows in reserve, King-chan!" Urara whispered back with solemn determination, clutching her pastel mouse. "And my Finewood Bow is repaired to quality level two!"

"Got my building hammer, got forty wood for emergency carpentry, and got my blue troll hood pulled down tight," Gold Ship chuckled quietly, rolling her shoulders. Her avatar was doing jumping jacks in the sand beside their beachhead campfire. "Let's turn this thirty-foot overgrown salad into matchsticks."

"And once he falls," Bakushin noted with academic enthusiasm, "we shall claim the Elder's Forsaken Power at the Sacrificial Stones! A sixty percent acceleration to woodchopping efficiency! Our industrial forestry operations will quadruple in velocity!"

"Indeed," King said. "A vital economic asset for our future naval and architectural expansions. Check your bowstrings. Ensure everyone holds at least forty wood arrows as backup in case your fire arrows exhaust their reserve. Maintain five-meter spacing. Keep your weapons drawn."

From their beachhead palisade, the four avatars advanced inland. 

The terrain of the southern continent climbed steeply into a desolate, mist-shrouded mountain plateau. The black fir trees here were ancient and gnarled, their weeping boughs hung with gray lichen that brushed against their helmets like cold spiderwebs. The wind moaned through the ravines, carrying the damp chill of deep pine fog. Along the path, two Greydwarves hissed from the shadows, but Bakushin cut them down in mid-stride with clean sweeps of her bronze axe, never breaking formation. Beside a fallen log, Urara spotted a cluster of red mushrooms, tucking them into her satchel for tomorrow's stew pots.

Perched atop a natural stone promontory overlooking the shadowed wilderness stood the arena.

Four colossal, indestructible stone pillars, each thirty feet high and thick as castle bastions, surrounded an elevated square stone dais. In the center of the dais rested a massive bronze sacrificial bowl, filled with glowing orange embers and carved with the writhing likeness of ancient roots. Beside the bowl stood an ancient weathered runestone, carved with the ominous Nordic inscription: *Burn their young.*

King Halo brought her avatar to the center of the dais, her polished Bronze Plate armor gleaming beneath the dark clouds.

"Review the tactical doctrine," King commanded in a low, measured voice. "The compendium is explicit. The Elder relies on three attack modes. First: a long-range vine needle barrage. It acts as a continuous machine-gun stream. You cannot out-parry fifty consecutive needles; you must break line of sight behind the four indestructible granite pillars. Second: Grasping Roots. He will summon thrashing root tentacles from the earth beneath your boots. When the ground cracks, move immediately. Third: melee stomp and sweeping branch cleave if you linger in his radius."

"Understood!" Bakushin nodded, her eyes glued to her crosshairs.

"Urara, you and Bakushin hold the eastern and western pillars," King continued. "Maintain continuous fire arrows. The Elder is composed of living heartwood and petrified bark; he possesses an extreme vulnerability to fire. Keep the burn debuff ticking at all times. Gold Ship... execute whatever madness you have planned, but do not get squashed into the flagstones."

"Leave it to the master chef, King! Extra crispy is my specialty!" Gold Ship grinned.

King opened her inventory. She dragged the three glowing, amber-pulsing Ancient Seeds from her satchel and dropped them into the flames of the bronze bowl.

"Three... two... one," King whispered.

The seeds hit the embers.

Instantly, the fire in the bronze bowl surged into a violent, roaring column of cerulean blue and emerald flame that reached sixty feet into the stormy sky.

The earth trembled. 

A deep, groaning sound—like a continent of ancient timber snapping under tectonic pressure—shook their headsets. The gentle wind died instantly, replaced by a suffocating, ozone-heavy hush.

Then the ambient audio exploded into a thunderous storm of primal war drums and screaming, distorted metal guitars.

From the dark soil beside the dais, the ground bulged upward. Massive roots the thickness of oak trunks tore through the solid granite. Rising into the night sky, towering thirty feet tall above the stone pillars, materialized the ancient lord of the forest:

`The Elder`

The beast was a living titan of petrified ironwood, blackened bark, and ancient moss. Its crown burned with hollow, incandescent orange eyes. Two massive wooden arms, tipped with razor-sharp root claws, reached toward the storm clouds, and a skirt of twisting, living briers dragged along the granite behind its colossal wooden legs.

"Gyahaha! It's a walking broccoli!" Gold Ship yelled, her whisper cracking in sheer awe. "A thirty-foot steamed vegetable with an attitude!"

"Take cover behind the pillars!" King shrieked.

The Elder raised its left arm toward the sky. Its wooden torso expanded like a bellowing furnace. 

From its outstretched palm, a devastating, machine-gun barrage of glowing green vine needles erupted, screaming across the arena at terminal velocity.

*THT-THT-THT-THT-THT-THT!*

"Scramble!" King hammered her keys.

The four avatars dove behind the nearest thirty-foot granite pillar just as the volley struck. The indestructible stone shuddered under the concussive assault, vine needles shattering against the granite in showers of green splinters and crushed bark. 

Any player caught in that stream would have been perforated into kindling in three seconds.

"Urara, Bakushin, suppressive fire from the flanks!" King commanded.

Urara stepped around the pillar's curved edge, drew her Finewood Bow to full tension, and released.

*Thwack-hiss!*

A Flaming Arrow streaked across the dark air, sinking deep into the Elder's mossy shoulder.

Bright, roaring orange flames erupted across the petrified wood. A massive red damage number popped up, followed by rapid, ticking burn ticks: *[-28 Fire Damage]... [-14]... [-14]...*

Because the ancient god was composed entirely of dry timber and living root, the fire caught like dry tinder. The Elder let out a deep, echoing wooden howl that vibrated the windowpanes of Room 304.

"It burns! It burns like paper!" Urara squealed in hushed delight.

Bakushin leaned around the southern pillar, firing three fire arrows in rapid succession into the beast's chest: *Thump! Thump! Thump!* Fire consumed the Elder's torso, painting the dark arena in brilliant shades of amber and gold.

Enraged, The Elder raised its massive root fist and slammed it into the stone floor of the arena.

*BOOOOM!*

From the cracks between the granite flagstones, dozens of twisting, thorned Root Tentacles sprouted from the earth, whipping the air in violent, threshing circles.

"Grasping roots!" King warned, rolling backward as a wooden tentacle whipped her shoulder for twenty damage. "Rotate clockwise! When roots spawn around your pillar, abandon the position and shift to the next bastion!"

"Pillar rotation executed at top speed!" Bakushin shouted. 

Activating the Eikthyr blessing, Bakushin sprinted across the open flagstones, drawing the titan's gaze. With zero movement penalty from her lightweight Troll Leather armor, Bakushin wove through the thrashing root field like a phantom sprinter. Tentacles lashed the stone behind her heels, missing by inches. Urara scampered behind her, taking shelter behind the northern bastion, loosing two more arrows that ignited the Elder's mossy flank in fresh orange flames.

Meanwhile, Gold Ship went completely unhinged.

"Order up! One extra-crispy salad coming right up!" Gold Ship shouted.

Instead of hiding behind a pillar, Gold Ship pulled out her building hammer, sprinted directly beneath the Elder's thirty-foot wooden legs, and clicked right-click three times.

*Crack. Crack. Crack.*

Squarely beneath the titan's wooden groin and feet, Gold Ship dropped three stone Campfires directly onto the dirt.

The open flames caught the underside of the Elder's root skirt. Dense smoke billowed upward, and the constant, ticking burn damage from three simultaneous bonfires began melting the boss's health bar like butter on a hot griddle: *[-15]... [-15]... [-15]... [-15]!*

"Gyahaha! Broccoli barbecue, baby! How's that smoke inhalation feel, tree-boy?!" Gold Ship cackled, diving sideways out from between its knees just as the Elder's massive root foot came down in a furious stomp.

The stomp pulverized two of the campfires, but the damage was already done. The Elder was ablaze from crown to root, its red health bar dwindling down to the final ten percent.

The titan staggered forward, its glowing orange eyes locking onto King Halo, who stood at the edge of the central dais.

The Elder swept its massive, burning wooden arm horizontally in a devastating, wide-arc branch cleave intended to wipe her from the platform.

King Halo stepped into the open.

She planted her boots on the edge of the stone. She raised her circular **Bronze Buckler**, its polished bronze surface reflecting the towering inferno.

She stood firm, timing the sweep with absolute aristocratic mastery.

At the exact millisecond the burning branch struck her shield, King tapped right-click.

*CLAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAANG!*

The parry sound rang out like a bronze cannon shot. A blinding, sun-bright shockwave of golden sparks exploded across the dais.

The **2.5x parry multiplier** caught the thirty-foot tree god’s sweeping momentum. The Elder’s massive wooden torso was thrown backward into the air, its balance shattered, stunned and defenseless for three full seconds.

"This is the pride of King Halo!" King declared, her voice ringing with triumphant fury.

King dropped her guard, surged forward across the scorched stones, drew back her gleaming Bronze Spear, and drove the heavy metal tip straight through the glowing heartwood in the center of the titan's chest.

*SHIIIING-CRACK!*

A blinding column of emerald light erupted from the Elder's core.

The thunderous war drums and screaming guitars ceased, replaced by the soft, falling hiss of dying embers and the distant murmur of the sea wind.

The thirty-foot ancient god stiffened. Its burning orange eyes flickered and died. With a long, groaning crash that echoed across the southern continent, The Elder collapsed, crumbling into a colossal mound of harmless ash, burning firewood, and glowing charcoal.

Resting in the center of the ashes lay two magnificent, shining relics:

`The Elder Trophy`  
`Swamp Key`

In Room 304, the silence held for three breathtaking seconds.

Then Haru Urara buried her face in her fleece blanket, squealing with giddy, muffled joy. "We won! King-chan, we beat the giant tree! We actually beat him!"

"A glorious sprint across the entire tactical field!" Bakushin whispered fiercely, pumping both fists into the air with triumphant, trembling pride. "Zero casualties! Perfect coordination! Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O certifies this victory as first-rate!"

"Gyahaha! The broccoli has been thoroughly roasted!" Gold Ship cheered, leaning back on her cushion and kicking her legs up. "Did you see those bonfires?! Pure culinary warfare!"

King Halo sat back in her chair. Her hands trembled slightly as she released her mouse. Her shoulders, held rigid with tension for six hours of intense leadership, finally eased.

She guided her avatar to the dais, picked up The Elder Trophy, and claimed the heavy, ornate brass artifact resting in the ashes.

`Swamp Key`  
`A heavy iron-and-brass key, cold to the touch. It smells of stagnant water and submerged iron gates.`

A quiet, radiant smile curved King Halo's lips. The tooltips confirmed what her research had promised: this single key would unlock the submerged crypt gates scattered throughout the murky bogs to the south, granting them access to muddy scrap piles and true iron metallurgy.

"A truly first-rate campaign," King said softly, her aristocratic composure wrapping around her like a warm cashmere shawl. "Every tactical instruction was obeyed to the letter. You performed admirably, all of you."

She brought up the system menu, saved their progress, and closed the game client.

One by one, the four monitors clicked dark, returning the room to the gentle, shadowed amber of the night lamp.

King stood up, stretched her arms, and walked around the table. She helped Urara gather the empty snack bowls, placed the whiteboard neatly against the wall, and pulled out two spare futon mattresses from the closet.

Within fifteen minutes, Urara was fast asleep, curled under her pink blanket with a smile on her face. Gold Ship was snoring softly into a sofa cushion, and Bakushin lay on the rug, her arms folded peacefully across her chest.

King Halo picked up her teacup, walked to the French window, and looked out into the silent, star-filled night over Tracen Academy.

She took a slow, refreshing sip of cool water, set the glass on her desk, and slipped beneath her covers.

The tenth realm was vast, and the dark waters of the Swamps awaited them. But tonight, the victory belonged to them.
