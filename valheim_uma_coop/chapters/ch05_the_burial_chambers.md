# Chapter 5: The Whiteboard of Vengeance & The Burial Chambers

Saturday morning sunlight spilled through the sheer lace curtains of Room 304, illuminating a space transformed into an operational command center.

King Halo stood beside a portable magnetic whiteboard balanced on an easel between her bookshelf and wardrobe. On the enamel surface, drawn with black and emerald dry-erase markers, was a detailed topographical grid of the tenth realm: sector boundaries, resource markers, and a large crimson circle enclosing the words `BLUE TROLL (EXTERMINATION PENDING)`.

King tapped the metal frame of the board with the tip of an eraser, her posture flawless in an ivory cable-knit sweater and pleated tartan skirt.

"Order," King announced, her voice ringing with clear executive authority. "Our retreat yesterday evening was a temporary tactical recalculation. A first-rate competitor does not accept humiliation at the hands of an overgrown blue primate with atrocious spinal posture. Today, we conquer the Bronze Age."

"Hear, hear!" Sakura Bakushin O sat at the tea table, her athletic blazer replaced by a crisp track jacket. Her notebook hummed quietly atop an aluminum cooling pad, its display already showing their wooden cabin beside the river. "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O is fully prepared to execute rapid-response logistics! My mouse sensitivity has been increased by twelve percent for optimal turning radius on the track!"

"Look, King-chan! I brought team snacks!" Haru Urara sat curled in her usual corner on a soft rose-pink floor cushion, presenting a round tin of Danish butter shortbread biscuits. "These have little sugar crystals on top! They give you energy for mining!"

"Thank you, Urara. Food remains restricted to the designated placemat," King said, setting down her marker and pouring steaming Earl Grey tea into four Royal Albert cups. The sharp, fragrant bergamot steam cut through the quiet hum of four idling processors. "Now, observe the whiteboard. To forge bronze, we require a Smelter and a Charcoal Kiln. Each facility demands twenty blocks of quarried stone and five Surtling Cores. Without ten cores in total, our metallurgical ambition remains a pipe dream."

"And where do we find these glowing batteries, boss?" Gold Ship leaned over the back of King’s sofa, a strip of dried candied squid dangling from her mouth like an unruly cigar. She had already duct-taped an auxiliary cooling fan to the chassis of her laptop with silver insulation tape, generating a sound like a distant propeller plane.

"Burial Chambers," King replied, clicking a button on her mouse to load her avatar into the server. "Subterranean catacombs carved into the hills of the Black Forest. According to the compendium, they are dark, labyrinthine, and guarded by undead sentries."

The four monitors synchronized.

Outside the longhouse, the morning sun in Valheim shone pale and crisp through the meadow grasses. King Halo’s avatar led the column north, crossing the river boundary into the dense, shadowy wall of the Black Forest.

The cheerful meadow flute faded away. In its place, the music shifted into the familiar, brooding cello drone. Green-gray mist rolled along the mossy ground beneath towering firs.

Ten minutes of cautious scouting through the ravines brought them to a granite bluff. Embedded in the rock face stood a low archway made of weathered black stones. Piles of cracked yellow ribs and broken skulls lay scattered across the entrance, illuminated by the sinister green flicker of two standing tomb torches.

`Burial Chambers`

"There," King whispered, drawing her circular Wood Shield and Flint Spear. "Check your torches. Urara, hold the flame high. Skeletons lurk within."

"Right behind you, King-chan!" Urara ignited her resin torch, casting a warm yellow flare across the dark stone staircase leading underground.

They stepped through the threshold. 

A brief loading pause gave way to the interior of the catacomb. The air inside the tomb smelled of damp subterranean moss, ancient dust, and rotting timber beams. Narrow stone corridors stretched out in three directions, walled in tightly by rough-hewn granite blocks. Along the damp floor, clusters of phosphorescent yellow mushrooms glowed softly in the gloom, casting a warm golden radiance across the stone pavers.

"Ooh! Glowing yellow mushrooms!" Urara gasped, immediately stooping her avatar to harvest them. "They're so pretty! Can we eat them, King-chan?"

"Harvest them, Urara," King instructed quietly. "They provide valuable base sustenance and alchemy reserves. Now quiet. Listen."

*Clack-clack-clack.*

The sound of dry, calcified bone striking stone echoed from the corridor ahead.

Stepping out from a junction stood three tall Skeletons. Two gripped notched iron broadswords, their hollow eye sockets burning with cold red embers. The third held a curved hunting bow, a crude wooden arrow already drawn to its cheek.

"Skeletons!" Bakushin shouted, lunging forward with her flint knife. "Class Representative executing frontal penetration!"

Bakushin slashed her flint blade across the lead skeleton’s ribcage. 

*Clink.*

A tiny gray damage number popped up: *[-4]*. The skeleton barely flinched, raising its broadsword for a heavy cleave.

"Bakushin, step back!" King shouted, reading the combat log. "Skeletons are resistant to piercing attacks—blades and arrows deal half-damage as they slip between their ribs! But they are weak to blunt force! Equip clubs!"

"Did someone say blunt force?!" Gold Ship roared.

Gold Ship’s avatar charged past King, brandishing her thick wooden club with both hands like a baseball slugger stepping into the batter's box.

"Grand slam, bonehead!"

Gold Ship swung. The heavy wooden club caught the lead skeleton square in the jaw.

*CRACK!*

A brilliant yellow critical damage indicator erupted across the screen: *[-48]!*

The skeleton shattered into twenty separate spinning fragments of bleached bone, disintegrating into a pile of Bone Fragments and loose stone with a dry clatter.

"Gyahaha! Home run! Next batter up!" Gold Ship lunged into the second swordsman, swinging the club like a threshing machine. 

*CRACK! CRACK!*

The second skeleton folded in half, its ribs flying across the corridor. 

Bakushin immediately adapted. She unequipped her flint knife, drew her backup wooden club from her belt, and slammed down her Shift key. "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O shall provide supplementary demolition!"

Bakushin sprinted into the archer, dodging its wooden arrow with a clean sidestep, and drove her club into its pelvis, turning the archer into kindling.

"Hold your positions!" King commanded, holding her shield high. "Hear that pulsing hum? There is an active spawner nearby!"

Ahead in the central burial vault, a sinister mound of tangled bones and rotting skulls glowed with an unholy purple haze:

`Evil Bone Pile`

As they watched, two more skeletal warriors assembled out of the bone pile, eyes flaring with crimson hate.

"The nest!" King shouted. "Smash the bone pile or they will spawn endlessly!"

"Demolition charge!" Bakushin yelled. 

Bakushin and Gold Ship charged together, ignoring the swordsmen's swipes. Their wooden clubs hammered into the purple-glowing ossuary in rapid, thunderous rhythm: *THUD! CRACK! SHATTER!* The evil bone pile collapsed into powder, cutting off the unholy reinforcement conduit. King parried the remaining swordsman with her shield boss, shattering its guard, while Urara finished it with a clean club strike.

Silence settled over the chamber, broken only by the soft drip of condensation from the vaulted stone ceiling.

"Orderly advancement," King breathed, adjusting her headset. "Check every burial niche."

Resting atop carved stone pedestals in the corners of the inner vault sat four glowing, fiery cubic crystals, pulsing with intense red-and-orange heat like miniature molten stars:

`Surtling Core`

King approached, hovering her crosshairs over the fire. With four quick clicks, she gathered the glowing cubes into her pouch.

A bright notification flashed across all four screens:

`New Building Piece: Smelter`  
`New Building Piece: Charcoal Kiln`

"Four cores secured," King said, a surge of adrenaline sharpening her voice. "We require six more for our complete industrial facility. Search the eastern wing."

They swept the east wing, smashing two more skeletal sentries and collecting another three cores from a stone crypt altar, alongside forty-five gold coins and two glowing amber gems tucked inside an ancient burial chest.

At the end of the deepest corridor, behind a wooden door bound in rusted bronze straps, they entered a spacious, cavernous burial vault. In the center of the chamber, bathed in an eerie crimson aura, stood an ancient granite runestone carved with a stylized tree whose roots coiled like grasping serpents.

King approached the stone. The prompt appeared:

`Register Location: Vegvisir The Elder`

King pressed the interaction key.

The game audio dropped into a resonant, bass-heavy gong sound. The parchment map table automatically snapped open, zooming out past the boundary of their starting island, scanning across hundreds of miles of open blue ocean, and locking onto a massive, unexplored southern continent.

A bright red horned-tree marker flared onto the parchment, labeled in bold Nordic runes:

`The Elder`

In the quiet dorm room, the four girls stared at King’s screen.

"It's... across the ocean," Bakushin murmured, her fingers curling around her mouse. "An overseas expedition! A long-distance straightaway across the high seas!"

"Gyahaha! A real pirate voyage!" Gold Ship cackled, rubbing her hands together with glee. "I'm calling captain on the vessel! We're gonna need a dreadnought!"

King Halo smiled a slow, predatory smile of absolute aristocratic vindication. She grabbed the final three Surtling Cores resting on the chamber altar, bringing their total haul to exactly ten.

"We have our ten cores," King said, her voice rich with satisfaction. She picked up her Royal Albert cup, took a measured sip of the fragrant Earl Grey, and set it down with a crisp chime. "Return to the surface. It is time to ignite the fires of industry."
