# Chapter 10: The Muddy Slaughter

Sunday evening carried the rich, oily scent of garlic butter, oregano, and baked mozzarella into Room 304.

King Halo rested a slice of thin-crust mushroom pizza upon a fluted paper plate, dabbing the corner of her mouth with an embroidered linen napkin. On her sleek white laptop display, her avatar stood proud in gleaming Bronze Plate Armor, the polished Bronze Buckler slung over her back like an emperor’s mantle.

"Order," King said, her tone brimming with unassailable aristocratic confidence. "Our execution during yesterday's campaign against The Elder was exemplary. We possess thirty cooked venison stews, our bronze wargear is reinforced to quality tier two, and the brass Swamp Key rests securely in my belt pouch. Tonight, we claim our iron sovereignty."

"Hear, hear!" Sakura Bakushin O sat across the table, her cheeks flushed with post-dinner energy as she gnawed on a seasoned breadstick. "With my lightweight Troll Leather tunic, Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O boasts zero percent aerodynamic drag! No swamp marsh shall impede my forward acceleration!"

"Gyahaha! We’re basically demigods now!" Gold Ship leaned back against King’s velvet bolster, tilting a two-liter bottle of orange soda directly over a plastic cup with careless swagger. "I roasted a thirty-foot broccoli god with three campfires! What's a little puddle of mud gonna do? I’ll punch the first zombie we see right into the middle of next week!"

"Look, King-chan!" Urara pointed her pastel mouse toward the south horizon on her screen. "The map says there are water lilies and big trees down there! Maybe we can catch frogs!"

"We are not catching amphibians, Urara, we are securing industrial metallurgy," King replied smoothly, taking a sip of sparkling water. "Board the Karve. Full sail to the south."

The birch longboat surged across the open channel under a brisk northern wind. 

For ten minutes, the voyage felt like a triumphant parade. The blue ocean rolled peacefully beneath their keel, the white wool sail billowing with pride.

Then, with an abruptness that made the hair on King’s arms stand up, the sun vanished.

The bright cerulean sky curdled into an unnatural, bruised charcoal gloom. A heavy, weeping downpour began to fall—not the fresh, rhythmic autumn showers of the Meadows, but a stagnant, oily drizzle that coated the screen in streaks of brown moisture.

A grim, decaying title card faded onto their monitors:

`Swamp`

Instantly, an ominous blue status alert flared in the corner of all four interfaces:

`Wet`  
`Health regen: -25% - Stamina regen: -15%`

The ambient music died, replaced by the wet squelch of bubbling muck, the distant croaking of unseen monstrosities, and the hollow dripping of stagnant rain through dead branches.

Ahead, towering out of black, murky water, stood a nightmare forest. Ancient, gnarled trees, their bark blackened by centuries of rot, leaned at grotesque angles. Sickly green, pulsating sacs of glowing slime clung to the trunks like festering boils. Deep, black water channels snaked between slippery mudbanks littered with rusted iron debris and half-buried ribs.

"Oof, smells like a clogged storm drain," Gold Ship muttered, her voice dropping a fraction of an octave. "Where’s the beach?"

"There is no dry shore," King observed, her brow furrowing as she steered the Karve into a shallow, weed-choked estuary. "The water is stagnant. Gold Ship, scout ahead on that low hummock."

"Leave it to Captain Golshi!"

Gold Ship leaped from the wooden gunwale.

She hit the black, opaque water with a heavy splash. The water reached her avatar's chest.

Before she could take two steps toward the muddy bank, the surface of the stagnant pool rippled. Three sleek, segmented, black-and-crimson shapes darted beneath the water with the speed of striking vipers.

`Leech`  
`Leech (x2)`

*Squelch-crunch.*

The leeches clamped onto Gold Ship's legs with visible snapping jaws.

A violent, neon-green status icon flared in the top corner of her screen:

`Poison (-6.2/sec)`

Her health bar turned sickly lime-green. The hit points began melting away with terrifying, relentless velocity: *[-6]... [-6]... [-6]...*

"Gwah! Something bit my shins!" Gold Ship yelled, hammering her jump key. But swimming in deep water drained her stamina bar to zero instantly. Her avatar could neither swing her club nor climb the slippery mud ledge. "Hey! My health! MY HEALTH IS DRAINING LIKE A BROKEN FAUCET! HELP!"

"Gold Ship, get out of the pool!" King shouted, lunging toward the gunwale.

"I can't jump! The water’s too deep!"

*[-6]... [-6]...*

In seven seconds, Gold Ship’s entire 110-point constitution dissolved into green bile. Her avatar let out a wet, strangled gasp, folded forward into the murky pool, and exploded into a floating wooden tombstone.

A cold server alert crossed their screens:

`Cap_Golshi_X was killed by Poison.`

"G-Golshi-senpai!" Urara shrieked, pressing her hands against her cheeks in horror. "Golshi-senpai melted!"

"An aquatic ambush!" Bakushin roared, her competitive fury overriding all caution. "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O shall secure the high ground and eliminate the hostile fauna!"

Bakushin vaulted off the Karve, her boots clearing the deep water and landing squarely on a raised ridge of dark peat thirty yards inland. She drew her Bronze Axe, scanning the reeds.

*THWIP.*

From the shadowy depths of the weeping firs eighty paces away, an arrow tore through the rain at blinding speed.

*CRACK.*

A jagged flinthead shaft struck Bakushin squarely between the shoulders. A massive red damage number erupted across her interface: *[-78 Pierce Damage!]*.

"Guh!" Bakushin’s avatar staggered forward, three-quarters of her health bar erased in a single heartbeat. "A concealed marksman! At extreme distance!"

Through the murky gray mist, stepped a terrifying silhouette. 

It stood seven feet tall, a desiccated, hollow-eyed ancient viking warrior wrapped in rotting leather and rusted chainmail. Two burning, malevolent crimson embers flared within its blackened eye sockets, and in its bony hands it gripped an ancient hunting bow drawn to full tension:

`Draugr Archer`

Before Bakushin could raise her hands to parry, a heavy, wet *THUD* landed directly behind her boots.

A massive, gelatinous green mass—the size of a wine barrel—bounced out of the reeds with a wet squelch.

`Blob`

The Blob shuddered. Its translucent green membrane expanded like a blacksmith's bellows, and with a sickening hiss, it expelled a ten-foot cloud of dense, emerald toxic spores that engulfed Bakushin entirely.

`Poison (-8.5/sec)`

"The air... has turned to acid!" Bakushin cried out, her avatar stumbling through the mire as her remaining twenty hit points evaporated in two agonizing seconds. Her avatar collapsed into the black mud, leaving a second headstone beside the reeds.

`Bakushin_Pres_1200 was killed by Poison.`

"Bakushin!" King screamed into her headset. 

King grabbed the steering oar, desperate to back the Karve out into open water. "Urara, paddle! We must break contact!"

*THUD. SQUELCH.*

From the deep marsh beside their boat, the water surged violently. 

Towering over the vessel stood a colossal undead champion twice the width of a normal man, its ancient iron plate armor encrusted with barnacles and dead moss. Its eyes burned with furious violet fire, and in its right fist it brandished a massive, jagged iron battleaxe:

`Draugr Elite`

The Draugr Elite raised the iron axe with both hands and brought it crashing down onto the deck of the Karve.

*KABOOOOOM!*

The wooden longboat sheared in half with a deafening splintering roar. Fifty flying birch planks, four deer hides, and eighty bronze nails sprayed across the murky water as the boat’s 500-point structural integrity vanished in two catastrophic swings.

King Halo and Haru Urara were thrown headfirst into the deep, black swamp pool.

The permanent *Wet* debuff dragged at their limbs. Three leeches swarmed King's ankles; a toxic poison cloud from the advancing Blob rolled across the water's surface, blinding them; and the Draugr Elite brought its heavy iron boot down directly upon Urara's head.

*STAB. SQUELCH. CRACK.*

`Urara_RunRun was killed by Leech.`  
`KingHalo_Ojou was killed by Draugr Elite.`

Four wooden tombstones bobbed quietly in the dark, stagnant bog, surrounded by floating birch timber, dead moss, and the triumphant, guttural snarls of ancient undead warriors.

In Room 304, the silence arrived with the weight of an anvil falling onto a mattress.

The pizza sat cold on the study table. The ice in King’s glass had melted into a flat puddle of water.

Four girls stared in paralyzed, wordless disbelief at four grayed-out monitors.

Gold Ship’s jaw hung open, her soda cup frozen halfway to her lips. 

Bakushin sat completely motionless, her hands hovering above her keyboard as if waiting for a referee to call an unforced foul. 

Urara sniffled softly, burying her nose into the collar of her track jacket.

King Halo slowly took off her headset, setting it down upon the mahogany table with mechanical, trembling care. She picked up a stray crust of mushroom pizza, set it back onto the paper plate, and closed her eyes for five full seconds.

When she opened them, her voice was barely above a whisper—hollow, calm, and terrifyingly cold.

"Our armor," King whispered, "did not matter. Our bronze weapons did not matter. Our victory over the tree god did not matter."

She turned to face her three companions, her aristocratic composure stripped down to raw, icy shock.

"We have just suffered," King said, "a total, humiliating, catastrophic party wipe."
