# Chapter 7: The Troll Rematch and the Ancient Seeds

Late afternoon painted the sky beyond the French windows in shades of burnt persimmon and pale violet. Inside Room 304, the cooling fans of four laptops droned in steady harmony with the hum of the small refrigerator in the entryway.

King Halo reached across the tea table, opening a chilled bottle of melon soda with a crisp metallic hiss. She poured the fizzy, bright emerald soda over ice into four tall glasses, passing them out alongside a ceramic bowl filled with salted pretzel sticks.

"Drink," King said, settling back into her chair. "Our nutritional index in-game is secured. We possess fifty bowls of Deer Stew and Minced Meat Sauce. But the Vegvisir confirmed our ultimate objective: The Elder. To summon the ancient arboreal entity, the altar requires an offering of three Ancient Seeds."

"And where are these seeds rooted, Class Rep?" Gold Ship asked, crunching a pretzel stick between her molars with the force of a timber shear.

"Greydwarf Nests," King replied, adjusting her headset. "Pulsating root incubators found in the darkest thickets of the fir groves. Destroying them yields the seeds. Furthermore... scouting reports indicate heavy troll presence along the coastal tin veins."

"The blue giant!" Bakushin sat forward, her eyes narrowing with intense athletic rivalry. "The homerun hitter! Today, Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O shall settle the score!"

"We do not settle scores with reckless brawling," King said, her aristocratic chin tilting upward. "We settle them with first-rate equipment. Our immediate itinerary consists of two objectives: first, harvesting the silvery Tin Ore deposits hugging the rocky shoreline; second, locating the Greydwarf nests deeper in the fir groves."

The four avatars marched north along the riverbank, pulling Gold Ship's newly built wooden Cart behind them to haul spare tools and ammunition.

Where the freshwater stream emptied into the tidal shallows, gleaming silvery nodes of **Tin Ore** lined the wet black pebbles. With rhythmic pickaxe swings, Bakushin and Urara chipped away four shiny veins, loading thirty-two chunks of raw tin into the cart's timber hold.

"Cargo secured!" Bakushin announced, wiping spray from her visor. "Stamina consumption remains optimal on level gravel!"

"Now, pivot inland toward the high ridges," King commanded, strapping her Bronze Buckler tight to her forearm.

Two miles north of their homestead, the ancient firs grew so dense that the sun disappeared entirely. The air smelled of damp pine needles, rotting moss, and pungent tree sap. Ahead, between the roots of three intertwined fir trees, a sinister violet luminescence pulsed through the gloom.

`Greydwarf Nest`

The spawner resembled a monstrous, beating wooden heart woven from thorny brambles and tangled roots, suspended three feet above the ground in a shroud of purple mist.

Around it, a defensive swarm had already gathered: four Greydwarves, two stone-throwing skirmishers, and a towering Greydwarf Brute with mossy shoulders.

"Engage!" King ordered, drawing her gleaming Bronze Buckler and Bronze Spear. "Urara, ignite the perimeter! Bakushin, circle behind!"

*Thwack-hiss!*

Urara released her Finewood Bow. A flaming arrow struck the wooden nest, setting the thorny brambles ablaze with crackling orange fire.

A Greydwarf Shaman lunged forward, coughing a vile cloud of sulfurous yellow poison spores. 

"Evasive sprint!" Bakushin barked.

Buoyed by the Eikthyr blessing, Bakushin sprinted in a wide arc, clearing the toxic cloud without a scratch. She closed the distance to the Shaman, ducked under its gnarled staff, and unleashed a rapid three-hit combo with her Bronze Axe: *Chop! Chop! Hack!* The bronze blade severed the monster's wooden arms, turning it into kindling.

Meanwhile, King Halo stepped directly up to the pulsating purple nest. She raised her bronze spear with both hands, ignored the pelting river stones from the skirmishers against her bronze breastplate, and drove the heavy metal tip straight into the center of the spawner.

*CRACK-SHATTER!*

The nest imploded in a burst of purple sparks and splintered briars.

Tumbling out of the shattered core into the dark moss lay two large, rough-barked seed pods that glowed with a deep, pulsing amber light:

`Ancient Seed (x2)`

"Two secured!" King announced, scooping them into her pouch. 

Before they could search for the third, a terrifyingly familiar sound shook the earth beneath their boots.

*THUD.*

The virtual camera jolted. Showers of pine cones and broken needles rained down from the canopy.

*THUD. THUD.*

Fifty yards away, emerging from the tree line beside the coastal rocks where they had planned to mine tin, stepped an eighteen-foot-tall blue Forest Troll. 

It was the very same breed of nightmare that had swatted Gold Ship across the forest the previous evening: mottled cyan skin, black moss covering its shoulders, and an entire uprooted, thirty-foot pine trunk clutched in its massive fist.

The red boss bar spanned their screens:

`Troll`

"Gyahaha! It's him!" Gold Ship screamed in glee, dropping her cart handles. "The timber tyrant has returned for round two! Batter up, you overgrown smurf!"

"Maintain your positions!" King commanded, her voice cutting through the panic like a honed blade. "Do not retreat! Form the vanguard!"

The Troll let out an earth-shaking, guttural roar. It took three colossal strides across the moss, raised the massive pine trunk with both hands, and brought the entire tree crashing down in a vertical overhead smash aimed directly at King Halo’s head.

In the dorm room, Bakushin gasped. Urara squeezed her eyes shut.

King stood her ground with icy poise. 

She tracked the descending shadow of the pine trunk, reading the exact frame of the swing animation. At the absolute split-second before the bark crushed her avatar, King tapped right-click.

*CLAAAAAAANG!*

The acoustic impact was deafening—a ringing, metallic shockwave that reverberated through their headsets like the chime of a cathedral bell.

The upgraded Bronze Buckler’s monstrous **2.5x parry multiplier** caught the full momentum of the thirty-foot log. A blinding dome of golden parry sparks erupted from the shield's polished bronze boss.

The eighteen-foot giant groaned in shock. The concussive rebound tore the tree trunk backward, stumbling the Troll onto its heels, its guard completely shattered, stunned and defenseless for three full seconds.

"STAGGERED!" King yelled. "ALL UNITS, MAXIMUM ACCELERATION!"

"BAKUSHIN! BAKUSHIN!" 

Bakushin closed the distance in two strides, driving her bronze axe into the Troll’s exposed kneecap with blinding fury. 

Urara fired three consecutive Flaming Arrows into the beast’s chest: *Thump! Thump! Thump!* Bright orange fire engulfed the blue leather hide, ticking down massive bursts of burning damage.

Gold Ship scrambled onto a mossy granite boulder, brandishing her heavy two-handed club above her head.

"Payback for my ribs, blueberry!"

Gold Ship leaped through the air, bringing her club down squarely between the monster’s eyes.

*CRACK!*

The Troll let out a final, shuddering groan. Its massive knees buckled, and the colossal blue giant collapsed forward onto the forest floor with the thunderous impact of a falling tower, dissolving into a mountain of loot:

`Troll Hide (x8)`  
`Coins (x45)`  
`Ancient Seed`

"The seed!" King gasped, grabbing the third amber-glowing pod from the soil. "The third seed was in its hoard!"

"We did it!" Urara cheered, jumping up on her cushion. "King-chan parried the giant tree! That was amazing!"

"Gyahaha! Look at all this blue leather!" Gold Ship was already spamming her mouse to gather the hides. "Dibs on the hoodie! I'm making high fashion!"

They loaded the cart with fifty chunks of coastal tin, the troll hides, and the seeds, hauling the bounty safely back to King's Outpost as the sun dipped beneath the horizon.

At the level-three workbench, the spoils of victory were transformed into specialized wargear.

Gold Ship crafted the oversized **Troll Leather Hood** and **Troll Leather Pants**, giving her avatar the look of a deranged, blue-eared forest goblin. "Fear me, peasants! I am one with the shrubbery!"

Bakushin inspected the armor options at the forge. She hovered over the heavy Bronze Plate Cuirass: *Movement speed: -5%*. Then she looked at the **Troll Leather Tunic**: *Armor: 6, Movement speed: 0%*. Furthermore, equipping the full four-piece troll set granted the *Sneak +15* bonus.

"A sprinter does not burden her stride with five percent mechanical drag!" Bakushin declared firmly. She forged the lightweight blue Troll Leather Tunic and greaves, strapping them over her racing frame. "Aerodynamic perfection! Zero drag on the straightaway!"

King Halo walked to the forge, spending twenty bars of bronze and six deer hides to craft the full, gleaming **Bronze Plate Cuirass** and polished **Bronze Helmet**.

When King stepped out of the forge, her avatar shone like a golden knight commander, resplendent beneath the rising moon.

She walked to the wooden chest marked with an emerald banner, opened the lid, and carefully placed the three glowing Ancient Seeds inside:

`Ancient Seed (x3)`

King Halo took off her headset, picked up her glass of icy melon soda, and took a long, refreshing drink, the sweet carbonation effervescing on her tongue.

She looked across the table at the parchment map on her laptop display, where the red tree marker pulsed far across the southern waters.

"We possess our seeds, our armor, and our wargear," King said, setting her glass onto its cork coaster. "The land has yielded all it can. Tonight... we build the ship."
