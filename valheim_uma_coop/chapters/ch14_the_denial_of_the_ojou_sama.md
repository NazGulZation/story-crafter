# Chapter 14: The Denial of the Ojou-Sama

Rain drummed against the double-paned glass of Room 304, casting grey ripples across the hardwood floor. Outside, the dusk of a damp Tuesday hung over the Tracen Academy courtyards, smelling of wet asphalt and sodden autumn leaves. Inside, the radiator hissed a steady counterpoint to the quiet click of King Halo’s ceramic teapot.

King smoothed the lapels of her clean green academy blazer. On the low cherry-wood table before her, four porcelain mugs stood in a neat row, each filled with steaming roasted hojicha. Next to them rested a plate of toasted milk bread glazed with salted butter and wildflower honey, sliced into precise diagonal halves. 

Beside the tea set lay an open ring binder. In it, ruled grid paper displayed three columns of ink: *Smelting Throughput*, *Fuel Burn Rate*, and *Nail Conversion Efficiency*. King touched the corner of the binder, aligning it perfectly parallel to the mahogany laptop stand.

A sudden, thunderous barrage shook the hall door.

*BAM-BAM-BAM!*

"Open up, Chief Smelter!" Gold Ship’s voice bellowed through the wood paneling, accompanied by the distinct plastic clatter of a tangled charging brick. "The iron age waits for no horse! We’ve got energy gummies and raw ambition!"

King sighed, closed her eyes for two deliberate seconds to collect her composure, and rose with measured dignity. She opened the door.

Gold Ship tumbled across the threshold first, her gym uniform rumpled and a heavy gaming laptop tucked under one arm like a rugby ball. Behind her marched Sakura Bakushin O, her posture rigid at attention, hauling a heavy backpack bulging with three extension cables and an extra keyboard. Haru Urara hopped in behind them, wearing an oversized pink fleece sweater and clutching a wireless mouse decorated with cartoon carrots.

"Pardon the intrusion, King-chan!" Urara chirped, shaking droplets from her hair. "The rain is super cold today!"

"Salutations, Class Representative!" Bakushin announced, snapping a crisp salute that nearly knocked the doorframe. "Track training concluded at seventeen hundred hours! Recovery stretches completed! We have arrived for further operational duties!"

King folded her arms, raising her chin into a sharp, aristocratic angle. Her voice rang with cold, uncompromising authority.

"Hold your tongues, the lot of you," King declared. "Take off your wet track shoes at the genkan immediately. Place your towels in the hamper, not on the carpet. And do not misunderstand why you are here."

Gold Ship kicked off her sneakers into the shoe cubby and squinted at the table. "Uh-huh. Sure. That’s why you made four mugs of hot tea and cut the crusts off the honey toast before we even knocked?"

A faint rose tint touched King’s ears. She pressed a hand to her collarbone and gave a haughty sniff.

"Civility is the duty of a hostess, Gold Ship. But let me make one thing abundantly clear: I am not addicted to this crude survival simulation. The very notion is preposterous. A first-rate lady of House Halo does not waste her precious evenings playing video games."

Gold Ship plugged her charging cable into the wall with a theatrical smirk. "Oh, absolutely. That’s why your laptop is already humming on the title screen, King-chan."

"Silence!" King snapped, her face flushing deeper. "This gathering is an extracurricular Applied Digital Resource Allocation and Kinesthetic Coordination Seminar! I observed your disgraceful operational chaos during our previous voyage. Left to your own devices, you three would drown our supply lines in negligence and leave raw iron ingots scattered across the muddy reeds. I am merely extending my administrative supervision to safeguard institutional standards!"

"Oho!" Gold Ship cackled, dropping onto a floor cushion. "A seminar! Hear that, Bakushin? We’re getting college credits for hauling bog mud!"

"An exceptional initiative!" Bakushin declared, dropping to her knees and unfolding her laptop with mechanical speed. "Logistical discipline directly enhances straight-line sprinting! Class President Bakushin fully endorses this seminar!"

"Yay, seminar!" Urara cheered, already nibbling a warm slice of honey toast. "The toast is delicious, King-chan!"

King cleared her throat, took her seat before her gaming laptop, and settled her wrists onto her ergonomic gel pad. She slipped her headset on, checking the audio balance.

"Drink your tea," King instructed, pointing a manicured finger at the screen. "Then log in. We have an ocean to conquer."

***

The monitor blossomed with the grey dawn of the tenth realm.

King Halo’s avatar stood on the stone-paved courtyard of King’s Outpost, clad in polished troll leather and bearing the newly forged Iron Buckler upon her arm. Beside the central forge, the reinforced chest held the hard-won spoils of their previous midnight trek: ninety-six black iron ingots, cold and gleaming under the morning drizzle.

Gold Ship’s avatar spawned by the hearth, wearing her bronze horned helm and brandishing a wooden club like a baton. "Alright, Boss! What’s the lesson plan for the seminar? Are we going back into the muck to punch more skeletons?"

"No," King said firmly, tapping the open binder beside her keyboard. "Our Karve served its purpose, but its four-slot cargo hold is an insult to modern transport logistics. Transporting our required metallurgical quota four stacks at a time would require dozens of maritime runs through stormy waters. Therefore, step one of our seminar is the construction of a proper vessel: the Longship."

"The big boat!" Urara gasped, her eyes shining on screen as she bounced on her boots. "The giant warship from the forge menu!"

"Indeed," King said. "Bakushin, review the material manifest."

Bakushin leaned in, her voice barking over the headset like a field marshal reading orders: "Understood! Longship blueprint requirements: One hundred Iron Nails! Forty units of Fine Wood! Ten Deer Hides! And forty units of Ancient Bark!"

"We already hauled the bark from the swamp trees," King said, dragging items from the storage vault into her inventory. "Urara, fetch the deer hides from the tanning chest. Gold Ship, harvest the fine wood from our birch timber reserve."

King walked her avatar to the forge anvil. With a series of sharp, metallic strikes, she converted ten iron ingots into a heavy crate of jagged black fasteners:

`Crafted: Iron Nails (x100)`

She gathered the four stacks of ancient bark, the polished birch lumber, and the cured hides into her satchel. Equipping her wooden hammer, she walked down the cobblestone ramp to the ocean dock. The wind howled off the open sea, whipping white foam against the wooden pilings.

King selected the Longship blueprint. A massive, spectral green silhouette appeared over the water, stretching more than twice the length of their old Karve.

"Stand clear of the slipway," King commanded.

She clicked the mouse.

*SPLASH!*

A colossal wooden titan dropped into the surf, sending a towering spray of seawater washing over the dock. 

The Longship rocked upon the grey swells, groaning with immense structural power. It was magnificent: sixty feet of dark ancient timber, reinforced with black iron bands, boasting a high, snarling serpent prow that curved toward the sky. Along the gunwales rested four pairs of rowing benches. In the center deck sat a recessed, reinforced cargo bay holding eighteen separate storage compartments. A towering pine mast reached toward the storm clouds, bearing a broad, heavy square sail dyed in alternating stripes of crimson and gold.

"Whoa..." Urara breathed, her avatar running along the dock to gaze up at the carved serpent head. "It’s so big! It looks like a real warship!"

"Gyahaha!" Gold Ship leaped off the dock, landing squarely on the rowing benches with a thud of virtual boots. "Look at this deck! You could hold a four-horse sprint race on this bad boy! I’m the captain now! Hoist the colors, scallywags!"

"You will touch nothing without authorization, Gold Ship!" King barked, boarding the vessel via the wooden boarding ramp and claiming the stern rudder. "I am at the helm. Bakushin, man the forward mast. Urara, secure the passenger bench. Gold Ship, you are on lookout at the prow."

"Aye aye, Commodore Ojou-sama!" Gold Ship cheered, scrambling onto the serpent's snout and drawing a bronze spear.

King engaged the tiller. The massive canvas sail dropped with a heavy snap, catching the tailwind. The Longship surged forward, cutting through the ocean swells with breathtaking speed. Waves shattered against the curved iron keel, throwing sheets of white salt spray across the deck. Unlike the fragile Karve, which pitched and shuddered in rough water, the Longship rode the storm like an iron knife slicing through velvet.

They sped across the deep blue ocean, the Meadows shrinking behind them into a green ribbon along the horizon.

Ten minutes into the voyage, the grey sea gave way to calmer water. On the starboard side, the dark silhouette of the Black Forest gave way to an unfamiliar shoreline. The murky gloom lifted, replaced by bright golden sunlight breaking through the clouds.

Urara stood up on the deck, pointing her avatar’s arm toward the distant coast. "Look, look! The sun is out over there! And look at that pretty golden grass! There are little yellow berries everywhere!"

Gold Ship tilted her camera, squinted at the screen, and let out a bloodcurdling shriek into her headset.

"HARD TO PORT! HARD TO PORT, KING! TURN THE DAMN WHEEL!"

King’s fingers twitched on the keys. "What are you screaming about—"

"THE YELLOW GRASS!" Gold Ship yelled, slamming her hands against her desk in the real room, rattling her empty tea mug. "IT’S THE GRASS OF DEATH! THE FORBIDDEN SUNSHINE! IT’S THE BUZZ-BUZZ MEADOW!"

Urara gasped, her avatar immediately ducking behind the mast. "The mosquito! The needle bird that poked me!"

A chill shot down King Halo’s spine. Through the salt mist, she saw the biome title flash briefly along the top edge of her display:

`Plains`

A tiny black speck hovered above the golden shoreline, darting erratically over the surf like a speck of animated malice.

"ALL HANDS ASSIST THE RUDDER!" King shouted, slamming her mouse to the left and jamming the rudder into a violent port turn. "BAKUSHIN, PADDLE!"

"EMERGENCY EVASIVE MANEUVER!" Bakushin roared, mashing her keyboard with furious sprint cadence. "FULL SPEED AHEAD! RETREAT! RETREAT AT MAXIMUM VELOCITY!"

The Longship’s massive keel groaned under the strain of the turn. King watched the wind gauge with white-knuckled focus, catching the cross-breeze and throwing the ship into the safety of the deep, empty ocean. They carved a wide, sweeping arc, putting a half-mile of open water between their hull and the golden shore.

The tiny speck circled above the breakers, found no targets within range, and drifted back toward the sunlit dunes.

On the Longship's deck, all four avatars stood motionless, listening to the safe, rhythmic wash of the ocean waves.

In Room 304, the four girls let out a collective, ragged breath.

"That..." King muttered, brushing a stray lock of hair from her damp forehead, "was an acceptable demonstration of situational vigilance. We shall never speak of this course correction again."

"I saw my ancestors waving from the golden grass," Gold Ship whimpered, slumped over her keyboard. "They were offering me cloudberries and holding tombstones."

"Maintain course," King said, her voice recovering its crisp, haughty edge. "The Swamp lies ahead."

***

The golden sunlight faded behind them, swallowed once more by the permanent, sulfurous gloom of the marshlands. Rain poured down through the twisted canopy of ancient gnarled willows as King steered the Longship into the deep-water inlet beside their fortified treehouse outpost.

*SCRAPE.*

The keel settled smoothly into the sandy shallows near the wooden palisade. King dropped the anchor and secured the tiller.

"We have arrived," King declared. "Session objective: complete extraction of iron scrap from Crypts Three and Four. Deploy through the elevated boardwalks. We clear room by room, maintaining standard formation."

Equipped with their newly crafted Iron Pickaxes, the four girls descended into the dark stone portals of the Drowned Crypts. 

Inside the subterranean tombs, the difference was immediate. Where their old bronze pickaxes had chipped away at the muddy scrap piles with tedious clatter, the heavy iron pickaxes struck with devastating force:

*THUD! CRACK! SHATTER!*

`Acquired: Scrap Iron (x4)`  
`Acquired: Scrap Iron (x5)`  
`Acquired: Withered Bone (x1)`

Vein after vein of black bog ore disintegrated under their rhythm. Whenever draugr groans echoed from the sodden hallways, King stepped forward, meeting their rusty axes with the steel boss of her Iron Buckler. 

*CLANG!*

The iron buckler absorbed the blows without giving an inch, staggering the undead horrors backward into the muck. Bakushin charged through the opening, her iron spear thrusting with rapid-fire precision, while Gold Ship crushed creeping blobs with heavy swings of her bronze mace.

Load by load, they carried the dense, muddy ore out into the rain. Because metals could not pass through the portal, they formed an orderly bucket brigade, trudging along the wooden causeway to the beach.

One by one, the eighteen slots of the Longship’s hold filled up:

`Scrap Iron (x30)`  
`Scrap Iron (x30)`  
`Scrap Iron (x30)`  
`Scrap Iron (x30)`  
`Scrap Iron (x30)`  
`Scrap Iron (x30)`  
`Ancient Bark (x50)`  
`Withered Bone (x6)`

By the time the two crypts stood hollowed and picked clean, the Longship's deck sat deep in the brackish water, laden with over four hundred units of raw, unrefined metal.

"Look at that hoard!" Gold Ship whistled, leaning over the wooden gunwale to admire the overflowing crates. "We’re rich! We could buy out the entire academy cafeteria with this much black scrap!"

"It is not wealth until it is refined," King said, though a proud, triumphant smile tugged at the corner of her mouth. "Board the ship. We ride the tide home."

With the wind blowing steadily at their backs, the heavily laden vessel glided across the ocean, cutting a steady wake through the falling dusk. When the wooden watchtowers of King’s Outpost appeared through the coastal mist, the evening lamps were already glowing warm and golden along the palisades.

King brought the ship alongside the main pier with practiced precision, easing the massive hull against the timber bumpers.

*CREAK.*

She set the rudder straight, climbed onto the dock, and tied off the mooring line with a firm knot.

Inside Room 304, the wall clock struck eight o'clock.

King lifted her hands from the keyboard, rolling her shoulders to work out the stiffness from an hour of intense navigation. Beside her, Bakushin was busily marking checkmarks in her notepad, Gold Ship was greedily finishing the last crust of honey toast, and Urara was humming a happy tune while stretching her legs on the carpet.

King picked up her porcelain mug. The hojicha had cooled to a mild, soothing warmth. She took a slow sip, set the cup down with a clean, delicate click, and closed her ring binder.

"An acceptable preliminary haul," King said, smoothing her skirt. "Rest your eyes for five minutes. Then we fire up the kilns."
