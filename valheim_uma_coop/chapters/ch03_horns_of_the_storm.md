# Chapter 3: The Horns of the Storm

The grandfather clock in the dim hallway beyond Room 304 chimed half-past eleven. 

By the strict edicts of the Tracen Academy student handbook, the residential wings had been dark and silent for ninety minutes. Inside King Halo’s quarters, the crystal ceiling chandelier remained dark. The heavy forest-green velvet curtains were drawn tight across the double-glazed French doors, sealing the room against the stormy courtyard below and blocking any telltale spill of light.

The room glowed in shifting, electric shades of azure, amber, and gold. Four faces, washed in harsh display glare, stared into their screens with rapt intensity. The dry, metallic scent of four hard-driven copper heatpipes filled the enclosed air, punctuated by the high-pitched whistle of cooling fans spinning at four thousand revolutions per minute. On the corner of the low mahogany table sat an insulated thermos of roasted barley tea and four empty ceramic saucers.

King Halo kept her voice dropped to a disciplined, commanding whisper through her headset microphone.

"Check your status bars," King instructed, her fingers resting lightly on the W-A-S-D cluster of her silent mechanical keyboard. "Do not rush. Confirm your three nutritional timers. We do not set foot on that headland on empty stomachs."

"Cooked meat at sixteen minutes! Cooked neck tail at fourteen! Honeycomb active!" Bakushin whispered back with the solemn urgency of a field general reviewing supply manifests. Her avatar stood clad in a tailored Leather Tunic and stenciled leather greaves, gripping a freshly strung Crude Bow loaded with flinthead shafts. "My health pool registers at one hundred and twelve units! Stamina reserves are primed for maximum combat output!"

"Got my lucky club, got my aerodynamic rag pants, got a pocket full of roasted pork chops," Gold Ship muttered, her voice muffled as she leaned within six inches of her display. Her avatar was doing rhythmic jumping jacks in place atop a stone boulder outside their longhouse. "Let's crack some Allfather antlers, boys!"

"I have fifty fire arrows, King-chan!" Urara whispered cheerfully from inside her pink fleece cocoon, her fingers steady on her pastel mouse. "Eight wood, eight resin, and two feathers per bundle! And my bow has fresh birch resin on the string so it won't slip!"

"And everyone's implements are at maximum durability?" King asked, guiding her avatar to the covered workbench. She clicked the glowing bronze hammer icon on the crafting interface. With rapid, metallic clicks, her Flint Spear, circular Wood Shield, and stone tools repaired to pristine condition. In Valheim, maintenance cost zero raw materials—a rare operational grace that King appreciated deeply. "Gold Ship, repair that wooden club. You have already chipped the striking face against river boulders."

"Good as new, Boss!" Gold Ship clicked her hammer, swinging the restored club in wide arcs. "Zero scrap cost! The Scandinavian welfare state at work!"

"Good," King said, her heart beating with quiet anticipation. "Our target lies at the southern headland. Move out in a tight column. Conserve your stamina. Do not sprint unless engaged."

The four avatars marched south across the coastal meadows. 

They filed past the quiet roundpole enclosure where Melon and Carrot lay sleeping in the soft clover, small pink acclimatization hearts drifting lazily above their bristled backs. In the distance, along the riverbank shallows, two wild Necks hissed in the dark, their bioluminescent green tails bobbing above the reeds before retreating into the tall grass.

Outside Room 304, the real-world autumn rain beat against the glass in heavy, rhythmic surges. Inside the tenth realm, the weather mirrored the night. Dense, low-hanging purple storm clouds rolled over the ocean, blotting out the stars. White-capped swells crashed violently against jagged sea stacks along the shore, spraying sheets of gray saltwater across the bluffs. The wind groaned through the coastal grass, whipping their avatars' tunics.

Perched atop a wind-blasted promontory overlooking the roaring expanse of the sea stood the altar.

Four weathered megaliths, each twenty feet high, circled a broad stone slab carved with the likeness of a crowned stag. Blue lightning flickered silently across the dark water miles offshore, illuminating the crude runic carving on the central stone: *Hunt his kin.*

King brought her avatar to the edge of the sacrificial dais. She opened her inventory, hovered her cursor over the two severed Deer Trophies they had harvested with such painstaking care, and dragged them onto the offering slot.

"Remember the tactical briefing," King whispered into the microphone. "Eikthyr possesses three distinct attacks. At long range, he casts a lightning cone from his horns. At close quarters, he stomps his hooves to discharge an electrical radius. And when he charges, he sweeps his rack in a horizontal gore. Avoid the lightning, maintain spacing, and use the megaliths for physical cover."

"Understood!" Bakushin nodded sharply.

"Ready your weapons," King said. "Three... two... one."

She pressed the interaction key.

The two deer heads vanished into the stone slab in a burst of crackling violet embers.

The ambient ocean breeze died instantly. 

A heavy, unnatural silence pressed down upon the headland. The chirping of crickets and the rustle of grass cut out completely. Then, with the concussive force of a detonating artillery shell, a jagged bolt of electric-blue lightning ripped through the center of the megaliths. 

Thunder exploded through their headsets with a concussive boom that rattled the ceramic teacups on King’s table. 

The calm meadow music vanished, replaced by the furious, tribal thunder of war drums and screaming, distorted metal guitar riffs that surged with frantic energy. The sky blackened into an apocalyptic swirl of violet clouds.

From the swirling heart of the storm, a colossal silhouette descended.

`Eikthyr`

The mythical stag hit the turf with the weight of an avalanche. It stood three times the height of their avatars, its pelt a swirling storm of gray mist and jagged electricity. Its eyes burned with brilliant cyan fire, and wrapped across its colossal, razor-tipped antlers were heavy iron chains that sparked with arcs of high-voltage lightning.

"Gyahaha! Look at the rack on that beast!" Gold Ship yelled, forgetting her indoor volume for a fraction of a second. "That's enough venison to feed the entire student body for a semester!"

"Silence, keep your voices down!" King hissed, planting her boots. "Form up! Urara, take the rear megalith! Bakushin, flank from the right!"

Eikthyr lowered its massive crown. The air hummed with ozone. Arcs of blue electricity pooled between the tips of its antlers like a ball of lightning.

The beast reared up on its hind hooves and stomped into the earth.

A wide, fan-shaped wave of crackling lightning tore across the meadow grass, scorching the turf black.

"Flanking at maximum speed!" Bakushin shouted. 

She slammed down her Shift key, sprinting in a wide arc along the shoreline to bypass the electricity. But the wet sand dragged at her avatar’s strides, and her green stamina bar plummeted like a stone in deep water. 

Just as she reached the beast's flank, her stamina bar hit zero, flashing dull gray. Her avatar ground to an agonizing, panting halt right in the path of the secondary discharge.

*ZAP!*

The lightning struck her avatar dead center. The screen flashed stark white. 

"Guh! My acceleration... cut short!" Bakushin gasped as her health bar dropped from 112 to 34 in a single blow. The concussive blast threw her backward into the surf, tumbling across wet river pebbles.

"Bakushin, roll! Break line of sight behind the megalith!" King yelled.

Bakushin tapped her directional keys, rolling her smoking avatar behind a colossal standing stone just as a second arc of lightning shattered the surf where she had lain.

Eikthyr turned its burning turquoise eyes away from the rocks, fixing its gaze upon Haru Urara, who stood twenty paces away beside the eastern monolith. The beast snorted a cloud of sparks, lowered its six-foot antler tines, and launched into a thunderous headlong charge.

"Urara, evade!" King shouted.

"W-Waaaah! It's so fast!" Urara scrambled backwards, her avatar's back hitting the cold granite of the standing stone.

King reacted in a heartbeat. 

She hammered her movement keys, cutting across the scorched grass. She planted her avatar squarely between the charging storm god and Urara’s trembling archer.

King Halo raised her circular Wood Shield, gripping the leather arm-strap tight. She braced her avatar's stance, right-clicking at the exact split-second the electrified antler tines swept toward her chest.

"Face me, you overgrown stag!" King declared, her voice ringing with aristocratic fury. "The pride of King Halo does not yield to brute force!"

*CLAAAANG!*

The parry chime echoed like a bronze church bell. A brilliant white concussive flash erupted from her shield boss, the 1.5x parry multiplier catching Eikthyr's momentum head-on. Eikthyr’s massive antler crown recoiled with violent force, throwing the beast backward into the dirt, staggered and off-balance.

"Now!" King shouted into her microphone. "Strike while it is staggered!"

*Thwack-hiss!*

From behind the megalith, Urara released her bowstring. A Flaming Arrow streaked across the dark air, sinking deep into Eikthyr’s mist-shrouded neck. Bright orange flames erupted across the stag’s pelt, ticking down damage with steady, burning hiss: *[-12]... [-12]... [-12]*.

"Eat timber, Bambi!" Gold Ship roared.

Gold Ship’s avatar had scrambled to the top of the western megalith. She leaped off the ten-foot stone precipice like a deranged flying squirrel, raising her heavy two-handed club above her head.

She crashed down onto Eikthyr’s shoulders with a shattering overhead smash.

*CRACK!*

The impact snapped one of the iron chains wrapped around the stag's antlers. Eikthyr let out a high-pitched, roaring bellow that shook the ocean cliffs, stumbling sideways onto three legs.

"Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O has recovered her stride!" 

Bakushin charged out from behind the megalith, her bow drawn to full draw. Three flinthead arrows hissed through the rain in rapid, rhythmic succession: *Thump. Thump. Thump.* Each arrow struck the stag’s exposed shoulder, tearing chunks from its red health bar until barely a sliver remained.

Eikthyr thrash-kicked its hind hooves, sending Gold Ship tumbling into a gorse bush. The beast gathered its fury, rearing high onto its hind legs to bring down a close-quarters electrical ground stomp.

*BOOM!*

Arcs of turquoise lightning erupted outward in a ten-meter ring, churning the sodden soil into burning mud and sending static dancing across King's monitor. 

"Stomp telegraph! Jump!" King yelled.

Bakushin vaulted clear over the expanding shockwave with sprinters' agility, landing cleanly in the wet grass and nocking another arrow. Urara loosed two more flaming arrows from the flank: *Thwip-thwip!* The shafts struck true, refreshing the fire debuff on the beast's flank and whittling away its remaining health points with crackling hiss.

Eikthyr let out a rasping screech of primal fury. The beast gathered the last of its storm energy, rearing back for a catastrophic 360-degree lightning nova that would wipe the promontory clean.

King Halo saw the telegraph. The sparks gathered at the base of the beast's throat, pooling like a collapsing cyan sun.

King dropped her defensive shield stance and surged straight into the teeth of the rising gale.

She slid between the stag's descending forelegs, drew back her Flint Spear with both hands, and drove the razor-sharp stone tip upward with clean, ruthless precision straight into the glowing heart of the storm.

*SHIIIING!*

A blinding flash of white light consumed the screen.

The thunderous heavy metal guitar cut out abruptly, replaced by the soft, rushing murmur of the sea wind and the distant cry of coastal gulls.

Eikthyr stiffened. The turquoise fire in its eyes flickered and died. The massive spectral body dissolved into a swirling vortex of white smoke and sparkling embers, carried away into the night sky by the offshore breeze.

Where the storm god had stood, three glowing, calcified items lay resting in the trampled clover:

`Hard Antler (x3)`  
`Eikthyr Trophy`

For three full seconds, the only sound in Room 304 was the soft, rapid breathing of four girls and the rhythmic tick of laptop heat sinks cooling down.

Then Urara let out a muffled squeal, pulling her pink fleece blanket over her face to suppress her laughter. "We won! King-chan, we did it! We beat the giant deer!"

"Gyahaha!" Gold Ship threw her hands in the air, rolling backward onto the carpet and kicking her legs in silent triumph. "Did you see that leap?! Peak tactical execution! Call me the Valkyrie of the Turf!"

"An exemplary collective sprint!" Bakushin whispered fiercely, pumping both fists at her screen with restrained, trembling energy. "Our tactical coordination crossed the finish line in record time!"

"Shh! Quiet, both of you!" King hissed, though the corners of her mouth twitched upward into an undeniable smile of satisfaction. Her own palms were slick with cool sweat against the mouse.

She guided her avatar forward, picking up the three Hard Antlers and the magnificent, chain-draped Eikthyr Trophy.

They marched back to the Sacrificial Stones where their journey had begun. King mounted the trophy upon the central megalith. 

A deep, resonant hum echoed through the stones. A golden seal flared to life:

`Forgotten Power Activated: Eikthyr`  
`Run and jump stamina drain reduced by 60%`

Bakushin gasped in reverence. "A blessing of pure sprinting efficiency!"

King Halo smiled, reached out, and took a slow sip from her glass of iced barley tea. The cold liquid washed away the dry tightness in her throat.

She opened her crafting inventory at the workbench. A new icon glowed beneath the tool tab:

`Antler Pickaxe`  
`Requirements: 10 Wood, 1 Hard Antler`

King inspected the yield. Exactly three antlers from the Allfather's stag. 

"Three pickaxes," King noted, turning to look across the table at the other three. "A clear division of labor. Bakushin, Gold Ship, Urara—you three shall form our vanguard mining detachment. I shall retain my shield and spear to keep watch over our flanks."

King took off her headset, resting it gently upon her laptop keyboard, and rubbed the tired bridge of her nose.

"We have our tools," King said softly, her eyes reflecting the quiet amber glow of the cabin hearth. "Tomorrow night... we enter the Black Forest."
