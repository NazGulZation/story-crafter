# Chapter 17: The Dissolution of the Toxic Sovereign

At eleven-thirty at night, the Tracen Academy dormitory settled into absolute stillness. The outside downpour had subsided into a cold autumn mist, clinging to the dark trees like white gauze.

Inside Room 304, the only sound was the rhythmic hum of four laptop cooling fans and the steady ticking of the bedside clock. The amber circle of King Halo’s brass desk lamp cast long shadows across the polished floorboards, where four pairs of track socks rested beside empty ceramic tea mugs.

On the screens, King Halo stood at the edge of the leveled dirt arena in the heart of the Swamp. Beside her, Sakura Bakushin O, Gold Ship, and Haru Urara formed a tight phalanx, their dark iron scale mail gleaming with dull gray menace in the emerald glare of the petrified titan skull.

"Check your status bars," King whispered into her headset, keeping her voice low to avoid alerting the floor proctors conducting routine hall checks. "Buffs verified?"

"Turnip stew, sausages, and deer stew active! Rested bonus at fifteen minutes!" Bakushin murmured back with suppressed intensity. "Stamina reserves primed at one hundred and thirty-eight points! Health pool steady at one hundred and forty-three!"

"I've got all three foods in my tummy too!" Urara whispered, her eyes shining in the screen light. "My health bar is huge and red, King-chan! One hundred and forty-three points!"

"Poison Resistance Mead queued in quick-slot three," Gold Ship reported, rolling her shoulders. "Iron Sledge polished and ready for tenderizing. Let's turn this oversized bog booger into garden mulch."

"Drink your resistance mead," King commanded.

Four simultaneous swallows sounded over the voice channel. On each player's status bar, a green flask icon bordered by an iron shield illuminated:

`Poison Resistance: 10m (-75% Poison Damage Taken)`

"Remember," King murmured, centering her mouse on the stone basin. "Poison resistance in Valheim mitigates incoming poison damage by three-quarters, but it must be active before exposure. If your timer drops below two minutes during the fight, drink your backup flask immediately. Do not let the buff expire."

King walked her avatar forward to the sacrificial iron basin resting beneath the titan's stone jaw. She opened her inventory, dragged the bundle of ten bleached Withered Bones into her hotbar, and pressed the interact key:

`Offered: Withered Bone (x10)`

*CRACK-BOOM!*

The iron basin erupted with a concussive roar. A pillar of emerald miasma shot eighty feet into the sky, tearing through the mossy canopy and churning the heavy clouds into an unnatural, swirling chartreuse vortex. Thunder rumbled through the mist, vibrating the bass drivers of their headsets.

From the boiling depths of the cauldron, the mire heaved.

A grotesque mountain of liquified rot surged upward. It rose twenty, thirty, forty feet into the air—a towering, amorphous colossus of bubbling toxic sludge, tangled human ribcages, suspended elk skulls, and dripping black grease. Two hollow eye sockets burned with malevolent yellow fire atop its formless head.

A colossal crimson health bar slammed across the top of their screens:

`[============================= BONEMASS =============================]`

"Target verified!" Bakushin shouted in a fierce, breathless whisper. "The Toxic Sovereign has materialized!"

"Maintain formation!" King ordered, her heart thumping against her ribs. "Do not break ranks! Let him commit to his first attack!"

Bonemass let out a wet, gurgling roar that rattled the speakers. The behemoth drew back a massive arm composed of compressed swamp muck and rotting timber, sweeping it horizontally toward King with the crushing momentum of a battering ram.

King did not roll away. She planted her iron greaves into the dry, raised earth they had terraformed with their hoes, squared her shoulders, and raised her upgraded Iron Buckler directly into the path of the blow.

*CLANG-SHATTER!*

A brilliant burst of golden sparks illuminated the earthen arena.

`Parried! (Block Power: 98)`

The colossal sludge fist bounced off the iron boss with a sharp ringing chime. The upgraded buckler completely soaked the physical impact, leaving King’s health bar untouched at one hundred and forty-three points. 

Yet, unlike a troll or a draugr, the titan did not stagger backward. Bonemass possessed no skeleton to fracture and no balance to disrupt; his formless bulk absorbed the kinetic deflection without pausing, his glowing yellow eyes already tracking King for a follow-up swipe.

"He didn't stagger!" Bakushin gasped. "The boss didn't break stance!"

"He has no posture to break!" King called out sharply. "A tidal wave of liquified peat has zero balance! Bonemass is immune to stagger! We do not rely on parry stun! Execute the All-Mace Doctrine! Strike!"

"BAKUSHIN! BAKUSHIN! BAKUSHIIIN!"

Sakura Bakushin O surged forward like a sprinting thoroughbred, closing the distance in half a second. She drove the spiked head of her Iron Mace into the monster's lower torso with a rapid three-hit attack sequence:

*CRUNCH! THUD! CRACK!*

In Valheim's combat engine, the third strike of a one-handed mace combo delivered a devastating two-times damage multiplier. Combined with Bonemass's innate vulnerability to blunt force, explosive numbers erupted over the monster's oily surface:

`[-54 Weakness!]`  
`[-58 Weakness!]`  
`[-118 Weakness!]`

"The numbers are yellow!" Bakushin cheered, maintaining her sprint cadence. "Direct weakness confirmed!"

Urara followed right on Bakushin's heels, gripping her iron mace with both hands. She timed her run to match the boss's recovery, swinging upward into a cluster of suspended ribs:

*CRACK!*

`[-62 Weakness!]`  
`[-124 Weakness!]`

King recovered from her block, stepped into the pocket, and drove the flanged head of her own mace into the monster's side. The heavy iron flanges crushed through the brittle bone lattice, tearing loose massive slabs of putrid muck:

`[-65 Weakness!]`  
`[-130 Weakness!]`

In six seconds of coordinated blunt bombardment, nearly a quarter of the colossal health bar vanished into thin air.

"Disengage! Stamina recovery!" King commanded, rolling backward onto the dry gravel terrace.

All three girls backed off in synchronized order. Because the arena was completely dry and level, their stamina bars regenerated at full speed without the heavy penalty of swamp water dragging at their limbs.

Bonemass drew in a deep, wet breath. His massive torso swelled like an overinflated leather bladder, the rotting elk ribs stretching taut beneath his translucent green skin. He leaned forward, opened his gaping maw, and vomited a churning torrent of thick emerald bile that blanketed the entire fifty-meter arena.

The toxic fog swallowed all four avatars in a blinding chartreuse haze.

"Hold your ground!" King commanded, keeping her gaze locked on her health bar.

The poison icon flashed violently on their screens. Yet, under the chemical protection of the fermented Poison Resistance Mead, the lethal mire lost its lethality:

`[-2]`  
`[-3]`  
`[-2]`  
`[-2]`

The damage was a trivial trickle of minor ticks. The combined vitality of their Sausages, Turnip Stew, and Deer Stew regenerated their health faster than the diluted toxin could strip it away.

"Hah!" Gold Ship cackled, dancing her avatar through the green fog like an unruly brawler. "Is that all you got, booger mountain? My grandma sneezes with more venom than that! You smell like sour cabbage and bad decisions!"

Bonemass bellowed in frustration. Seeing his toxic cloud fail to dissolve the intruders, the titan reached a dripping hand deep into his hollow armpit. He scooped out a massive, squirming glob of black tar and flung it high into the air.

*SPLAT!*

The foul projectile slammed into the terraformed dirt behind them. From the splattered sludge emerged four leaping green Blobs, two foul Oozers, and three armed Skeletons brandishing rusty iron swords.

"Hostiles in the rear!" Bakushin warned, spinning her avatar around to shield Urara. "Minion wave inbound!"

"Stand aside, track runners!" Gold Ship roared. "Heavy artillery coming through!"

Gold Ship sprinted past them, charging directly into the center of the undead cluster. She hoisted the colossal two-handed Iron Sledge above her horned iron helmet. Crafted from twenty iron ingots, ten pieces of Ymir flesh, and an elite draugr skull, the colossal war hammer carried immense concussive force.

She brought the massive hammer down into the packed soil.

*KABOOOOOM!*

A deafening shockwave ripped through the dirt in an eight-meter radius. The sheer kinetic detonation obliterated all three skeletons into flying clouds of chalk-white bone dust:

`Skeleton destroyed.`  
`Skeleton destroyed.`  
`Skeleton destroyed.`

The Blobs and Oozers were hurled into the air by the massive knockback, their gelatinous bodies ruptured by blunt concussion:

`[-78 Weakness!]`  
`[-84 Weakness!]`

Gold Ship did not give them room to recover. She planted her boots, pivoted on her heel, and brought the sledge crashing down a second time.

*KABOOOOOM!*

`Blob destroyed.`  
`Blob destroyed.`  
`Oozer destroyed.`

The dying Oozer split into two smaller Blobs upon death, but the secondary shockwave caught them mid-spawn, splattering them across the gravel before they could launch a single poison spit:

`Blob destroyed.`  
`Blob destroyed.`

The entire minion wave was eradicated in four seconds flat, leaving the arena floor clean and clear.

"Clear skies on the runway!" Gold Ship crowed, resting the giant hammer across her avatar's shoulder with a smug grin. "Who's next for the tenderizer?"

"Splendid crowd control, Gold Ship!" King praised, her eyes alight with tactical satisfaction. "Look at the boss! He is turning back toward the center!"

Bonemass lumbered forward, trailing black grease across the packed gravel. He raised both massive fists high above his formless shoulders, telegraphing a devastating two-handed ground slam.

"Stamina check!" King ordered. "Urara, what is your stamina?"

"Full green bar, King-chan! One hundred and thirty-five points!"

"Bakushin?"

"One hundred and thirty-eight points! Ready for high-speed engagement!"

"When the fists come down, roll through the shockwave and strike his flank," King commanded. "Do not get greedy. Three swings, then step back."

The titan brought his colossal sludge fists down with the force of an avalanche.

*CRASH!*

The earth groaned under the impact, but King, Bakushin, and Urara executed synchronized forward rolls, slipping cleanly beneath the arc of the descending arms. They popped up on the monster's blind side, their spiked iron maces swinging in tight, punishing arcs.

*CRUNCH! THUD! CRACK!*

`[-58 Weakness!]`  
`[-64 Weakness!]`  
`[-128 Weakness!]`

*CRUNCH! THUD! CRACK!*

`[-61 Weakness!]`  
`[-66 Weakness!]`  
`[-132 Weakness!]`

Bonemass's health bar plunged beneath thirty percent. The monster flailed in desperation, vomiting another wave of green bile, but the girls simply ignored the mild poison ticks, relying on their mead to absorb the toxin while their maces hammered away at his crumbling core.

"Check your mead timers!" King called out over the din of battle.

"Three minutes remaining on the poison buff!" Bakushin reported.

"Mine says three minutes too!" Urara echoed.

"We have ample time," King said, stepping into the pocket as the boss prepared another wide arm sweep. "Maintain the rhythm. Flank and strike!"

Bonemass made one final, desperate effort to swing his left arm. King timed her parry with textbook precision, planting her Iron Buckler to absorb the concussive force with a crisp metallic chime. The deflection opened a clear path directly into the monster's central torso, where the glowing yellow eye of his core burned among rotting peat.

"All units, finish him!" King declared.

Bakushin struck from the rear flank with a blinding sprint combo. Urara swung her mace with all her strength from the left. Gold Ship charged in from the right, delivering a crushing overhead swing with her Iron Sledge. 

King stepped forward, gripped her Iron Mace with both hands, and drove the flanged iron head directly into the center of the titan's burning core.

*CRACK!*

`[-136 Weakness!]`

Bonemass let out a prolonged, gurgling wail that echoed across the dark waters of the Swamp. 

The titan's internal bone lattice collapsed completely under the relentless blunt bombardment. His towering green bulk lost all coherence, melting into an enormous, frothing wave of peat, dead leaves, and rainwater that sank peacefully into the leveled soil of their arena.

The green fires in the petrified titan skull flickered and died.

Above the marsh canopy, the swirling emerald vortex dispersed into thin air. The heavy fog parted, revealing a calm, starlit sky washed in pale silver moonlight.

In the center of the quiet arena, resting upon the packed dry earth, floated two glowing items:

`Acquired: Bonemass Trophy (x1)`  
`Acquired: Wishbone (x4)`

Urara stared at her screen for two stunned seconds before letting out a joyous squeal that she quickly smothered with both hands over her mouth. "We squished him! King-chan, we squished the giant green mountain!"

"A decisive tactical victory!" Bakushin whispered fiercely, pumping both fists in front of her screen. "The All-Mace Doctrine reigns supreme! Zero deaths recorded across all personnel! Flawless execution!"

"Gyahaha!" Gold Ship cackled, inspecting her newly acquired Wishbone in her inventory window. "Look at this little chicken wishbone! The tooltip says it tingles when you walk near buried silver! We are officially walking metal detectors, girls! Bring on the shiny rocks!"

King Halo sat back against the wooden backrest of her desk chair, exhaling a long, slow breath of pure relief. Her fingers felt warm and steady against the keyboard, the tension that had gripped her shoulders since dinner finally melting away.

"A thoroughly acceptable outcome," King said, allowing a radiant, satisfied smile to grace her face. "Every calculation in my ledger proved accurate. The seminar has concluded with distinction."

They gathered their gear and stepped back through the `BONEMASS_01` portal, returning to the warm, torchlit halls of King’s Outpost in the Meadows. 

From the outpost courtyard, they marched back to the Sacrificial Stones where their journey had first begun. Beneath the quiet night sky, King walked her avatar to the third weathered megalith, mounting the grotesque, dripping Bonemass Trophy upon the ancient iron hook. 

A deep, resonant hum echoed across the megaliths as the stone lit with an unearthly emerald seal:

`New Power Unlocked: Bonemass (Physical Damage -50%)`

"Whenever we face difficult battles in the future," King explained, showing them the tooltip, "activating this power grants fifty percent resistance to all physical damage for five minutes. A proper defensive asset for any disciplined expedition."

The bedside clock in Room 304 chimed quietly, signaling five minutes to midnight.

Around the table, the adrenaline of the battle ebbed into deep, comfortable exhaustion.

Urara yawned softly, rubbed her sleepy eyes with the sleeve of her track jacket, and slid off her chair onto the floor cushion. She pulled her pink fleece blanket up to her chin, curled into a small ball, and within thirty seconds her breathing settled into the gentle rhythm of deep sleep.

Bakushin stood up quietly. She neatly wound her laptop charger cord into a tidy coil, collected her mousepad, and bowed respectfully toward King with a hushed whisper: "Thank you for the superior operational guidance, Class Representative. I shall report for morning track training at six sharp."

She slipped out the door on silent socked feet, heading back to her own room before the night proctors began their midnight sweep.

Gold Ship stretched her arms until her joints popped like firecrackers. She unrolled a spare futon across the tatami rug beneath the window, flopped facedown onto the pillow, and kicked off her socks. "G'night, Boss Lady. Wake me up when it's time to freeze our tails off digging silver in the mountains."

"Sleep, you incorrigible clown," King murmured with fond exasperation.

King turned back to her laptop. She brought up the system menu, clicked *Save and Exit*, and watched the dedicated server safely unload before closing the laptop lid.

She stood up from her desk, took a clean microfiber cloth, and carefully wiped down the keyboard and mouse. She picked up the empty ceramic mugs, rinsed them at the washbasin, and placed her green whiteboard marker back into its tray.

On her whiteboard, beside the five phases of the campaign, King drew a crisp, deliberate line through the final objective:

~~*5. Execution via All-Mace Doctrine*~~

She set down the eraser, switched off the brass desk lamp, and stepped across the room.

In the gentle moonlight filtering through the bedroom window, King Halo slipped beneath her duvet, closed her eyes, and let sleep claim her, completely and indisputably first-rate.
