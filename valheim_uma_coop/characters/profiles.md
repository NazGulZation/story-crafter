# Character Profiles: Valheim Co-Op Session

---

## 1. King Halo (POV / Team Leader & Straight Man)
- **Role in Room**: Reluctant host and tactical coordinator. Hosted the session in her pristine dorm room under the promise of a "dignified digital leadership workshop." Spends her real-world time guarding her velvet upholstery, refilling porcelain teacups, and reading wiki tabs on a second monitor.
- **Hardware**: High-end sleek white gaming laptop, ergonomic wrist rest, silent mechanical keyboard, ceramic coaster set.
- **In-Game Avatar**:
  - *Name*: `KingHalo_Ojou`
  - *Gear*: Wood Shield (circular round shield, 1.5x parry multiplier, painted green and white), Flint Spear, Rag Tunic, Leather Helmet.
  - *Playstyle*: Methodical, disciplined, defensive. Studies attack patterns, parries incoming strikes with precise timing to stagger opponents, and thrusts from behind her guard. Frantically tries to organize chest storage with labeled wooden signs.
- **Key Mannerisms & Voice**:
  - "Listen to me! A first-rate victory requires a first-rate foundation!"
  - Flustered "Ohoho!" laughs when cornered; loud indignant shrieks when Gold Ship fells timber onto her roof.
  - Fiercely protective of Urara; exasperated but secretly fond of her chaotic peers.

---

## 2. Gold Ship (The Agent of Entropy)
- **Role in Room**: Sitting cross-legged on a floor cushion with a bag of sour plum chips balanced on her knee, constantly kicking King's desk leg when laughing.
- **Hardware**: Heavy black laptop with flashing rainbow RGB fans that sound like a jet turbine, covered in peeled peeling stickers.
- **In-Game Avatar**:
  - *Name*: `Cap_Golshi_X`
  - *Gear*: Crude Club, Woodcutter's Flint Axe, Rag Pants (no tunic for "maximum aerodynamic intimidation").
  - *Playstyle*: Tree-chopping maniac, physics abuser, cursed architect. Discovers that falling logs deal crushing damage and deliberately weapons-tests it against boars and teammates. Builds roofs without chimneys to fill houses with smoke.
- **Key Mannerisms & Voice**:
  - "Gyahaha! Look at that birch timber roll! Outta the way, peasants!"
  - Constant nonsensical challenges, blaming game physics on "ancient Viking space conspiracies," encouraging Urara's wild ideas.

---

## 3. Sakura Bakushin O (The Sprint Addict)
- **Role in Room**: Sitting ramrod-straight in an ergonomic chair, eyes glued to the screen six inches away, typing with thunderous force on standard laptop keys.
- **Hardware**: Standard Tracen-issued notebook laptop hooked to a rapid-fire red gaming mouse.
- **In-Game Avatar**:
  - *Name*: `Bakushin_Pres_1200`
  - *Gear*: Flint Knife, Crude Bow, Rag Tunic.
  - *Playstyle*: Permanent Shift-key sprinter. Treats every meadow field like the final 200-meter straight of the Sprinters Stakes. Constantly empties her stamina bar within three seconds of combat or swimming, drowning or getting stunned by necks.
- **Key Mannerisms & Voice**:
  - "Bakushin! Bakushin! A straight sprint forward solves all tactical obstacles!"
  - "Class Representative Sakura Bakushin O will now outrun this wild boar at maximum velocity!"
  - Loud, cheerful declarations of duty followed by panicked gasps when her avatar's stamina bar turns gray and starts flashing.

---

## 4. Haru Urara (The Gentle Gatherer & Morale Center)
- **Role in Room**: Sitting wrapped in a soft pink fleece blanket on the carpet, clutching a wireless mouse with both hands, snacking on strawberry gummy drops.
- **Hardware**: Compact pastel pink laptop propped on a low wooden tray table.
- **In-Game Avatar**:
  - *Name*: `Urara_RunRun`
  - *Gear*: Torch, Stone Axe, Flower Crown (dandelion headband mod or aesthetic), Rag Dress.
  - *Playstyle*: Total pacifist gatherer. Hoards stacks of raspberries, mushrooms, and dandelions. Tries to build pens to tame every aggressive wild boar with flowers. Lights dark paths with her torch and cheers for everyone during fights.
- **Key Mannerisms & Voice**:
  - "Waai! Look King-chan, red berries! And yellow flowers!"
  - "Mr. Boar, please don't be angry! I brought lunch for you!"
  - Pure, infectious optimism that defuses King's rising blood pressure.

---

## 5. Special Week (Guest Spectator / Team Spica Envoy)
- **Role in Room**: Dropped by Room 304 while searching for Gold Ship after evening track practice. Brought freshly steamed Hokkaido sweet potatoes from her family. Stayed as an enthusiastic, wide-eyed spectator during Chapter 15's Stonecutter renovations and Bonemass combat audit.
- **Dynamic**:
  - Shocked to see the chaotic, rebellious Gold Ship willingly doing heavy manual labor, hauling stone carts, and obeying King Halo's orders without protest.
  - Inquires about the game's food UI, prompting King and Bakushin's breakdown of the 3-food-slot system (+55 HP Sausages, +55 Stamina Turnip Stew, base 25 HP).
  - Marvels at King's stone castle architecture and laughs with amazement at Golshi's Iron Sledge shockwaves before returning to Room 201 before curfew.
- **Key Mannerisms & Voice**:
  - "Ehhhh?! Golshi-san is doing chores?! Willingly?!"
  - "My mom sent a huge box of autumn sweet potatoes from Hokkaido!"
  - Wholesome country earnestness, deep love of food, and sincere encouragement for her peers.
