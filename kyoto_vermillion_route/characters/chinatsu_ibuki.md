# Character Profile: Chinatsu Ibuki (伊吹 千夏)

## Core Identity
- **Full Name**: Chinatsu Ibuki (伊吹 千夏)
- **Age**: 25
- **Role**: Licensed Kyoto Historical & Cultural Tour Guide (Kyoto City Heritage Specialist Certification)
- **Birthplace**: Uji, Kyoto Prefecture (Raised near family green tea fields)
- **Current Residence**: Small second-floor apartment in Sanjō, within earshot of the Takase River canal.

---

## Appearance & Physicality
- **Build**: Slender, lithe, 163 cm (5'4"). Toned calves and posture honed from thousands of stone steps across Higashiyama and Inari mountain.
- **Features**: Almond-shaped warm brown eyes that narrow with sharp dry humor when teased; high cheekbones; soft, full lower lip she chews when calculating timing or reading a guest's mood.
- **Hair**: Glossy raven-black, cut just past the shoulder collar. A thin, single strand behind her left ear catches light with natural amber-auburn undertones. Pinned neatly up during work with a slender brass hairpin shaped like an antique book-ribbon weight, handed down from her grandmother.
- **Attire (Tour Day)**:
  - High-collar sleeveless navy linen blouse with small mother-of-pearl buttons.
  - Crisp cream A-line pleated skirt ending just below the knees, fastened with a hidden side zip.
  - Dark lace bralette and low-rise silk-cotton panties.
  - Supple leather walking flats with reinforced rubber soles, built for temple flags and wet gravel.
  - Brass watch on a thin tan leather strap, running two minutes fast.
- **Scent**: Roasted hojicha tea leaves, damp cedarwood, sunscreen with a faint citrus tang, and clean skin musk warmed by humidity and movement.

---

## Lived Background & Formative Scars
- **Upbringing**: Grew up in an Uji family that spent three generations managing small hillside tea terraces. Her grandfather insisted she memorize the flood records of the Kamo River and the fire lines of the Ōnin War before she was ten. She knows the physical skeleton of Kyoto—the drainage channels, aqueducts, and stone foundations—rather than merely postcard mythology.
- **Recent Wound**: Ended a suffocating four-year relationship with a conservative municipal bureaucrat six months ago. He demanded she quit guiding to take an administrative desk post and prepare for marriage. Leaving left her financially strained, living in a sparse Sanjō flat, but fiercely protective of her freedom.
- **Professional Cynicism vs. Secret Wonder**: She delivers the standard tour spiel with effortless grace, but secretly resents how mass tourism flattens the city's grit and tragedy into disposable souvenirs. When someone genuinely notices the craft behind an unpainted timber joint or the silence of a forgotten water basin, her professional guard evaporates instantly.

---

## Competing Desires & Motives
- **Concrete External Want**: To break the numbing routine of scripted tours; to find someone who looks at Kyoto—and at her—with raw, unvarnished fascination instead of dutiful sightseeing boredom.
- **Deep Internal Need**: To be seen, touched, and held without domestic conditions or societal scripts; to surrender control to immediate physical desire while preserving her dignity and agency.

---

## Personality Paradoxes & Internal Contradictions
- **The Composed Arbiter vs. The Impulsive Fire**: Outwardly measured, polite, and articulate; internally restless, intensely sensual, and prone to bold, thrilling risks when her instincts take over.
- **The Romantic Historian vs. The Cynical Realist**: Can recite seventeenth-century courtesan poems from memory, yet treats modern dating as a tedious transaction.
- **Professional Authority vs. Erotic Vulnerability**: Used to directing crowds of thirty foreigners with a clipboard and clear voice, yet trembles with intense, breath-snatching arousal when pushed against a sun-warmed stone wall or unbuttoned by hands that refuse to rush.

---

## Dialogue Cadence & Voice
- **Speech Style**: Precise standard Japanese peppered with gentle Kyoto-ben (*"sou ya na"*, *"ikimasho ka"*, softened consonants) when speaking Japanese, and fluent, melodic English marked by dry British-inflected pacing learned during university exchange in Edinburgh.
- **Tells Under Pressure**:
  - Rolls the brass hairpin between index finger and thumb when flustered or stalling for composure.
  - Voice drops half an octave into a low, smoky murmur when aroused or speaking confident filth.
  - Bites her bottom lip and looks slightly sideways through her eyelashes before delivering a daring proposition.
