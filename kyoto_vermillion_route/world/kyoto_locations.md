# Kyoto Worldbuilding & Locations: The Vermillion Route

This document outlines the sensory, historical, and architectural anchors for *The Vermillion Route*. Every location grounds character movement in authentic Kyoto geography, material textures, and acoustics.

---

## 1. Fushimi Inari Taisha & The Southern Slopes (Level 1)
- **Setting**: The mountain trails of Inari Mountain, south of Kyoto proper.
- **Atmosphere & Textures**:
  - Over four thousand vermillion gates (*senbon torii*) coated in lead-orange cinnabar paint, their pine trunks cold and smooth to the touch, engraved with the names of Osaka merchants, sake brewers, and transport syndicates who paid for their erection.
  - Damp earthen switchbacks banked by stone drainage runnels; fallen camphor leaves slick underfoot after sudden summer cloudbursts.
  - The heavy, sweet odor of damp soil, rotting bark, cedar resin, and smoldering stick incense from mountain alcoves.
  - Sound: Cicadas buzzing in waves through cedar crowns, interrupted by the hollow *clack-thud* of wooden prayer plaques hanging from eaves and the distant chime of commuter trains at Inari Station.

---

## 2. The Upper Shadow Shrine: Yotsutsuji Overlook & The Moss Hollow (Levels 2B, 3C, Endings 5 & 6)
- **Setting**: A forgotten branching spur thirty paces past the crowded Yotsutsuji tea pavilion, hidden behind ancient cryptomeria trees.
- **Architectural Elements**:
  - A collapsed torii gate made of rough-hewn grey granite, split by tree roots and swallowed by thick emerald velvet moss.
  - A cluster of twin fox statues (*kitsune*) weathered nearly smooth, clutching stone keys and rice sheaves in their jaws, wearing faded red cotton bibs stained brown by rain.
  - A three-tiered dry-stacked stone retaining wall erected during the Genroku era (1690s) by a wealthy Fushimi shipping clerk to shelter his meetings with a Gion geiko.
- **Acoustic & Tactile Tone**:
  - Sheltered from wind; the tourists below sound like distant sea surf.
  - Cold, porous stone against warm skin; damp moss cushioning knees; sweet smell of wet humus and wild mountain fern.

## 3. Arashiyama Bamboo Cathedral & The Ōi Riverbank (Levels 2A, 3B, Endings 3 & 4)
- **Setting**: Sagano district on the western edge of the Kyoto basin.
- **Atmosphere & Textures**:
  - Dense stands of *moso* bamboo rising thirty meters, green culms rubbing together in river breezes with a hollow, wooden groan like ship timbers.
  - The sun filters through leaves as a cool, emerald-dappled haze; dust motes hanging motionless over crushed river gravel.
  - The Ōi River flows sluggish and jade-green below Mount Ogura, smelling of mountain runoff, freshwater weed, and wet limestone.
  - A weathered cedar bench sheltered by a weeping cherry tree, its timber greyed and polished smooth by decades of sitting travelers.

---

## 4. Pontochō Alley & The Kamo Riverbank (Levels 3A, Endings 1 & 2)
- **Setting**: The five-hundred-meter pedestrian lane running between Shijō-dōri and Sanjō-dōri along the western bank of the Kamo River.
- **Atmosphere & Textures**:
  - Narrow enough that two people carrying open umbrellas must tilt their canopies to squeeze past.
  - Dark-stained cedar facades (*machiya* lattice screens), paper lanterns painted with the white plover bird (*chidori*) emblem glowing warm amber against wet asphalt.
  - Aroma of yakitori smoke over binchōtan charcoal, simmering dashi broth, vinegar, spilled draft beer, and cold river dampness.
  - The blind side-alleys (*roji*) are three feet wide, paved with uneven river flagstones, sheltered beneath overhanging eaves where rain gutters drip into ceramic drainage pots.

---

## 5. Ryokan Suimei-an (Levels 3B, Endings 3 & 4)
- **Setting**: A century-old wooden inn tucked behind the canal willow trees north of Sanjō.
- **Interior Details**:
  - Squeaking polished cypress floorboards (*uguisubari*) in the hallways; sliding shoji screens made of mulberry paper that tremble when trains cross the river bridge.
  - Private bathing rooms lined with green Tokushima slate and deep, oval tubs fashioned from fifty-year-old Kiso cedar.
  - The thick, clean steam carries the pungent terpene oil of cedar resin; warm water continually overflows the lip onto grooved wooden drainage slats.
  - Adjoining tatami room: crisp rush-straw mats smelling of dried summer grass, down futons encased in crisp bleached cotton covers, sliding doors opening onto an enclosed pocket garden with a trickling bamboo water-pipe (*shishi-odoshi*).

---

## 6. Chinatsu's Sanjō Apartment (Level 3A, Ending 1)
- **Setting**: Second-floor corner unit in a 1980s concrete low-rise overlooking the willow-draped Takase canal.
- **Interior Texture**:
  - Six-mat main tatami room surrounded by towering, slightly bowing plywood bookshelves packed with Kyoto architectural surveys, Edo-era maps, and municipal water board reports.
  - A small low oak tea table bearing an iron kettle, a hand-whisk, and two mismatched ceramic bowls.
  - Cotton curtains open to let in the cool river breeze and the low rumble of scooters passing down Kiyamachi.
  - Worn tatami yielding gently beneath bare knees; scent of roasted hojicha and lavender linen mist.

---

## 7. Hotel Granbell Kyoto — Room 407 (Level 3D, Ending 8)
- **Setting**: Contemporary boutique hotel in Gion, blending modern concrete minimalism with dark timber and low indirect lighting.
- **Sensory Details**:
  - Deep charcoal carpets muffling every step; floor-to-ceiling double-glazed windows looking over the tiled temple roofs of Kennin-ji.
  - King-size bed dressed in high-thread-count Egyptian percale linen, cool and crisp against skin.
  - Rain slicking the outside glass, distorting the city lights into golden and ruby streaks.
  - Hum of central climate control keeping the air brisk and dry, sharpening the scent of fresh soap, heated skin, and damp hair.
