# Setting & World Bible: The Mockingbird's Ledger

A grounded reference for the geography, economy, social hierarchy, and systemic rot of Bell-Garde and the Grand Realm of Osterlund.

---

## 1. Geography & Urban Topography

### Bell-Garde (The City of the Seven Chimes)
A sprawling, fortified coastal metropolis constructed at the mouth of the grey River Sunder where it empties into the cold northern reaches of the Bitter Sea.

- **The High Citadel (The Gilded Spire)**:
  - Perched atop a sheer chalk cliff overlooking the bay.
  - Built from polished white Caen limestone, topped with slate roofs and copper banners.
  - Houses the Sovereign Council Hall, the Ducal Palace, the Hall of Heroes, and the luxury salons where Duchess Lynette Morrow and Lord Godfrey Sterling reside.
  - Separated from the city below by the Iron Gate and two hundred guarded marble stairs.

- **The High Wharf & Merchant Quarter**:
  - The commercial lung of Osterlund. Paved in granite flags, lined with five-story half-timbered merchant counting-houses, goldsmiths' exchanges, and the sovereign customs house.
  - Dominated by the League of Factors and the Guild of Stevedores.

- **The Canal Quarter & The Sump**:
  - A dense labyrinth of tidal ditches, slaughterhouses, tanneries, cheap brothels, and peat warehouses.
  - The air tastes of sulfur, wet wool, brine, and horse manure.
  - Home to *The Brazen Quill*, the nondescript three-story tavern and bathhouse acquired by Petyr Baelish. Beneath its cellar lies the subterranean network connecting to the old Romanesque drainage vaults.

- **The Outlying Marches (Weirs-End)**:
  - A sodden river hamlet thirty miles up the Sunder.
  - Characterized by peat-cutters' hovels, reed-thatched roofs, and muddy cabbage patches. The birthplace of Alden Croft and Lynette Morrow, where vows of eternal love were once sworn over chipped earthenware bowls.

## 2. Economy & Currency Systems

The economy of Osterlund is built upon agricultural surpluses, coastal fishing, and iron mining, but has been crippled by five years of unchecked chivalric indulgence.

### Currency Denominations:
- **Copper Penny / Farthing**: Daily currency of laborers, dockhands, and soldiers. (1 loaf of coarse rye = 2 copper farthings).
- **Silver Shilling (Groat)**: Medium of commercial trade. (1 silver shilling = 12 copper pennies).
- **Gold Sovereign**: High-denomination coinage minted with the image of the Sun-Gilded Hero. (1 gold sovereign = 20 silver shillings = 240 copper pennies).

### The Sovereign Debt Crisis:
- Under Lord Godfrey Sterling's heroic administration, state spending increased fourfold:
  - Annual martial tournaments costing 15,000 gold sovereigns each.
  - Gilded triumphal statues erected in four municipal squares.
  - Unchecked court pensions bestowed upon Godfrey's sycophants and mistresses.
- **The Financial Vulnerability**: The crown coffers hold less than 4,000 sovereigns in hard bullion, while short-term bearer bonds totaling 280,000 sovereigns circulate among private merchant syndicates. Whoever buys these notes at discount controls the kingdom's credit.

## 3. Political Factions & Power Triads

### The High Stewardship (Civil Administration)
- **Overseer**: Duchess Lynette Morrow.
- **Function**: Manages domestic grain stockpiles, port tariffs, textile monopolies, and palace maintenance.
- **Rot**: Pervasive embezzlement and catastrophic mismanagement. Lynette signs off on exorbitant requisitions to fund private jewelers and vanity projects, hiding shortfalls with high-interest promissory notes.

### The Sovereign Vanguard (Military Arm)
- **Commander**: Grand Marshal Gisela Brandt.
- **Function**: Eight thousand heavy infantry, horsemen, and border rangers garrisoned across the outer perimeter.
- **Rot**: Garrison arrears of four months. Armor contracts have been falsified with brittle slag-iron. Captains are quietly pledging their companies to private credit-brokers to keep their men fed.

### The Sanctum of the Radiant Dawn (Religious & Judicial Arm)
- **High Chancellor & Reliquary Keeper**: Vivienne Claire.
- **Function**: Religious orthodoxy, church tithes, licensing of apothecaries, and the trial of heretics.
- **Rot**: Rampant simony and reliquary counterfeiting. Vivienne Claire smuggles stolen temple silver and forged holy relics through black-market coastal schooners to sustain her private gambling debts and luxurious villas.

## 4. Petyr Baelish's Ladder of Ascendance

- **Vehicle**: *The Grey River Syndicate* (fronted by puppet factors and silent partnerships).
- **Intelligence Base**: *The Brazen Quill* — a bathhouse and tavern where confidential confessions are gathered from drunk captains, indebted clerks, and discarded mistresses.
- **Economic Trap**:
  1. Buying distressed crown bonds at forty cents on the silver shilling.
  2. Cornering the seasonal grain fleets descending the River Sunder.
  3. Underwriting the iron smithies supplying the Sovereign Vanguard.
  4. Securing Vivienne Claire's illicit bills of lading under notarized seal.
- **The Culmination**: The transformation of debt from a private ledger entry into an unassailable weapon of state sovereignty.
