# World Lore: Gourmet Guild After Dark

## Setting
- **Location**: Mountain Thermal Lodge in the ridge woods above Landosol.
- **Atmosphere**: Cedar floorboards, open-air stone hot springs, moonlit courtyard, bamboo groves, sulfur steam.

## Key Catalyst: Moonpetal Sap
- **Origin**: Rare alpine herb gathered near subterranean water vaults in the labyrinth.
- **Properties**: Restorative herbal bloom that acts as a potent natural aphrodisiac when steeping in boiling water.
- **Effects**: Radiates through steam and water, absorbed through skin and pulse points, amplifying physical sensitivity and uninhibited desire.
