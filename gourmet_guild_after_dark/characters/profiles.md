# Gourmet Guild Canonical Character Profiles

> [!NOTE]
> All characters in this fan fiction narrative are depicted as consenting adults (18+).

---

## 1. Yuuki (The Protagonist)
- **Role**: Protagonist / Princess Knight / Knight of Labyrinth (First-Person POV)
- **Race**: Human
- **Appearance**: Dark short hair, observant dark eyes, lean athletic build suited for swordsmanship, lightly bandaged left forearm recovering from the labyrinth guardian encounter. Wears comfortable cotton traveling yukata at the lodge.
- **Canonical Abilities & Resonance**:
  - Wielder of the *Princess Knight* power bestowed by Ames, allowing him to amplify the magical and physical strength of his companions through resonant focus.
  - Brave and selfless in combat, instinctively stepping in front of lethal strikes to shield his comrades.
- **Personality & Dynamics**:
  - Gentle, emotionally grounded, attentive, and quietly courageous under pressure.
  - Naturally reassuring and non-judgmental, accepting each guild member's quirks, appetites, and vulnerabilities unconditionally.
  - Deeply loving toward all three girls, serving as their emotional anchor and trusted partner.

---

## 2. Kokkoro (Natsume Kokoro / コッコロ)
- **Role**: Elven Priestess Guide & Caretaker
- **Race**: Elf (Forest Elf of the sacred ritual order)
- **Appearance**:
  - **Hair**: Short, pale silver-white (light mint-silver sheen) with soft straight bangs and a small floral hair ornament.
  - **Eyes**: Deep luminous rose-pink / crimson.
  - **Physiology**: Long pointed elven ears (highly sensitive), fair porcelain skin, petite and slender frame, modest and delicate bust.
  - **Attire**: Sleeveless white and green ritual priestess tunic with gold embroidery (leaving shoulders and underarm hollows bare for spear mobility), dark green sash/skirt, ceremonial talisman brooch, long blue-crystal ceremonial spear.
- **Speech Style & Honorifics**:
  - Refers to Yuuki respectfully as **"Aruji-sama"** (主様 - Lord/Master); uses his true name **"Yuuki"** only in moments of profound, private emotional intimacy.
  - Often refers to herself in the third person (**"Kokkoro wa..."**) in formal elven cadence.
  - Addresses Pecorine as **"Pecorine-sama"** (reverent toward guild leader and royalty) and Karyl as **"Karyl-chan"** (affectionate peer).
- **Personality & Canon Lore**:
  - Devoted, serene, nurturing, and maternal despite her youthful frame. Manages guild ledgers, medicine foraging, gear maintenance, and campfire cooking.
  - Guided by the mandates of the Great Goddess Ames to guide and protect the chosen Lord.
  - Bashful regarding carnal desires due to her ascetic forest upbringing, but her heart belongs wholly and unconditionally to Yuuki.

## 3. Pecorine (Eustiana von Astraea / ペコリーヌ)
- **Role**: Crown Princess of Landosol / Gourmet Guild Master
- **Race**: Human
- **Appearance**:
  - **Hair**: Long, radiant golden-orange hair cascading in thick, sunny waves down past her waist, adorned with a royal golden tiara.
  - **Eyes**: Sparkling sapphire-blue eyes full of vitality.
  - **Physiology**: Voluptuous, tall, athletic warrior physique; massive, heavy bust; strong shapely thighs; vibrant sun-kissed health.
  - **Attire**: Crimson and white adventuring dress with golden royal trim, white gloves, high leather boots; carries the colossal golden broadsword (Princess Sword) and royal cuirass.
- **Speech Style & Honorifics**:
  - Signature catchphrase: **"Yabai desu ne!"** (ヤバいですね！).
  - Addresses Yuuki as **"Yuuki"** or affectionately **"Princess Knight"**; calls Kokkoro **"Kokkoro-chan"** and Karyl **"Karyl-chan"**.
- **Personality & Canon Lore**:
  - Boundlessly optimistic, boisterous, loving, and physically affectionate (spontaneous hugs, feeding others).
  - Possesses an insatiable, ravenous appetite caused by her *Royal Equipment*, which consumes immense caloric and magical energy to maintain its god-like offensive and defensive strength.
  - True identity is **Crown Princess Eustiana** of Landosol. Exiled and stripped of her royal identity by Kaiser Insight, she wandered starving before founding the Gourmet Guild. Cherishes sharing food as the ultimate celebration of life and family.

## 4. Karyl (Momochi Kiruya / キャル)
- **Role**: Cat-Beastkin Mage / Backline Elemental Spellcaster
- **Race**: Beastkin (Feline)
- **Appearance**:
  - **Hair**: Dark navy/black hair styled in two long, low twin-tails secured by white ribbons, with straight dark bangs.
  - **Eyes**: Striking deep violet/purple eyes.
  - **Physiology**: Triangular black cat ears on the crown of her head (covered in soft fur, highly sensitive), slender black cat tail that moves expressively, small feline fangs, lithe and delicate waist, perky firm bust.
  - **Attire**: Dark gothic adventuring robes with purple/gold accents, fitted leather corset/bodice, pleated skirt, dark stockings and boots; wields the Chaos Grimoire and black catalyst staff.
- **Speech Style & Honorifics**:
  - Quintessential tsundere: sharp-tongued, flustered, frequently exclaims **"Anta baka?!"** (Are you an idiot?!) and **"Mou!"**.
  - Often threatens to blast companions with *Grim Burst* or transform them into toads/frogs when teased.
  - Addresses Yuuki as **"Yuuki"** (or teasingly **"idiot"** / **"sword boy"**, NEVER "Master"). Calls Pecorine **"Pecorine"** and Kokkoro **"Kokkoro"**.
- **Personality & Canon Lore**:
  - Prickly exterior masking deep insecurity, fear of abandonment, and profound loyalty.
  - Involuntary feline mannerisms: ears perk or flatten with emotion, tail coils possessively around Yuuki's waist or legs when seeking comfort, and emits a loud, vibrating chest purr when stroked, held, or overwhelmed with affection, which mortifies her pride.
  - Originally an impoverished, neglected stray coerced into acting as Kaiser Insight's undercover spy. Found true warmth, safety, and a chosen family in the Gourmet Guild.
