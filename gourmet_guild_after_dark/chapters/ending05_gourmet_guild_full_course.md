# Ending 5: Gourmet Guild — Full Course

The thermal waters of the outdoor hot spring pool glistened under the silver light of the full moon, veiled in swirling plumes of aromatic steam. The four of us rested together on the broad, submerged stone bench in the shallows, our nude bodies intertwined in a peaceful, tangled heap of warm limbs, glistening skin, and deep contentment.

We had reached the summit of our shared passion—a synchronized, breathtaking release that had united all four members of the Gourmet Guild in a single, magnificent tempest of desire. The waters had calmed, but the quiet intimacy that remained was palpable, humming through the warm mist like a gentle current of living magic.

Pecorine lay on my right, her head pillowed comfortably against my shoulder, her long golden hair floating across the surface of the pool like spun copper. Kokkoro lay on my left, her slender elven frame curled tightly against my side, her small hand holding mine beneath the warm ripples while her long ears twitched in serene rhythm. And Karyl sat between Pecorine and me, her slender legs draped across my thighs, her glossy black cat tail loosely coiled around Kokkoro's ankle and my hip under the water. From Karyl's chest came a steady, resonant, rumbling purr that she seemed completely powerless to suppress.

"Ahhh... that was... the most incredible night of my entire life!" Pecorine sighed happily, her bright blue eyes gazing up at the sea of stars glittering above the mountain valley. "We conquered the dungeon guardian, feasted on roasted mountain boar, and now... we're all wrapped up together like this! Yabai desu ne!"

Kokkoro giggled softly, the delicate tip of her pointed ear brushing against my throat. "It was indeed an unforgettable evening, Pecorine-sama. Sharing such sacred intimacy with Aruji-sama... and with both of you... has filled my heart with a peace I have never known before."

Karyl stirred between us, her pale cheeks burning a bright crimson in the amber glow of the stone lanterns. Her cat ears swiveled backward in flustered embarrassment, though she made no move whatsoever to untangle her legs or pull her tail away.

"Hmph! Keep your voices down, you two!" she muttered, hiding her mouth behind her hands while her tail tightened its grip on my hip. "If the inn staff hear you shouting about how incredible it was, we'll never be able to show our faces in this lodge again! And don't look at me like that, Yuuki! Just because... because we did something so shameless together tonight doesn't mean you can slack off on morning training tomorrow!"

Pecorine laughed heartily, leaning across Karyl's lap to plant a loud, wet kiss on the catgirl's burning cheek.

"Oh, lighten up, Karyl-chan!" Pecorine teased with boundless affection. "Your tail has been purring against Kokkoro's leg and Yuuki's hip for the last half hour! You're the happiest one here!"

"My tail is NOT purring!" Karyl squeaked in indignant protest, her face turning as red as a ripe tomato. "Tails don't purr! It's... it's an involuntary magical resonance caused by lingering mana in the thermal minerals! I'm a black mage, you know!"

"It is quite alright, Karyl-chan," Kokkoro murmured with gentle tenderness, reaching beneath the water to stroke the velvet base of Karyl's tail. "Your purr is a sweet and comforting melody. We are all grateful that you are here with us."

Karyl let out a tiny, breathless squeak as Kokkoro stroked her tail, her eyelids fluttering shut for a moment of helpless bliss before she buried her face into the crook of my neck, groaning in total defeat.

"I hate you guys so much..." she grumbled into my collarbone, though her small hands clutched my shoulder with undeniable warmth.

I smiled, wrapping my right arm around Pecorine and my left around Kokkoro, while keeping Karyl cradled securely against my chest. In that quiet pool beneath the Astraean moon, my Princess Knight resonance pulsed softly in my chest—a warm, golden ember that reached out to touch the souls of the three women who had become my entire world.

When I had awakened in this land with no memories, no past, and no knowledge of who I was, I had been helpless. But the Great Guide Ames had sent Kokkoro to guide me; Pecorine had found me starving on the plain and shared her bread with an open heart; and Karyl had joined our journey, fighting fiercely at our side through every trial. We had gathered wild ingredients in monster-infested forests, cooked meals over damp campfire embers, and defended each other with our lives.

Tonight, that shared journey had crossed the threshold into an eternal, unshakeable devotion. We were no longer just a wandering adventuring guild. We were a family bound by love, trust, and shared desire.

Pecorine reached out toward the stone edge of the pool and pulled over a floating cedar tray that had been moored to a wooden post. Upon the tray sat a collection of delicacies the innkeeper had prepared for us: small porcelain bowls of chilled wild mountain blackberries, candied chestnuts glazed in clover honey, sweet almond fruit tarts, and a carafe of iced plum wine.

"Midnight snack time!" Pecorine announced with triumphant cheer.

She selected a plump, glistening blackberry and held it up to my mouth. I leaned forward and ate the sweet, tart fruit from her fingers, licking away the crimson juice that lingered on her skin. Pecorine giggled with delight, then turned and fed one to Kokkoro, who accepted it with a gracious, formal bow of her head.

"Thank you, Pecorine-sama," Kokkoro said softly, chewing with delicate grace. "The fruit is wonderfully crisp."

"Here, Karyl-chan, open wide!" Pecorine urged, holding a candied chestnut toward the blushing mage.

"I can feed myself, you know!" Karyl protested, but when Pecorine pouted with exaggerated sadness, Karyl rolled her eyes and snapped the chestnut from her fingers with a huff. As she chewed, her eyes widened slightly at the rich, honeyed flavor, and her purr vibrated a notch louder against my chest. "...Okay, fine, it's actually really good."

"Everything tastes amazing when the four of us eat together!" Pecorine beamed, popping two tarts into her own mouth and chewing happily, her bare bosom bouncing with her joyous laughter.

Kokkoro reached for the carafe of iced plum wine, pouring the sweet, fragrant liquid into four small lacquered cups. She passed them around with careful, reverent movements, her silver hair catching the amber glow of the lantern light.

"To the Gourmet Guild," Kokkoro proposed softly, holding her cup aloft with both hands. "To our beloved Aruji-sama, to our valiant leader Pecorine-sama, and to our dear companion Karyl-chan. May our table always be bountiful, our path always illuminated, and our bonds never broken."

We touched our cups together above the steaming water, the light chime of lacquer echoing into the quiet night, and drank. The plum wine was cool, sweet, and soothing, washing down our throats and warming our bellies from within.

I leaned in and kissed Kokkoro on her soft lips, tasting the sweet wine on her breath. Her elf ears twitched with pure delight, and she leaned into the embrace with a gentle sigh. Turning to my right, I kissed Pecorine deeply, her full lips parting eagerly to meet mine in a passionate, lingering caress that left her gasping happily. Finally, I turned to Karyl. The catgirl averted her eyes for a fleeting second before tilting her chin up, meeting my lips in a fierce, possessive kiss that made her tail coil tightly around my leg.

"You three are the greatest treasure I could ever ask for," I told them honestly, looking into each of their eyes in turn. "No matter what challenges face us in Landosol, as long as we have each other, we will always find our way forward."

Pecorine smiled, her bright blue eyes glowing with royal warmth. Kokkoro nodded solemnly, her pink eyes filled with devotional certainty. Karyl sniffled softly, looking down at her empty wine cup with a radiant, affectionate smile curving her lips.

As the midnight hour passed, the cool mountain breeze blowing over the pool began to grow sharper, carrying the crisp chill of high-altitude frost. Our fingers and toes had begun to wrinkle from the extended soak, and the comforting lethargy of deep physical satisfaction weighed heavy on our eyelids.

"Let's move inside to the futons," I suggested, helping Karyl steady herself on the stone bench. "We'll catch a chill if we stay in the water all night."

"A wise suggestion, Aruji-sama," Kokkoro agreed, standing gracefully in the shallow water.

We climbed out of the thermal pool together, our wet skin glistening under the moonlight. The crisp night air sent a sudden shiver through all of us, but large, thick linen towels and warm cotton yukata were already waiting on the cedar benches beneath the eaves.

We helped each other dry off—Pecorine energetically rubbing a towel over Karyl's wet hair while the catgirl complained about her ears getting tangled; Kokkoro carefully patting down my back and shoulders with tender efficiency; and I drying Kokkoro's slender frame before draping a soft green yukata over her shoulders.

Once dressed in our roomy, comfortable sleeping robes, we made our way into the inn's grand guest suite. The room was spacious, floored with fragrant tatami mats and illuminated by the warm, gentle glow of an oil lamp placed in the alcove. The innkeeper had laid out four thick, silk-stuffed futons side by side, pushed together to form one continuous, luxurious bed beneath heavy down quilts.

Pecorine dove onto the bedding first, landing with a soft bounce and rolling onto her back with a loud, contented groan.

"Ahhh! Soft futons are the best invention in the entire world!" she cheered, patting the mattress on both sides of her. "Come on, everyone! Pile in!"

I lay down beside her, and Pecorine immediately hauled me against her side, wrapping an arm and a leg over me like an eager bear. Kokkoro slipped into the futon on my other side, tucking herself neatly beneath the quilt and resting her head on my chest, her small hand finding mine beneath the heavy blankets.

Karyl stood at the edge of the tatami mats for a moment, crossing her arms and looking down at the tangled pile of bodies with a skeptical frown.

"You really expect all four of us to sleep in one giant knot like this?" she questioned, raising an eyebrow. "It's absurdly cramped."

"There's plenty of room right in the middle, Karyl-chan!" Pecorine beamed, reaching out and grabbing Karyl by the hem of her yukata, pulling her down onto the mattress with effortless strength.

"Eek! Pecorine, let go!" Karyl yelped, tumbling into the center of the bed between Pecorine and me.

Within seconds, the quilts were pulled up to our chins. Despite her protests, Karyl quickly settled into the warmth, curling onto her side with her back against Pecorine's chest and her face tucked into my collarbone. Her black cat tail snaked out from beneath her robe, wrapping securely around my wrist. Within two minutes, her breathing slowed to a peaceful rhythm, accompanied by that deep, gentle purr that vibrated through the shared blankets.

Kokkoro snuggled closer against my left flank, letting out a soft sigh of utter serenity. Pecorine's furnace-like warmth radiated through the quilts, keeping the midnight chill completely at bay. Wrapped in the embrace of the three women I loved, surrounded by the scent of clean cotton, pine needles, and sweet moonflowers, I closed my eyes and fell into the deepest, most restful sleep of my life.

Dawn arrived with a gentle cascade of golden light spilling through the wooden lattice of the sliding paper screens. The mountain birds sang in the cedar groves outside, their clear, melodious chirps announcing the birth of a glorious new day.

I opened my eyes to find the room bathed in soft amber radiance. The three girls were still sound asleep around me, their faces peaceful and serene in the morning light. Pecorine was snoring softly, a faint smile on her lips as she dreamed of banquets; Kokkoro lay curled like a delicate flower against my chest; and Karyl's cat ears twitched occasionally at the bird calls outside, her tail still faithfully linked to my wrist.

A quiet knock sounded at the outer sliding screen, followed by the respectful, subdued voice of the innkeeper.

"Pardon the interruption, honored guests. The grand morning banquet you requested has been prepared."

At the word *banquet*, Pecorine's blue eyes snapped wide open with lightning speed.

"FOOD?!" she gasped, sitting bolt upright in the bed and flinging the down quilt across the room in a burst of boundless morning energy.

"Gah! Pecorine, it's freezing! Put the blanket back!" Karyl shrieked, curling into a tight ball as the crisp morning air struck her.

Kokkoro blinked awake with a gentle yawn, rubbing her pink eyes before bowing her head toward me with a serene smile. "Good morning, Aruji-sama. Did you rest well?"

"I rested wonderfully, Kokkoro," I laughed, sitting up and gathering the blankets back around a shivering Karyl.

The sliding screens opened, and the innkeeper, accompanied by two lodge attendants, carried in four large lacquer dining trays laden with an extraordinary morning feast. There were thick fillets of grilled river salmon sizzling with savory fat, golden tamagoyaki rolled with sweet dashi, bowls of simmered mountain bamboo shoots and wild mushrooms, steaming pots of dark miso soup, pickled plum dishes, mounds of fragrant white rice, and large bowls of sweet red bean soup with toasted mochi.

"Yabai desu ne!" Pecorine squealed in pure ecstasy, clapping her hands together. "It looks completely delicious! Thank you so much!"

The attendants bowed and retreated, sliding the screen shut to give us our privacy.

We gathered around the low cedar tables, sitting close together on the tatami mats in our yukata. Kokkoro poured freshly brewed green tea into our cups with practiced elegance, offering each bowl with a polite bow.

"Please enjoy the morning meal, everyone," Kokkoro said, her face glowing with youthful happiness.

"Itadakimasu!" Pecorine cheered, diving into her feast with breathtaking speed. She devoured a fillet of salmon in two massive bites, followed by half a bowl of steaming rice, humming in profound culinary bliss. "Mmm! The salmon skin is so crispy and savory! Yuuki, you have to try some of this rolled egg!"

She picked up a thick slice of tamagoyaki with her chopsticks and fed it to me. The egg was sweet, fluffy, and bursting with rich broth.

Karyl sat beside me, sipping her green tea with a calm, contented expression. When she noticed me looking at her, she blushed lightly, picked up a choice morsel of grilled fish from her plate, and dropped it into my rice bowl with an aloof toss of her head.

"Here. You need the protein more than I do, sword boy. Don't let Pecorine eat everything."

"Thank you, Karyl," I smiled, eating the fish with relish.

Kokkoro passed me a bowl of hot miso soup, her small fingers lingering against mine as I accepted the bowl. "May this meal grant you abundant strength for our journey home, Aruji-sama."

We sat together in the bright morning sun, eating, laughing, and sharing food from each other's platters. The air was filled with the delicious aroma of roasted sesame, sweet dashi, and savory fish. Outside the window, the majestic peaks of Astraea stretched across the horizon under an endless blue sky, full of mystery, danger, and untold wonders waiting to be discovered.

We raised our teacups together in a final toast, our laughter ringing out through the quiet mountain lodge. We were the Gourmet Guild—and together, there was no adventure we could not conquer, and no feast we could not savor.
