# "You" — Reader-Insert Protagonist Dossier

> *No name is ever written here. "You" signs the adventurer card "Arith," because what else do you call a man who remembers every number he has ever touched?*

## Identity
- **Full name:** Unstated (reader-insert; keep it vague, never fill it in).
- **Adventurer name (card):** Arith — registered on a promissory note in Chapter 1.
- **Age:** "Old enough to regret a preferred-risk clause." Adult; no number is given.
- **Previous life:** Import-export broker for a third-generation trading house. Deadline-haunted, decimal-minded, allergic to losing margin.
- **Death:** A freight container, a failed strap, a wet dock. He had time to compute the insurance write-off, which is, he tells anyone who asks, a humiliation he carries.

## The Gift (AU, declared in `world/au_notes.md`)
Eris's parting grant: a perfect, deliberate memory for everything with **a number, a face, a price, or a promise**. It is a memory aid only — no mind-reading, no omniscience, no deus ex machina. He remembers:
- Every sum he has seen tallied (guild payouts, quest slips, the price of a tart, the interest on his own registration note).
- Every face he has met — one glance, and the face is logged.
- Every promise uttered in his hearing. His favorite: *"One tart, payment on delivery."*

## Concrete Desire (Chapter One)
Land on his feet as an independent principal (never a clerk, never a mugger's prey): clear the registration note, pay the first quest forward, and get invited back to the counter where Luna works.

## Core Wound / Misbelief
Died mid-deal, margins memorized and nothing else loved. Misbelief: *"If I can keep the numbers straight, I never have to feel the rest."* Luna is the first thing that stops adding up neatly — which is exactly what keeps him there.

## Tactile Tic
Half-invisible abacus: fingers and thumb moving through sums when he's listening — on quest slips, on her clock, and once, memorably, on the small of her back.

## Dialogue Cadence
- Dry, deliberate; speaks in clauses a contract lawyer would recognize.
- Reads people by their ledgers — the guild's, the tavern's, hers.
- Teases by *misquoting* numbers he absolutely remembers incorrectly, just to see her correct him. She always does. Correction is foreplay and they both know it.

## Notes for Drafting (Anti-OOC / Reader-Insert Rules)
- Appearance-agnostic: no hair color, eye color, height, or distinctive feature is ever assigned to the body.
- Agency rule: he earns everything (the note, the quest, the nights) — nothing arrives as luck.
- He is competent and gentle in ways that matter, but never a fantasy wish-machine; he is as reckless with promises as she is with paperwork.
