# Luna — Character Dossier

> *"I keep impeccable records. It's the only thing I'm allowed to be reckless about."*

## Identity
- **Full name:** Luna (canon; the guild registers her by one name — she insists there is no second name on the records, "and if you find one, you've kept better notes than I have").
- **Epithet:** The Guild Lady; *the prettiest receptionist in Axel* (canon Kazuma's assessment, which ours silently agrees with).
- **Age:** 20 (canon; birthday 15 August).
- **Occupation:** Senior receptionist, Axel branch, Adventurer's Guild.

## Appearance (canon)
- Blonde hair in a high bun, burgundy band, spiky tuft, two orange-tipped bangs.
- Gold eyes, warm smile that goes professional on a copper the moment a problem adventurer walks in.
- Full figure, slender waist, generous bust that the low-cut white guild top does exactly nothing to hide.
- Uniform: off-shoulder white top, red bowtie at the throat, black cuffs, gold star pin on the right sleeve, short blue cuffed shorts, brown ankle boots.

## Concrete Desire (Chapter One)
To feel *chosen* — by one competent, polite person — instead of being the woman everyone relies on and nobody keeps. She collects him because he is the first new adventurer in months who remembers her name, her announcements, and the price of a tart, and pays his debts on time.

## Core Wound / Misbelief
The guild's turnover is a wound she hides behind paperwork: every co-worker who married left her with double the ledger and zero prospects. Her misbelief: *"I am the one who stays and keeps the accounts, so I am the one who ends up alone."* It makes her demanding in love once she lets herself have it — she sets the terms because no one else ever has.

## Tactile Tic
**Counting under her breath.** Whether it's a queue of adventurers, a stack of quest slips, or (later) the number of foil packets on the nightstand, her lips move silently through sums. When truly lost in pleasure, the counting stops — which is how "you" always know she's gone.

## Dialogue Cadence
- Terse, efficient, ledger-flavored at work; fewer than ten words when flustered.
- Vocabulary register: bookkeeping, contract, coin — *interest, principal, balance, receipt, settlement, collateral*.
- In bed she stays in that register on purpose — "account settled," "deferred interest," "that's a receivable now" — because precision is the only filthy thing she says naturally. It becomes their private seal.
- Topic-deflection tactic: paperwork. When a question is too tender, she answers it with a count or a quest stipulation.

## Contradiction
A woman who enforces the guild's most precise paperwork is secretly the town's most impatient romantic. She has mastered the queue and never once mastered waiting.

## Notes for Drafting (Anti-OOC Rules)
- She is never a damsel and never a passive partner; *she seizes the moment* (canon) — she kisses him, she sets the pace, she demands the third round.
- She is frazzled and lonely, but never pathetic about it: her desire reads as hunger, not neediness.
- Authority is erotic to her: done *to* her, deferred to her, and — at the end — argued with and negotiated. Power flips are the point.
