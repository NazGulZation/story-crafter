A man built on import-export ledgers and exact arithmetic is crushed beneath his own cargo, meets the Goddess of Fortune, and is reincarnated into the Kingdom of Belzerg carrying one unfair advantage: a perfect, merciless memory for numbers, faces, prices, and promises.
