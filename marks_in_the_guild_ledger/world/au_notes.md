## Canon Facts Used (verified)

### Luna — receptionist of the Axel Adventurer's Guild
- **Age:** 20± (birthday 15 August, per author Natsume Akatsuki's official Twitter).
- **Race / gender:** Human, female. Occupation: guild staff.
- **Appearance (anime):** Blonde hair tied back in a high bun held by a burgundy band, with a spiky tuft on top and two orange-tipped bangs framing the face; gold / golden-yellow eyes; gentle smiling face; "generous bosom" that gives her a mature look; slim waist and noticeably full bust.
- **Uniform (anime):** Low-cut white off-shoulder top revealing cleavage, black shoulders/sleeve cuffs, red ribbon/bowtie at the neck, three orange toggle clasps, gold star pin on the right sleeve, short cuffed blue jean shorts, short brown boots with beige cuffs.
- **Personality:** Down-to-earth and gentle; polite and sweet with everyone while barely hiding her discontent with troublesome adventurers; strong-willed and cunning at work — willing to seize the moment and set the stage to compel adventurers to do quests. Constantly frazzled by problem adventurers (Kazuma's party, Dust), responsible for cleaning up their messes. Sleeps in her free time, has no social life, and is *desperate for romance*; co-workers keep quitting to marry, leaving her more work and slowly turning her toward spinsterhood. Her marital status is her weak spot — mocking her singledom is taboo in the guild.
- **Role:** Main receptionist at the Axel branch; announces emergency quests over the guild's magical speaker (anime) — used in Chapter 10.
- **Trivia used:** Kazuma considered her the prettiest receptionist (grounds "you" finding her beautiful); she became an established character from the anime onward (justifies a fleshed-out AU romance).

### The Adventurer's Guild
- Governmental organization with branches across the parallel world; Axel's branch is a meeting place + recreation facility: quest board, tavern serving monster-based dishes, buying monster materials.
- New adventurer registration costs **1,000 Eris**.
- Adventurers receive a **card**; completed quests are recorded when the monster's name is verified (card crystal / card list of slain monsters).
- No formal rank system (only informal beginner/intermediate/advanced terms).
- Magical speaker that can be heard city-wide for emergency quest announcements.
- Guild staff include waitresses and receptionists; the "Guild Lady" pre-anime manga is a different character — our Luna is the anime canon one.

### Axel (beginner town, Kingdom of Belzerg)
- Farthest town from the Demon King's territory; safe, peaceful starter town.
- Beginning adventurers level up on weak monsters nearby: **goblin scavengers**, **giant toads**, lovely rabbits.
- Caravans of cabbages pass through; the town's gates have a single guard.
- Landmarks: the Adventurer's Guild, Eris Church, Axis Church, Hydra's Lake (half a day away).

### Eris — Goddess of Fortune
- Bronze-haired goddess who handles reincarnated humans; whimsical, gentle, sharp. Currency is named after her ("Eris" coins and "small Eris" coins).
- Canon game/bonus material lets her send souls with one carried item/gift; our "perfect memory" gift is an AU extension of that mechanic (see AU below).

### Sena
- Royal prosecutor stationed in Axel; long straight black hair, purple-tinted eyes, gold-rimmed glasses, blue prosecutor uniform, stern but secretly soft; president of the "Association to Protect Women's Chances of Marriage." *Not* a guild receptionist — she appears only as a brief cameo in Chapter 10, canon-safe.

## AU (Author-Approved Inventions)

Everything below is *not* canon and is flagged here so the ledger stays honest:

1. **The gift.** The protagonist's "perfect memory" is an AU cheat-gift. It functions as a *memory aid*, not mind-reading and not omniscience — he can only recall what he has actually seen/heard/read. No deus ex machina.
2. **Luna's flat.** Canon never shows her residence. We place it above a shuttered bakery on a lane off Silver Street (bun smell, warm bread — her everyday sensory signature). Rented, modest, candlelit.
3. **The foil-packet subplot.** Axel's apothecaries sell waxed-foil condom packets (adventurer trade good; the world has anachronistic trade mechanics). Purchase, pocket, count, and disposal are Seeded (Ch1), Inventoried (Ch4), Used (Ch6/8/9), Bagged (Ch9), and Disposed (Ch10) — 3 in, 3 out, never conjured.
4. **Timeline compression.** The encounter arcs run on two contiguous evenings/day (Day 1 ~20:45–23:50; Day 2 ~07:30–15:00). Realistic rest/water/food bridges; soreness compounds; no instant re-hardening.
5. **The romantic resolution.** Canon Luna is lonely and desperate for romance; the fic gives her a genuine, earned one. Chapter 10 is an opening, not a landing — they are new lovers, not a fairy-tale ending.
