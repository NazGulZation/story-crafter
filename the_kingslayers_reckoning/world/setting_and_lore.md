# World Setting & Lore: The Kingslayer's Reckoning

---

## 1. Geography & Settlements

### The March of Oster-Reach & Barrow-Gate
- **Barrow-Gate**: A fortified frontier hub built upon ancient basalt terraces at the mouth of the Grey Spires. Surrounding limestone walls, wet timber scaffolding, muddy alleys, and slate-roofed guildhalls. It controls the toll routes between the rich southern agricultural lowlands and the mineral-laden highland crags.
- **The Grey Spires**: A jagged mountain range to the north, rich in iron veins, silver lodes, and quarry granite, but crawling with highland crag-stalkers, frost-trolls, and mountain fiends.
- **The Low Ward & The Gilded Boar**: The sprawling lower quarter outside the inner curtain wall, packed with taverns, stables, smithies, and cheap boarding houses. *The Gilded Boar* is a rowdy, timber-framed tavern catering to low-tier adventuring parties, carters, and mercenary recruiters.
- **The High Ward & The Sun-Plaza**: The aristocratic sector on the upper basalt terrace, featuring the Margrave's keep, the grand tourney grounds, and the gleaming marble cathedral of the Goddess Sol-Aethel.

## 2. Commerce & Currencies

- **Zinc Bits**: Cheap stamped frontier coins used by common laborers and tavern porters for small beer and coarse bread.
- **Copper Crowns**: Standard currency for day-to-day provisions, iron nails, arrows, and cheap lodging (100 zinc bits = 1 copper crown).
- **Silver Marks**: High-purity silver coinage minted by the Margraviate, used for mercenary bounties, armor purchases, and horse trades (20 copper crowns = 1 silver mark).
- **Gold Sovereigns**: Heavy, milled gold coins bearing the sunburst crest, held by merchant syndicates, nobility, and high-tier guild masters (10 silver marks = 1 gold sovereign).

## 3. The Divine Order & Theological Conflict

### The New Cult: Sol-Aethel (The Goddess of the Radiant Dawn)
- A youthful, ascendant deity whose worship has swept the southern kingdoms over the past two centuries.
- Her doctrine equates physical beauty, aristocratic symmetry, and charismatic charm with divine righteousness.
- She bestows cheat-like blessings upon chosen mortals based on vanity and stature: superhuman physical strength, blinding solar halos, automated sword-beam discharges, and instant wound cauterization.
- Her followers have grown decadent, arrogant, and reliant on statutory power, losing the understanding of true labor, martial discipline, and tactical patience.

### The Forgotten God: The Anvil of Ash
- An ancient, primal sovereign deity worshiped during the founding wars centuries ago when men had to quarry mountains with hand tools and hold shield-walls against frost hordes with muscle and blood.
- Possesses no grand marble temples; his shrines are weathered monoliths of dark granite hidden in deep pine forests, marked with cold iron anvils and grey ash.
- Demands respect for iron, discipline, bone, and sweat.
- Disgusted by Sol-Aethel's careless distribution of cheat blessings to vainglorious boys, the Anvil of Ash intervenes by granting Jaime Lannister the tool to shatter her false idols.

## 4. The Supernatural Mechanics: The Sovereign Duel

### Definition & Invocation
- A divine domain ability bestowed upon Ser Jaime Lannister in Chapter 3 by the Forgotten God.
- Jaime invokes the duel mentally or verbally, designating a single sentient opponent within line of sight.

### The Laws of the Duel
1. **Total Magical Nullification**:
   - For both the invoker (Jaime) and the target, **EVERY** divine blessing, magical enhancement, active skill, passive stat-assist, aura-shield, automated combat reflex, and spellcasting channel is instantly stripped and suppressed.
   - Both combatants are reduced strictly to their **natural human physiology, bone density, muscle endurance, and raw unassisted skill**.
2. **Fixed Duration**:
   - The null-zone lasts for exactly **60 seconds (1 minute)**, or terminates early if one combatant dies.
   - At the 60-second mark, the field collapses immediately, restoring normal physical and magical laws.
3. **External Damage Vulnerability & Battlefield Interference**:
   - The duel does **NOT** create an impenetrable barrier.
   - Third parties can intervene: archers can shoot arrows into the circle, bystanders can throw spears, and mages can cast spells from outside. Both duelists can be wounded or killed by external forces during the sixty seconds.
4. **Cooldown Cost & Physical Sensation**:
   - Strictly **1 hour** of physical recovery before the domain can be invoked again.
   - **Sensory & Physical Toll**: Consumes no magical mana, but releases a deep, freezing ash chill beneath Jaime's ribs that leaves his chest feeling like cold forged iron. During the sixty seconds, victory demands absolute, unassisted mortal muscle, stamina, and martial precision; once the zone collapses, the mortal body bears the full accumulated exhaustion of unbuffered combat.

## 5. Combat Systems & The Adventurer Illusion

- **The Modern Adventurer**:
   - Dependent on the Adventurers' Guild "Aura System"—a manifestation of ambient blessing that guides blade swings, absorbs concussive impact, and boosts agility.
   - Modern fighters telegraph their strikes wildly because the blessing corrects blade alignment mid-air. They do not understand leverage, distance management, or defensive recovery.
- **The Westerosi Knight (Jaime Lannister)**:
   - Trained from age eight in the brutal, unsparing realities of castle yards under Arthur Dayne, Barristan Selmy, and the iron discipline of Tywin Lannister's men-at-arms.
   - Senses balance in the hips, edge alignment in the forearm, weight distribution in the soles of the boots, and reads muscle twitches in the opponent's shoulder three heartbeats before a strike lands.
   - Within the 60 seconds of the *Sovereign Duel*, modern "champions" are merely clumsy sheep before an apex predator.
