# Character Dossiers: The Kingslayer's Reckoning

---

### Ser Jaime Lannister (Incarnated in the Mortal Vessel of Theron Marrow)
- **Identity & Epithet**: Former Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, the Kingslayer, Lion of Lannister; now occupying the nineteen-year-old mortal shell of Theron Marrow.
- **Physical Form**: Nineteen years of age, lean, broad-shouldered, with dark tangled hair and sharp slate-grey eyes. The boy's frame was wiry and undernourished, lacking heavy muscle mass, but blessed with swift reflexes, solid bone alignment, and exceptional reach. Crucially: **two intact, living, flesh-and-blood hands**.
- **Voice & Psychology**: Sardonic, aristocratic, sharply observant of human folly, cynicism masking a deadly martial intelligence. Jaime spent a lifetime listening to hypocrites swear sacred oaths only to break them when convenient. The weeping and heartbreak of the boy Theron over a faithless girl strikes Jaime as raw childish idiocy. He feels no grief, no sorrow—only cold amusement and the intoxicating joy of ten working fingers to grip a sword hilt once again.
- **Formative Westerosi Memory**: Slaying the Mad King Aerys Targaryen over the wildfire pyres; sitting upon the Iron Throne waiting to see who would claim it; being knighted at fifteen by Ser Arthur Dayne; having his sword hand hacked off by Vargo Hoat in the Riverlands; dying beneath the red granite ruins of the Red Keep holding Cersei.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Arrogant self-reliance and disregard for public opinion. Jaime assumes his masterclass swordsmanship will always prevail, occasionally underestimating the physical stamina wall of his younger, unconditioned vessel.
- **Tactile Mannerism**: Flexing the fingers of his right hand against his palm, rolling the wrist to feel the tendons fire, and flashing a cold, razor-edged grin when fools threaten him.
- **Guiding Cadence**: Terse, biting, conversational arrogance, punctuated by precise martial terminology and contempt for pompous ceremony.

### Evander Montfort (The "Sun-Gilded Lance")
- **Identity & Epithet**: Leader of the *Sun-Vanguard*, favored champion of the New Goddess Sol-Aethel; eldest son of a frontier horse-factor.
- **Physical Form**: Strikingly tall, broad-chested, with golden curls, porcelain skin, aquiline nose, and dazzling violet eyes. Clad in mirror-polished gilded plate chased with sunburst engravings.
- **Core Trait & "Fun-to-Hate" Flavour**: Flamboyant vanity and theatrical egotism. Evander was granted immense divine strength and blinding holy aura slashes by the young Goddess Sol-Aethel purely because of his handsome countenance and tall stature. He treats combat as a stage play meant to harvest adulation, women, and free wine. Without his divine buffs, his footwork is sloppy, his swings overcommitted, and his balance pathetic.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Total dependency on divine cheats. He has never experienced real pain, never learned defensive blade recovery, and believes his holy light makes him invincible.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Booming, self-congratulatory, theatrical baritone laced with condescending chuckles and rehearsed heroic slogans.

### Lyra Cole (The Osanajimi / Field Herbalist)
- **Identity & Epithet**: Former childhood companion of Theron Marrow; mender and alchemist of the *Sun-Vanguard*.
- **Physical Form**: Petite, with chestnut curls, a scatter of copper freckles across her nose, and wide amber eyes. Once dressed in rough wool homespun, now draped in costly southern silk, a silver chain, and fox-fur trim paid for by dungeon loot.
- **Core Trait**: Venal opportunism driven by deep-seated poverty panic. Having grown up in the same ditch-poor hamlets as Theron, she used his quiet devotion to haul her baskets and protect her from wolves. The moment Evander offered luxury, social status, and carnal indulgence, she abandoned Theron without hesitation. To prove her loyalty to the glamorous hero, she loudly mocks Theron's sentry shifts and sexual inadequacy in front of tavern crowds.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Arrogance rooted in temporary physical favor. She fails to see that to a self-obsessed peacock like Evander, a common-born field girl is disposable amusement until a highborn heiress is available.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Sweet and soft-spoken in court; petty, venomous, and shrill when mocking those she considers beneath her.

---

### Seraphina Kestrel (The High Elementalist)
- **Identity & Epithet**: Sorceress of the *Sun-Vanguard*; runaway daughter of an eastern magistrate.
- **Physical Form**: Slender, pale, with sleek dark hair fastened by ivory pins and sharp hazel eyes. Wears azure silk robes trimmed with silver lace, carrying an amber-headed wand.
- **Core Trait**: Intellectual elitism and hidden chemical dependency. Seraphina disdains martial fighters as brute oxen, yet she suffers from severe mana-shakes and secretly consumes expensive distilled frost-elixirs to fuel her high-tier flame and lightning spells.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: She assumes magic renders steel obsolete, entirely helpless when forced into close-quarters combat without mana.

---

### Renata Fell (The Vanguard Skirmisher)
- **Identity & Epithet**: Scout and archer; former mercenary cutthroat from the border ridges.
- **Physical Form**: Sinewy, sun-bronzed, with cropped dark hair, a crooked nose, and callused palms. Wears oiled leather reinforced with dark iron studs.
- **Core Trait**: Worship of raw violence and apex predators. She despised Theron because he was quiet, steady, and unglamorous. She gravitated to Evander's explosive destructive power.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Contempt for patience and tactical discipline. She mistakes reckless charges for courage.

---

### The Forgotten God (The Anvil of Ash)
- **Identity**: Ancient, weathered sovereign deity of iron, stone, labor, and martial blood, neglected by the populace following the rise of the superficial Goddess of the Radiant Dawn.
- **Manifestation**: A looming stone monolith in the deep pine woods; speaks with the grinding acoustic weight of millstones and cold iron.
- **Purpose**: Seeks to discipline the flighty New Goddess and restore the brutal, unsparing laws of martial reality to the mortal plane. Bestows upon Jaime the *Sovereign Duel*.
