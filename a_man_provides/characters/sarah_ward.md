# Character Profile: Sarah Ward (Reworked)

- **Full Name**: Sarah Elizabeth Ward (née Jenkins)
- **Age / Appearance**: 29 years old. Slender, auburn hair kept in soft shoulder-length waves, expressive hazel eyes, neat manicure, tasteful suburban wardrobe (cashmere blends, high-waisted linen, dainty gold jewelry).
- **Social Role & Employment**: Part-time junior stylist / visual consultant at a boutique interior showroom in the suburbs. Spends roughly twenty hours a week out of the house; the rest is spent managing the household, shopping, and entertaining.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Grew up in an upper-middle-class family that experienced quiet financial strain during the 2008 recession. Formed an intense psychological terror of poverty and social embarrassment. Married David Ward because he was reliable, harmless, unthreatening, and willing to work punishing corporate overtime to grant her an idyllic four-bedroom suburban house and a European SUV.
- **The Evolution of the Affair (From Rejection to Illicit Addiction)**:
  - Six months prior, she reconnected with Derek Holt, an aggressive boyfriend from her college years.
  - When Derek first re-entered her life, armed with old compromising collegiate photographs and private confidences, she desperately rejected and resisted his advances. She cried, pleaded, and tried to push him away to preserve her respectable marriage and social standing.
  - Derek, however, did not want cash; he demanded her submission, threatening total public humiliation if she refused. Under duress, she agreed to meet him.
  - Yet as the weeks progressed, Sarah’s psychological dynamic underwent a dark, insidious transformation. The dread shifted into an intoxicating, forbidden adrenaline rush.
  - She began secretly reveling in the double life. The stark contrast between her gentle, predictable, exhausted husband and the dangerous, swaggering arrogance of Derek fed a deep, narcissistic vanity.
  - She found herself secretly thrilled by the audacity of deceiving David under his own roof—leaving Derek's preferred drinks in the fridge, welcoming Derek into the house while David was at the office, and wearing an angelic, doting smile at the dinner table.
  - She still hides behind the rationalization that she was "forced into this to protect David from heartbreak," but in truth, she has become thoroughly addicted to the forbidden excitement and holds David's quiet softness in unspoken contempt.
- **Concrete External Want**: Keep Derek satisfied so he does not ruin her reputation; maintain the intoxicating illicit double life while keeping David working and compliant.
- **Deep Internal Need**: Constant physical and emotional stimulation paired with complete social safety; validation that she is desirable, exciting, and untamed, without paying the social penalty of being an outcast.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Narcissistic compartmentalization and extreme cowardice; believes she is an untouchable actress who can manipulate both men indefinitely without facing the catastrophic reckoning of reality.
- **Tactile Tic**: Twisting her diamond wedding band around her finger with her thumb; biting the corner of her lower lip while checking her phone beneath table edges.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: High, melodious, overly sweet when placating David; hurried, breathy, and teasing when speaking to Derek. Prone to passive-aggressive deflections ("You're just so tired, honey," "You're imagining things").
