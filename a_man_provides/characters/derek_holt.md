# Character Profile: Derek Holt (Reworked)

- **Full Name**: Derek Ryan Holt
- **Age / Appearance**: 31 years old. 6'2", heavily muscled with broad shoulders, buzz-cut dark brown hair, perpetual heavy stubble, prominent jaw, faded tribal/geometric sleeve tattoo down his right arm. Wears fitted compression shirts, distressed denim, heavy work boots, and a gold curb chain.
- **Social Role & Employment**: Head personal trainer at an upscale commercial fitness club (Iron Vault Fitness) and minority partner/manager at an independent auto-body and vehicle performance shop (Holt Customs & Performance).
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: High school athletic star who washed out of collegiate athletics due to chronic disciplinary infractions. Resents educated, white-collar professionals whom he views as soft, weak parasites hiding behind keyboards and spreadsheets. Built an underground reputation based on physical intimidation and illicit grey-market performance compounds.
- **The Core Motivation (Dominance & Territorial Conquest)**:
  - Derek does not target Sarah Ward for money. He operates out of pure predatory narcissism, class resentment, and sadistic ego.
  - To him, men like David—disciplined, quiet, white-collar providers—are pathetic sheep who don't deserve the comfort, the home, or the attractive wife they work themselves to death to provide.
  - Six months ago, upon discovering Sarah in the area, Derek leveraged old college secrets and compromising personal disclosures—not to extort cash, but to force her into his orbit and claim psychological dominion over David’s home, life, and marriage.
  - He relishes the supreme thrill of territorial conquest: strutting through David's house, drinking David's eighteen-year-old scotch, lounging in David's armchairs, and treating the marital home as his conquered ground.
- **Concrete External Want**: Maintain complete ownership of Sarah as a personal trophy; humiliate David Ward and force the husband into a state of total, acknowledged submission.
- **Deep Internal Need**: Constant, violent validation of his alpha dominance and superiority over men with more legitimate status and social respect than him.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Blinding hubris and contempt for quiet professionals; assumes quiet, polite men are incapable of calculated, devastating retaliation; completely oblivious to administrative, legal, and operational vulnerabilities.
- **Downfall Vector (Poetic Justice)**: His own obsession with dominance and intimidation leads him directly into an orchestrated trap where his predatory behavior, unauthorized surveillance, and illegal commercial practices are exposed, stripping away his alpha facade on-page.
- **Tactile Tic**: Cracking his knuckles by pressing his palms together; spitting toothpick slivers; running a thick thumb across his jawline when posturing.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Low, raspy, swaggering. Filled with aggressive taunts, mocking chuckles, sports metaphors, and vulgar nicknames ("pretty boy", "little girl", "hubby").
