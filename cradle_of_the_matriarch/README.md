# Cradle of the Matriarch

> *"No more girls die for me."*

A dark, tragic, bittersweet interactive branching narrative set in the world of *Princess Connect! Re:Dive*. 

## Overview
When the endless loops fractured the simulation beyond repair and true salvation was revealed to be a cruel impossibility, Kaiser Insight merged with the core of the Tower of Sol, becoming the **Primal Matriarch**. Under a leaden sky strung with neural mana-tethers, Landosol was plunged into an inescapable cradle of artificial peace, where individuality and pain are harvested to keep the dying world alive.

Yuuki—lucid, battle-hardened, and haunted by the memories of countless failed resets—refuses to let his companions sacrifice their lives. In every path, he shoulders the fatal toll of his Princess Knight power. In the True Ending, he launches an unassisted solo suicide run up the Tower of Sol, walking the path of the Reaper alone to bear the world's curse and buy a genuine dawn for everyone he loves.

## Features
- **12-Tier Narrative Spine**: A grueling climb from the ash-choked slums to the apex of the Tower of Sol.
- **Pure Out-Tree Architecture**: Zero convergence, strict in-degree = 1, ensuring all choices retain absolute causal weight.
- **6 Dedicated Terminal Endings**: 5 tragic guild sacrifices (Caon, Nightmare, Labyrinth, Twinkle Wish, Gourmet Guild) and 1 True Solo Martyr Ending.
- **Visceral Sensory Grounding & Anti-AI Discipline**: Written under the strict prose quality standards of `GEMINI.md`.

## Directory Layout
- `chapters/`: All 17 story nodes (Spine chapters, branch chapters, and 6 terminal endings).
- `characters/`: Character profiles and psychological tracking.
- `world/`: Detailed lore, mana-tether mechanics, and setting design.
- `outlines/`: Interactive graph schematics and chapter breakdown.
