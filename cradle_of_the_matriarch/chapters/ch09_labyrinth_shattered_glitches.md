# Chapter 9: Shattered Glitches

The moment my crystallized left palm locked into the terminal’s intake socket, my nervous system was seized by a roaring tempest of raw computational data.

It did not feel like magic. It was an electrical storm of pure mathematical logic, cascading through my synapses at the speed of light. Billions of hexadecimal parameters, system memory registers, and raw physics equations surged through my optic nerves, turning my vision into a blinding kaleidoscope of scrolling green code and wireframe grids. The sensory feedback was overwhelming: sound took on physical geometric weight, colors hummed with audible frequencies, and the taste of bitter ozone filled my mouth with every labored breath.

In that blinding rush of data, I saw Landosol not as stone and streets, but as a vast, living digital organism bleeding out from the inside.

Through the terminal's telemetry, I perceived the memory addresses of seven million sleeping souls, flickering like dim amber stars inside Kaiser's massive memory banks. I saw the silver tethers descending like cold computational pipelines, siphoning the warmth of human joy, the sharp sparks of grief, and the creative spark of living minds to keep the dying simulation from falling into total entropy. And through that sprawling web, my Princess Knight core acted as a colossal, burning lightning rod—a singleton administrative key that forced the compiler to accelerate with terrifying, deafening velocity.

Directly inside my auditory cortex, Akira’s voice crackled through the neural link, sharp, frantic, and stripped of all emotional distance.

*"Connection stabilized, Yuuki!"* her thoughts fired into my brain like telegraph clicks. *"The bandwidth is incredible! Your core is processing eight petabytes per clock cycle! The memory allocation tables are holding, and I'm routing the sanctuary protocols directly into the bedrock physics layer of the southern caverns! But the system's security immune response is deploying every daemon it has left!"*

The compilation progress bar on Akira's primary holographic display surged forward with an audible chime: *Kernel Compilation: 62%*.

The Null Sweeper shrieked—a sound like grinding sheet metal broadcast through a thousand blown speakers, vibrating with raw, algorithmic hatred.

The twelve-legged algorithmic monstrosity plunged downward, its front talons striking the obsidian glass platform twenty feet in front of the terminal. The impact sent jagged, polygonal fissures racing through the floor; four square yards of glass pixelated into grey static, falling away into the bottomless abyss of null-space below. From the gaping rift crawled a dozen smaller glitch stalkers, their multi-jointed bodies twitching with chaotic, corrupted data packets as they scrambled toward the dais, their needle-claws clicking rapidly against the remaining glass.

"Protect the terminal!" Shizuru roared, her voice stripped of all restraint, ringing with desperate maternal fury.

She leapt across the widening fissure, her katana trailing brilliant violet sparks as she struck. Her blade met the lead glitch stalker at the joint, severing the polygonal limb in a shower of screaming green code-fragments. She spun on her heel, her footwork clean and lethal despite the shifting gravitational anomalies, her blade tracing an unbroken circle of steel that bisected three more stalkers in rapid succession, kicking their twitching carcasses into the void.

Beside her, Rino moved with breathtaking courage, her composite bow drawn to its absolute limit, her fingers blistered and raw as she channeled pure spiritual mana directly through her bare flesh.

"Leave my sister alone, you ugly bugs!" Rino screamed, releasing a volley of three radiant mana-shafts in rapid succession. 

The glowing arrows struck the Sweeper’s central optical array with concussive precision. The creature's sensor cluster fractured in an explosion of blue light, throwing wild arcs of corrupted purple lightning across the chamber, blinding its automated targeting routines and sending its remaining legs thrashing blindly into the glass, gouging deep trenches into the obsidian floor.

I stood locked to the terminal, my left arm fused to the intake bus. 

The heat was staggering, an internal furnace that threatened to vaporize my sanity. The azure crystal along my shoulder and throat was glowing with blinding intensity, but where the crystal met my living skin, the flesh was dissolving into light. Tiny pixels of translucent blue code were flaking off my right forearm and ribs, drifting upward into the terminal's holographic field like glowing motes of dust in a beam of sunlight. Sensation was receding from my legs; my boots felt distant, disconnected, as if I were hovering inches above the glass.

*Kernel Compilation: 74%.*

"Hold it, Yuuki!" Akira shouted, her face pale as bone, blood dripping steadily from her nostrils onto the console frame. "The kernel structure is solidifying! Three more minutes! Just give me three more minutes of bandwidth!"

The Null Sweeper retracted its severed legs, rearing its massive iron-and-static chassis fifty feet into the air. 

Recognizing that conventional physical strikes could not breach the Princess Knight’s resonance shield, the creature shifted its operational protocol. Its central thorax split open with a sickening, mechanical squelch, revealing a pulsating, spherical void of absolute blackness—a localized Kernel Purge Daemon designed to delete corrupted system partitions.

A wave of null-gravity swept through the platform.

Loose cables, shattered pieces of glass, and discarded weapon fragments were yanked into the air, spiraling toward the black sphere like debris into a whirlpool. The floor beneath Shizuru’s boots began to disintegrate into grey noise, crumbling toward the void.

"Shizuru, jump!" I yelled, my voice sounding flat, modulated by digital static.

I forced a burst of Princess Knight resonance down my right arm, extending a tether of solid blue light across the widening abyss. 

Shizuru caught the light-thread with her left gauntlet just as the platform beneath her collapsed into nothingness. The kinetic jolt nearly tore my shoulder from its socket, sending jagged spikes of white-hot agony through my chest, but I dug my heels into the dais and hauled her upward with everything I had left.

She landed hard on the remaining ledge of the console dais, rolling to her feet, her breathing coming in ragged, whistling gasps. Her kimono was torn to ribbons, her knuckles bleeding, but she immediately planted her boots between me and the descending void, raising her notched katana with two shaking hands.

"I have you," Shizuru whispered, her voice fierce and trembling with tears. "I will not let them touch you, Yuuki. Not while I can draw a breath."

The Null Sweeper lowered its central core toward our platform. 

The black sphere expanded, devouring the light, devouring the code, pulling the physical walls of the Mirror Archives inward like paper collapsing into a furnace. The terminal’s outer monitors shattered into static, the glass raining down around our heads in glowing shards.

*Kernel Compilation: 82%.*

"The compilation is stalling!" Akira screamed through the howling electronic vortex, her hands slamming against the keyboard as warning sirens wailed. "The Sweeper is eating the memory addresses faster than I can write them! The sector boundary is crumbling! Yuuki, we need an administrative override! We have to purge the daemon from inside the wireframe!"

I looked at the black sphere hovering ten yards above our platform, its gravitational pull tearing the cloth from my sleeves.

I looked at Shizuru's bloodied blade, and at Rino standing with her empty bow, her eyes filled with unbroken, defiant courage.

The path ahead was narrow as a blade's edge.

If the daemon ate the compiler, the sanctuary would die before it was born. Landosol’s underground would be scrubbed, leaving no haven for the human souls who resisted Kaiser's peace. 

I took a deep breath of the ionized, ozone-choked air, felt the steady, stubborn beat of the Princess Knight core in my dissolving chest, and gripped my notched steel sword with my right hand. The decision was already made before the thought was formed.

"Akira," I said, my voice echoing through both her speakers and her neural implants. "Transfer the root override commands directly to my blade. I'll carry the execution code into the daemon's center."

Akira looked up from her monitors, her eyes wide with shock and horror. "Yuuki, you can't! That's a direct insertion into an active deletion vortex! The moment you cross that event horizon, the void will rip away your physical envelope! You won't be able to pull yourself back out!"

"The kernel is at eighty-two percent," I replied, feeling the blue crystal humming against the terminal socket, holding the data channel steady with my petrified left hand. "If I don't spear that eye, it drops to zero. Compile the sanctuary, Akira. Don't waste my bandwidth."

Shizuru reached out, her trembling, blood-stained fingers clutching my right wrist, her violet eyes swimming with unbearable grief. "Yuuki... please... don't leave me behind..."

"I'm not leaving you, Shizuru," I told her softly, leaning in to press my forehead against hers for one quiet, fleeting second. "I'm building you a house where the sky can never hurt you."

I pulled my arm free from her grasp, turned toward the gaping black sphere of the Null Sweeper, and channeled the entire remaining output of the Princess Knight core into the steel of my sword.
