# Guild Factions: The Remnants of Landosol

## 1. Caon (The Beastfolk Resistance)
- **Members**: Maho, Makoto, Kaori, Kasumi.
- **Location**: Southern Forest Perimeter & The Animal Quarter.
- **Condition & Stakes**: The animal quarter is being systematically cordoned off by Kaiser's neural harvesters to assimilate the beastfolk's feral, instinctive mana. Makoto leads vanguard ambushes with shattered claws; Maho uses the last drops of fairy dew to mask their children; Kasumi tracks the creeping perimeter lines; Kaori fights until her knuckles bleed.
- **Their Dilemma**: They cannot hold the southern woods forever. Without intervention, their people will be converted into mindless labor automatons.
- **Their Sacrificial Ending (Ending 1)**: Yuuki burns his core to anchor Maho's fairy rite, turning himself into a petrified living keystone. The sacred grove is enveloped in an eternal twilight barrier, saving Caon and their kin from assimilation, but severing them forever from the world and leaving Yuuki's body frozen in stone.

## 2. Nightmare (The Royal Guard Mutineers)
- **Members**: Jun, Christina, Tomo, Matsuri.
- **Location**: The Grand Barracks of the Royal Citadel.
- **Condition & Stakes**: Once Kaiser's personal enforcers, Nightmare broke their oaths when Kaiser issued the extermination edict for civilian defectors in Sector 4. Jun's heavy plate is splintered from fighting Kaiser's Porcelain Paladins; Christina's Number abilities are burning her muscle tissue; Tomo and Matsuri are organizing refugee evacuation routes under mortar fire.
- **Their Dilemma**: The armory and outer gate are flanked by overwhelming puppet legions. If the gate falls, ten thousand refugees will be butchered.
- **Their Sacrificial Ending (Ending 2)**: Yuuki overclocks Jun and Christina to catastrophic levels, tearing his own nervous system apart to blow the citadel portcullis. He stays behind at the collapsed archway to detonate the powder magazines with his dying mana, burying the puppet army and himself while Jun and Christina lead the refugees into freedom.

## 3. Labyrinth (The Digital Subversives)
- **Members**: Labyrista (Akira), Shizuru, Rino.
- **Location**: The Mirror Archives (Subterranean Data Rift).
- **Condition & Stakes**: Operating from the wireframe fractures beneath Landosol, Akira is fighting an asymmetrical cyber-war against Kaiser's root engine. Shizuru's sword is notched from guarding the sensory cables; Rino's quiver is down to its last enchanted bolts. Akira is bleeding from her temples, barely able to keep their pocket dimension from collapsing.
- **Their Dilemma**: Kaiser's root hounds have located their frequency. They must execute a full systemic rewrite or be scrubbed from existence.
- **Their Sacrificial Ending (Ending 3)**: Yuuki plugs himself directly into Labyrista's Object Creation terminal as a biological battery. His avatar data completely dissolves to construct an unbreachable subterranean sanctuary, preserving Labyrinth and the underground human resistance in safe isolation forever, with Shizuru holding only his shredded scarf.

## 4. Twinkle Wish (The Dawn Covenant)
- **Members**: Yui, Hiyori, Rei.
- **Location**: The Altar of the Wish (Central Plaza).
- **Condition & Stakes**: Twinkle Wish is bound to the core tragedy of Astraea. Yui's original, innocent wish for a world where everyone could smile was hijacked and inverted by Kaiser to create this cradle of numbness. Yui is in deep spiritual torment; Hiyori and Rei fight side by side through waves of shadow-tendrils to protect her.
- **Their Dilemma**: The altar is erupting with corrupted miasma. Unless the corrupted wish is absorbed, it will detonate, lobotomizing the entire central population.
- **Their Sacrificial Ending (Ending 4)**: Yuuki steps onto the altar and forcefully inhales the entire miasma into his own spiritual vessel. His Princess Knight heart purifies the wish into golden starlight, clearing the sky over the city center, but destroying his internal organs. He dies peacefully in the arms of Yui, Hiyori, and Rei, who vow to carry his light forward.

## 5. Gourmet Guild (The Hearth and Family)
- **Members**: Pecorine (Eustiana von Astraea), Kokkoro, Karyl.
- **Location**: The Sealed Vault beneath the Royal Throne.
- **Condition & Stakes**: Yuuki's closest family. Pecorine is emaciated and her Royal Equipment is running on fumes; Kokkoro tenderly rations their last crusts of bread and medicinal herbs; Karyl suffers from severe neural migraines caused by Kaiser's lingering control collar.
- **Their Dilemma**: They are poised right outside the throne room, battered and half-starved. Marching in together means taking horrific casualties.
- **Their Sacrificial Ending (Ending 5)**: The four storm the throne room together. In the chaotic final strike, Kaiser hurls a fatal neural lance at Pecorine. Yuuki intercepts the blow with his own chest, using his dying breath to drive his blade through Kaiser's forehead. The crown shatters, the tyranny ends, but Yuuki expires in Pecorine and Kokkoro's arms. The surviving girls return to a free Landosol, but their guild table forever holds an empty fourth chair.
