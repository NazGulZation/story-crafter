# Setting - Holly's Rental in Kenton Falls

Second-floor two-bed over a laundromat. Late July heat wave.

- **Living room:** Cracked faux-leather couch, coffee table with ring stains, 43-inch TV with dying remote, window AC that rattles on high, box fan in corner pushing warm air.
- **Sensory palette:** Buttered popcorn salt, cold black tea with lemon, detergent steam rising from the laundromat vent below, old leather warmed by sun, Tessa's coconut sunscreen and gym-bag citrus wipes.
- **Kitchen:** Narrow galley, iced tea pitcher, Holly's labeled leftover pasta.
- **Bathroom:** Small shower with thin water pressure, squeaking faucet, clean towels stacked on toilet tank.
- **Spare room:** Caleb's temp room for the week — air mattress plus folded futon, his duffel and toiletry bag on dresser, phone charger, spare sheet.

Rules: Front deadbolt sticks. Must lift handle to catch. TV stays on mute with captions during explicit scenes for background buzz.
