# Chapter 2B: Sleepy Embers

Golden sparks drifted lazily from the hearth pit, swirling upward into the shadowed hollow of the elder pine before fading into fragrant gray smoke. I stepped across the flagstones to where Hatsune had bundled herself among the fragrant cedar boughs and dry woven mats. She lay on her side beneath the thick wool blanket, curled into a tight, trembling crescent. Her bubblegum-pink hair, still damp at the tips from the mountain deluge, fanned across the cedar needles like crushed flower petals, catching the flickering amber light of the embers.

As my shadow fell across her, one pointed elf ear twitched beneath her locks. She peeked open a single hazel eye, blinking heavily against the warm firelight. Her pale face was flushed with the sudden return of blood, but her small nose remained adorably pink, and her toes were tucked tightly into the hem of her blanket to escape the draft whistling through the root curtains.

"Yuuki-kun..." she whispered, her voice a soft, sleepy murmur that ended in a tiny shudder. She lifted an edge of the coarse wool blanket with two fingers, shivering as the cold mountain air briefly rushed over her bare collarbones. "It's so cold on this side of the mat... My feet feel like two blocks of river ice. Come here... please? Share the blanket before my toes fall off completely."

I smiled, dropping to my knees beside her. "You shouldn't curl up so tight, Hatsune. It cramps your muscles and makes your circulation worse."

"Easy for you to say, Mr. Princess Knight," she pouted, though her lips curved into a weak, fond smile. She wriggled backward against the cedar wall, making room on the woven pallet. "You have all that tough knight muscle and steady stamina to keep you warm. I'm just a delicate psychic girl whose batteries are running completely on empty! Hurry, get under here before all the warm air escapes into the rafters!"

I pulled back the heavy wool and slid beneath the blanket beside her, settling my back against the smooth, curved wood of the ancient trunk. The moment the blanket draped over my shoulders, Hatsune lunged forward with an eager, breathless whimper, burrowing her entire upper body directly against my side.

Her thin cotton travel shirt was barely damp now, offering almost no barrier between us. Through the soft fabric, I could feel the delicate curve of her ribs, the warmth of her stomach, and the quick, erratic thumping of her heartbeat. She threw one slender leg over my shins, tucking her freezing toes directly beneath the crook of my calf with an unapologetic groan of absolute bliss.

"Ahhh... warm..." she sighed, burying her face into the curve of my neck. Her forehead was hot against my skin, but her small hands were still like polished river pebbles as she flattened both palms against my bare ribs. "Don't move, Yuuki-kun. You're an official guild heating stone now. Guild orders from your senior adventurer. Any sudden movements will be penalized by heavy snuggling."

I laughed softly, though the breath caught in my throat as her soft, perky chest pressed firmly against my arm. Every time she breathed, the thin cotton shifted against my skin, sending a jolt of static-like heat dancing across my nerves. I reached around her waist, pulling the heavy woolen blanket securely over her shoulders to trap our shared heat. Her frame was deceptively light, her bones fine like a songbird's, yet packed with an astonishing reservoir of psychic power that had turned the tide of battle more times than I could count.

"Is that better?" I asked softly, my hand resting gently on the small of her back.

"Mmm... so much better," she mumbled. Her long elven ears twitched against my cheek, soft and velvety to the touch. A sudden, involuntary spark of crackling azure light snapped at the tips of her fingers, making the blanket pop with static electricity. Across the floor, a small pinecone and an empty iron canteen twitched and rolled three inches toward us across the flagstones before settling back down.

She flinched, burying her face deeper into my shoulder with a muffled groan of embarrassment.

"Ah... sorry," she whispered, her cheeks burning hot against my throat. "Whenever I get too relaxed... or when my heart starts beating too fast... my telekinesis leaks out through my fingers. It's so embarrassing! Back in town, I have to try so hard to keep everything normal. Everyone expects me to be bright and bubbly and full of boundless energy all the time. But when it rains like this, and I'm stuck shivering in the dark... I feel so small. Like all my tricks are just parlor games."

I looked down at her. Her eyelashes fluttered against her flushed cheeks, thick and dark, casting delicate shadows in the firelight. Her lower lip was slightly parted, glossy and pink, releasing steady puffs of sweet, mint-scented breath against my skin. There was an unguarded innocence to Hatsune that had always drawn people to her, but seeing her like this—exhausted, vulnerable, and clinging to me in the amber glow of the shelter—stirred a protective, aching tenderness in my chest that went far deeper than simple friendship.

"You don't have to put on a show for me, Hatsune," I said quietly, raising my free hand to gently push a strand of damp pink hair behind her ear. Her pointed ear twitched at the contact, flushing a vivid crimson all the way to its sharp tip. "You carry a tremendous amount of weight on your shoulders. Protecting Shiori, watching over Forestier, exhausting your mana every time we fight... It makes sense that you need to rest. You don't have to be strong every single minute of the day."

Her hazel eyes opened wide, gazing up into mine with a sudden, breathless intensity. The playful teasing had vanished from her expression, replaced by a quiet, trembling wonder. Her fingers curled into the skin of my side, holding on as if she were afraid I might vanish into the smoke if she let go for an instant.

"Yuuki-kun..." she breathed, her voice dropping to a fragile hush that barely carried above the roar of the rain. "You always say the sweetest things without even realizing it. Do you know how dangerous that is? A girl could really lose her head over a knight like you. Especially a girl who spends half her life daydreaming about someone holding her hands when she wakes up."

She shifted her hips slightly, pressing herself closer until there was no space left between us beneath the blanket. The radiant heat building between our bodies was no longer just the lingering embers of the hearth; it was an electric, rising fever that made my skin prickle with anticipation. Her chest rose and fell in quick, shallow rhythms, her firm breasts flattening softly against my ribs with every inhalation. Beneath my palm, the dip of her spine arched subtly, drawing her waist into alignment with my hip.

"Hatsune..." I murmured, my hand smoothing down the curve of her back, feeling the delicate ridges of her vertebrae through the cotton.

"I mean it," she whispered, tilting her chin up. Her lips hovered barely an inch beneath mine, parted and trembling with a nervous, electric energy. A faint azure halo flickered around the crown of her head, making her pink hair float like thistle silk in an invisible, gravity-defying breeze. Above us, three dry cedar sprigs suspended from the rafters began to spin slowly in mid-air, caught in the orbit of her rising emotion. "Whenever you're around, my chest feels all tight and floaty at the same time. Like I'm about to take off into the sky, but I want to stay right here on the ground with you. Even when I'm half-asleep... I keep dreaming about your hands touching me. Holding me close so I don't float away into the clouds."

She lifted one of her small, warming hands from my ribs and pressed her palm against the center of my bare chest, right over my thumping heart. Her fingers were no longer cold; they radiated a tingling, resonant heat that seemed to seep directly into my bloodstream, answering the Princess Knight mana that pulsed beneath my collarbones.

"Feel that?" she whispered, her eyes half-lidded with an intoxicating mix of heavy drowsiness and sweet, rising arousal. "My heart is racing so fast it might bounce right out of my chest. And it's not from the cold anymore, Yuuki-kun. Not at all. It's because you're looking at me like I'm the only girl in the whole forest."

Her small toes twitched against my calf, warm and relaxed now, hooking over my ankle to keep me pinned to her side. A loose bronze buckle on my discarded belt clicked softly against the stone floor, nudged by a faint ripple of her restless telekinetic aura. Outside, the deluge drummed against the thick pine bark with relentless force, rattling the root curtains, but inside the cedar boughs, the air was warm, dense, and fragrant with the sweet scent of rain, drying needles, and her flushed skin.

Hatsune rested her forehead against my chin, her breath coming in soft, hitching sighs as her fingers curled gently into the hair at the nape of my neck.
