## 1. Yuuki (The Protagonist)
- **Role**: Protagonist / Princess Knight (First-Person POV)
- **Race**: Human
- **Appearance**: Dark short hair, observant dark eyes, lean athletic swordsman build suited for swift footwork, lightly bruised forearms from guiding his companions through the heavy storm timber. Wears simple traveling linen and leather, stripped down to bare chest and wool wraps at the hearth.
- **Canonical Abilities & Dynamics**:
  - Princess Knight power bestowed by Ames, resonating with and strengthening his companions.
  - Selfless, courageous, and deeply protective—instinctively stepping between danger and his comrades.
  - Emotionally attentive, patient, and non-judgmental, accepting each girl's quirks, vulnerabilities, and secret desires without hesitation.
  - Possesses immense physical stamina and restorative endurance.

## 4. Aoi (Futaba Aoi / アオイ)
- **Role**: Forestier Member / Elven Archer & Botanist
- **Race**: Elf
- **Appearance**:
  - **Hair**: Short, soft sage-green hair with a long side-swept fringe concealing her right eye.
  - **Eyes**: Large, expressive deep emerald-green eyes full of innocence and suppressed longing.
  - **Physiology**: Slender, athletic archer's build; smooth, finely toned bare arms with sensitive, pale underarm hollows; modest, firm bust; slender waist and sensitive flanks. Long pointed elf ears that burn bright scarlet when embarrassed.
  - **Attire**: Forest-green scout tunic with sleeves rolled up for archery, dark fitted leggings; inside the shelter, she removes her soaked tunic, remaining in a thin cotton under-camisole or bare-shouldered wraps.
- **Speech Style & Demeanor**:
  - Timid, soft-spoken, and hesitant. Often stammers or speaks in overly polite, formal phrases when flustered.
  - Reflexively carries a small carved wooden doll nicknamed **"Companion-kun"** (*Tomodachi-kun*) as an emotional anchor when facing social anxiety.
- **Personality & Canon Lore**:
  - Spent years in solitary forest training, struggling deeply to make friends and convinced she is awkward and unlovable.
  - Joining Forestier and meeting Yuuki transformed her world. She harbors an overwhelming, reverent crush on Yuuki, viewing him as her safe harbor.
  - Her smooth archer's arms and underarms are intensely ticklish and hypersensitive to touch, sweat, and breath.
