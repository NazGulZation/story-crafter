# Ending 9: The Vanguard's Feast

The brilliant morning sun broke over the high eastern gables of the Caon guildhall, bathing the courtyard in vivid amber light.

In the kitchen, the massive iron skillet roared over the woodstove with a deafening sizzle. Thick strips of salted wild boar belly browned in their own rendered lard, curling at the edges and releasing a rich, mouthwatering aroma of toasted fat, wild scallions, and cracked pepper that drifted straight through the open veranda into the dojo.

Kaori stood perched barefoot on a low wooden bench by the stove, armed with a long pair of cooking chopsticks in one hand and a giant wooden rice paddle in the other.

She wore only an oversized pair of dark linen brawler shorts and an unbuttoned red-patterned Ryukyu vest that hung loose around her ribs, exposing her sun-kissed waist and the pale, distinct bite mark prominently branded into the side of her right breast. Her dark brunette hair was tied up in a high, messy bun that bounced with every energetic flick of her wrist, while her short dog tail wagged in vigorous, happy rhythm against the pantry shelving.

"Rice is ready! Meat is crispy! Sun is shining!" Kaori cheered, scooping a mountain of steaming white grains into three oversized ceramic bowls. "Wake up, Captain! The breakfast buffet is officially open!"

At the low cedar table beside the garden window, Makoto groaned loudly.

She sat slouched across the tatami, her bare elbows propped on the polished timber, her chin resting heavily in her palms. She had pulled on a pair of loose black training trousers and a faded grey tank top, but her limbs were visibly heavy with contented fatigue. Across her broad, muscular shoulders, several deep scratches and dark purple bruises stood out in vivid relief against her sun-browned skin. Her triangular violet wolf ears twitched lazily in the morning draft, one ear folded down in comfortable exhaustion.

"Keep it down, Kaori," Makoto grunted, though her golden eyes locked onto the sizzling skillet with ravenous, predatory hunger. "My skull feels like someone took a war hammer to it. How the hell do you have this much energy after what happened on the mats?"

"Because moving around is how you shake off the stiffness!" Kaori laughed, spinning around with a heavy iron platter piled high with sizzling pork strips and placing it in the center of the table. "If you sit there groaning like an old grandma, your muscles lock up! Right, Yuuki?"

Kaori dropped onto the tatami beside me, throwing her warm, bare arm around my shoulder with effortless, sunny affection.

Her skin still carried the faint, sweet scent of jasmine bath salts mingled with the rich, grounded aroma of rendered lard and morning sweat. When she leaned in close, pressing her cheek against my temple, her breast pressed firm and warm against my ribs through the thin vest. Her ruby eyes sparkled with uncomplicated, radiant happiness as she looked at my face.

"Look at our Princess Knight," she purred, tapping my jaw playfully with the tip of her forefinger. "Not a single scratch on his face, but his shoulders are practically painted purple! You really gave us the full workout last night, Yuuki. My thighs are still shaking like dry reeds!"

Makoto choked on her tea, slamming her cup onto the timber with a sharp clatter. Her cheeks flared into an immediate, incandescent blush that spread all the way to the tips of her violet ears.

"Kaori! What did I say about your mouth?!" Makoto barked, glaring across the table with bared fangs. "Do you have to shout our private business across the entire district?! Kasumi and Maho are probably listening from the second floor right now!"

"Let them listen!" Kaori replied with a breezy, carefree grin, flashing her canine teeth. "We're the vanguard, aren't we? It's our job to test the defenses! And besides... Maho already knows. I saw her looking out her bedroom window twenty minutes ago, smiling like she just won a chest of gold coins."

Makoto’s ears flattened back in mortified defeat. She buried her face in her hands, letting out a long, muffled groan that sounded half-growl, half-whimper.

"I'm never going to hear the end of this," the wolf muttered into her palms. "She's going to make me write formal reports on vanguard stamina. I know it."

"Nankurunaisa, Captain!" Kaori laughed, shoving a massive bowl of rice and a pair of chopsticks into Makoto’s hands. "Eat first, worry later! You can't fight on an empty belly, and you definitely can't spar with Yuuki later if you're starving!"

Makoto peeked through her fingers at the steaming bowl.

The fragrant mountain of rice was topped with four thick, crispy slices of wild boar, two fried eggs with runny golden yolks, and a generous heap of chopped scallions glistening with chili oil. Her stomach let out a ferocious, vibrating roar that rattled the teacups on the tray, breaking the last of her grumpy defenses.

"Fine," Makoto muttered, snatching the chopsticks from Kaori’s hand with a gruff scowl. "Itadakimasu!"

She attacked the food with the raw, untamed ferocity of an alpha wolf after a long winter hunt. She shoveled enormous mouthfuls of rice and pork into her mouth, chewing with furious gusto, her eyes closing in pure, unadulterated bliss as the savory fat and savory seasoning exploded across her tongue.

Kaori giggled, picking up her own bowl and mirroring Makoto’s pace, her short tail wagging with continuous, contented thuds against the floorboards.

I picked up my chopsticks and joined them.

The pork was rich, smoky, and perfectly crisp, seasoned with coarse sea salt and crushed mountain berries that cut through the heaviness of the lard. The rice was hot and fluffy, soaking up the savory drippings from the meat. Between the two athletic girls, the massive platter of boar belly disappeared in a matter of minutes, accompanied by contented hums, loud crunches, and the clatter of porcelain bowls.

Makoto swallowed her final mouthful, set her empty bowl down on the tray with a heavy, satisfied thud, and let out a long, deep breath that fluttered the stray violet bangs clinging to her forehead.

She leaned back against the wooden support pillar, resting her hands over her stomach. Her golden eyes were warm, clear, and relaxed, stripped of all the defensive tension that usually guarded her posture. Her violet wolf tail draped over the edge of the mat, swishing slowly in the morning breeze.

"Damn," she breathed softly, a small, genuine smile curving her lips as she looked sideways at me. "That was good. Really good."

Kaori set her empty bowl down beside Makoto’s, wiped her mouth with the back of her wrist, and leaned heavily against my other side, resting her chin on my shoulder.

"See?" Kaori said softly, her ruby eyes softening with tender, quiet devotion as her fingers found mine beneath the table. "Food tastes a hundred times better when you eat it with the people you fought beside. The three of us... we make a pretty unstoppable team, don't we?"

Makoto looked at our joined hands, then up at Kaori’s sunny, open face. The customary blush crept back across the bridge of her nose, but this time, the wolf didn't pull away or grumble.

Instead, Makoto reached across the space between us, her calloused, scarred fingers closing firmly around my other hand. Her grip was strong, warm, and steady—the unshakeable grip of a sworn protector who had entrusted her heart and body to her champion.

"Yeah," Makoto murmured, her voice dropping into a quiet, honest rasp that vibrated in her chest. Her amber eyes held mine with fierce, unyielding loyalty. "We do. Just... don't expect me to go easy on you during the afternoon drills, Yuuki. You proved you can handle two canines at once, so I'm doubling your weight training starting today."

"Uwah! Doubling it?!" Kaori cried out, her ahoge cowlicks springing straight up in cheerful horror. "Have some mercy, Captain! Let the poor guy digest his breakfast first!"

"No mercy in the dojo, mutt!" Makoto smirked, her sharp canine fangs flashing in the morning sun. "That's the law of the pack!"

The kitchen filled with Kaori’s ringing laughter, followed by Makoto’s hearty, infectious chuckle.

The three of us sat together on the sunlit mats, our shoulders touching, the warmth of the morning air washing over our skin. Outside in the courtyard, the bamboo leaves whispered in the gentle breeze, while the wooden training posts in the yard stood tall and patient, waiting for the clatter of practice swords to begin anew.

I squeezed both of their hands, felt the firm, answering pressure from wolf and hound alike, and let the quiet strength of the pack settle into my bones.

Makoto picked up the empty teapot, stood up from the mats with an effortless flex of her calves, and stepped toward the courtyard well to draw fresh water for the morning tea.
