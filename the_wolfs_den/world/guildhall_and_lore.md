# Setting & Lore: The Caon Guildhall

## 1. Geographical & Architectural Overview

The **Caon Guildhall** is a sprawling, traditional estate nestled in the tranquil western residential district of Landosol. Originally purchased by Maho's affluent merchant family when she relocated to the capital, the residence doubles as the guild headquarters and private sanctuary for the beastfolk vigilante company.

### Architectural Layout:
1. **The Ground Floor (Brawling, Chores & Sustenance)**:
   - **Main Dojo / Training Hall**: Polished cedar timber floorboards overlaid with woven rush straw mats (*tatami*). Racks of blunt oak practice swords (*bokken*), quarterstaffs, weighted iron kettlebells, and leather striking posts line the perimeter. Smells of cedar resin, straw, dried sweat, and camphor liniment.
   - **Tavern-Style Galley Kitchen**: Adjoining the common dining hall, fitted with a broad slate hearth, cast-iron kettles, heavy butcher blocks, and rows of hanging copper pans. Makoto frequently commandeers this kitchen to prepare hearty beastfolk meals.
   - **The Communal Bathhouse**: Located in the western wing, paved with river cobblestones and centered around an oversized sunken cedar soaking tub fed by natural groundwater heated via an underfloor ceramic furnace. Smells of steam, pine wood, lavender bath salts, and cool mineral stone.
   - **The Archive & Deductive Nook**: Kasumi's dedicated workspace tucked beside the rear veranda. Crowded with wooden pigeonholes, incident ledgers, magnifying glasses, and dried ink stones.
2. **The Upper Level (Private Sanctuaries)**:
   - **Maho's Fairy-Tale Suite**: A spacious tatami bedchamber draped in silk quilts, velvet pillows, and an army of stuffed plushies (Arthur the rabbit, Lancelot the penguin, Gawain the puppy). Smells of dried chamomile, sweet incense, and polished cherry wood.
   - **Individual Quarters & Guest Futon Alcoves**: Partitioned by sliding paper *shoji* and sturdy wooden runners, offering personal retreat while allowing ambient sounds of the household to softly carry through the quiet hallways.

## 2. Sensory Grounding & Atmosphere

- **Acoustic Texture**: The rhythmic thud of bare feet on tatami, the dull clack of wooden sparring blades, the whistle of a boiling tea kettle, the rustle of sliding shoji screens, the cicadas winding down in the bamboo grove outside, and the sudden heavy intake of breath after an exhausting drill.
- **Scent Profile**: Clean mountain spring water, cedar shavings, salty skin, damp cotton dogi, charred scallions in sesame oil, and the natural animal musk of beastfolk companions after physical exertion.
- **Thermal & Tactile**: Sweltering late-afternoon sun fading into a cool evening breeze; skin hot and prickling under soaked linen; cool river stones under bare arches; the weight of bruised muscles settling into quiet ache.

---

## 3. The Daily Guild Dynamic

Unlike high-society knightly orders or academic mage circles, Caon operates with the close-knit, rowdy familiarity of a martial family:
- **Morning & Midday**: Active patrol routes across Landosol's markets and alleyways, intervening in minor scuffles, assisting beastfolk laborers, and hunting down dangerous wild beasts.
- **Late Afternoon**: Rigorous group conditioning—hundreds of sword strikes, calisthenics, sparring tournaments, and martial drills led by Makoto and Kaori.
- **Nightfall**: The collective shedding of armor, shared meals, banter, and well-earned rest. It is within this unguarded nocturnal window that physical proximity, lingering adrenaline, and deep emotional bonds naturally dissolve social restraint.
