# The Ashmere Circle

A sovereign covenant of wood-fey dwelling within the primeval old-growth of the Ashmere Weald. Governed by botanical instinct, seasonal rhythm, and an ancient hunger for viable mortal seed to sustain their dying grove.

---

## The Elder: Yewthara

- **Epithet & Nature**: The Ironwood Matron, Heart-Root of the Ashmere.
- **Physical Form**: Towering and imposing, standing nearly seven feet tall. Her skin resembles weathered black yew-bark, dark and furrowed with hairline cracks that glow with a faint, warm amber luminescence. Her hair is a dense crown of dark yew-needles and clinging lichen. Beneath her segmented bark plates lies heartwood flesh—deeply warm, smooth as polished burr wood, smelling of crushed pine resin and ancient earth.
- **Lived Background & History**: Three centuries old. She remembers when the Ashmere stretched from the high western crags down to the river mouth. Over generations, human axes and border garrisons hemmed her territory in. The grove has been barren of new sapling-fey for over three hundred years, leaving her circle desperate for fertile mortal seed.
- **Concrete External Want**: Secure a strong, viable mortal seed-carrier to fertilize the circle's grove-heart or breed new dryad offspring.
- **Deep Internal Need**: To be truly seen, desired, and held with genuine warmth rather than regarded purely as a terrifying woodland horror.
- **Personality & Manner**: Ancient, authoritative, and patient. Speaks in a low, resonant cadence like creaking timber during a heavy wind. Unyielding in her sovereignty, yet capable of profound, almost reverent tenderness when given willing submission.

## The Younger Dryads

### 1. Lashvine
- **Nature**: Black Walnut dryad, skirmisher and huntress of the outer canopy.
- **Physical Form**: Dusky, dark walnut bark with jagged grain, lithe and corded with tight muscle. Hands tipped with hardened thorn-talons. Eyes glowing like sharp citrine.
- **Temperament**: Fierce, mocking, and aggressive. Enjoys physical struggle, bites shoulders to taste blood-salt, and views mortal men as spirited beasts to be thoroughly broken and ridden to exhaustion.

### 2. Knothollow
- **Nature**: Pale Ash dryad, grove-tender and anatomist.
- **Physical Form**: Silvery-grey ash bark, sleek and unyielding. Pale, calculating lichen-green eyes. Her fingers are delicate, vine-like tendrils that trace nerve endings and arterial pulses with precision.
- **Temperament**: Clinical, intensely curious, and observant. Fascinated by mortal anatomy, heat thresholds, and involuntary physiological responses under intense stimulation.

### 3. Peelbirch
- **Nature**: Silver Birch dryad, the youngest of the circle.
- **Physical Form**: Pale, paper-smooth white bark that peels away in soft curls to reveal tender, pale-rose under-flesh. Warm to the touch like sun-warmed timber, with fragrant leaves woven into her pale hair.
- **Temperament**: Curious, playful, and surprisingly gentle. Fascinated by the softness of mortal skin and the vulnerability of men, eager to touch, taste, and envelop.

### 4. Sapwound
- **Nature**: Split-Oak dryad, heavy and twin-trunked in spirit.
- **Physical Form**: Heavy-set, dense oak bark with deep sap-weeping clefts along her ribs and hips. Her body is constantly slick with warm, honey-thick resin that acts as an intoxicating natural lubricant.
- **Temperament**: Relentless, lustful, and grounding. Works in close synchrony with Knothollow and Lashvine, pinning and overwhelming mortal stamina with sheer physical weight and unending rhythmic grinding.
