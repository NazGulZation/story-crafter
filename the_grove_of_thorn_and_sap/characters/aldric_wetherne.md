# Aldric Wetherne

- **Full Name & Title**: Aldric Wetherne, sworn border-rider of the Thornwall Garrison.
- **Physical Description**: Twenty-two summers old, broad across the shoulders from five years of arming doublet and ring-mail, with lean corded arms and hands calloused from broadsword hilt and saddle-leather. Dark hair cut short to fit beneath a steel kettle-hat, grey eyes, and a sharp jaw marked by a pale nick from a training foil above the left cheekbone.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Third son of a minor landed freeholder near the southern chalk hills. With no title, no herd, and no inheritance awaiting him, his father bought him a coarse wool gambeson, a second-hand arming blade, and a commission to the border marches. Three weeks into his garrison posting at Thornwall, he remains eager to prove he is more than an unlanded spare mouth, yet unaccustomed to the deep, primordial isolation of the ancient border wealds.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Complete his patrol perimeter, defend his honor, and return safely to the barracks fire.
  - *Internal Need*: To be desired and valued for his own flesh and presence, rather than treated as an expendable third son discarded on a quiet border.
- **Core Wound / Misbelief**: Believes duty and obedience are the only currencies that purchase a man worth, and that yielding control is synonymous with total ruin.
- **Fatal Blind Spot / Genuine Flaw**: Stubborn pride that borders on reckless defiance when cornered, paired with a hidden, simmering curiosity for ancient fey lore that slows his blade when he ought to retreat immediately.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: Sensory overwhelm under direct botanical intoxication and overwhelming physical touch; having his martial composure systematically stripped away by unyielding fey hands.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Rolling his sword-wrist to pop the joint when tense; digging the blunt edge of his thumb into his palm when attempting to steady his breath.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Plain, direct border-soldier register. Short, declarative sentences under stress, giving way to strained, ragged honesty when his composure breaks.
- **Internal Contradiction**: A disciplined knight bound by martial oaths who harbors an intense, latent hunger for wild, untamed physical intimacy beyond the cold rigidities of castle life.
