# Character Dossiers: The Iron Order of Karst

---

### 1. Supreme Commander Viktor Vogel (Host: Lorenz Falk)

- **Original Identity**: *Standartenführer* Viktor Vogel, 3rd Security Directorate, *Ordensstaat Burgund*. Mortally wounded in Ost-Paris by partisan gunfire after issuing a total sector clearance directive.
- **Host Body**: Lorenz Falk, twenty-one winters. Scrawny, frost-bitten, chronic pleurisy, callused hands scarred from sharpening weapons and mending boots for his ungrateful companions.
- **Core Philosophy & Personality**:
  - **Pragmatism Over Cruelty**: Viktor views gratuitous cruelty, sadism, and arbitrary beatings as the hallmark of an amateurish, decadent feudal warlord. Cruelty degrades muscle tissue, breeds typhus, wastes quicklime, and incites sabotage. A dead worker yields zero metric tons of coal.
  - **The Lesson of Antiquity: Bread and Circus (*Panem et Circenses*)**: Viktor learned from his historical studies of imperial antiquity that repression alone is an unstable pressure cooker. *Resentment breeds the blade.* When men are subjected to relentless, joyless toil, resentment festers in the dark until an assassin strikes. To govern millions perpetually, the state must monopolize both **Food and Entertainment**: guaranteed high-calorie bread and beer (*Panem*), paired with grand, thrilling public spectacles (*Circenses*)—arena derbies, athletic competitions, brass band marches, and theatrical civic trials.
  - **The Anti-Magic Ballistic Doctrine (The Military-Industrial Complex)**: Confronted by a realm of elven barrier-weavers, combat sorcerers, and magical beasts against which medieval steel is mathematically futile, Viktor deploys the total war machine: mass-produced bolt-action rifles, water-cooled machine guns, heavy artillery, tracked *Sturmwagen* tanks, and Close Air Support (CAS). Magic is treated as a finite metabolic energy system to be overwhelmed and ruptured through kinetic joule saturation, concussive hydrostatic shock, and precision aerial bombing.
  - **The Carrot-and-Stick Doctrine**: He rewards competence, honesty, and hard labor generously: high-calorie diets (2,400–3,500 kcal), clean warm barracks, merit-based promotions, real healthcare, cheap state-brewed beer (*Volksbier*), and performance bonuses in *Arbeitschein*. Under his rule, an obedient commoner eats better, lives safer, and is more thoroughly entertained than under any feudal lord.
  - **The Unforgivable Sin (Deliberate Treason & Conscious Betrayal)**: While ignorance, biological weakness, or honest errors can be trained and reformed through discipline, **conscious, deliberate betrayal of trust, breach of duty, and intentional disruption of public order** are treated as fatal social cancers. Those who consciously exploit and discard those who sacrificed for them—such as the Silver Dawn party—are broken with unbending, mathematical severity.
- **Tactile Tic / Mannerism**: Habitually glances at his bare wrist for a mechanical chronometer that does not exist; double-taps his index finger twice against his coat collar seam when calculating logistical quotas; maintains immaculate, posture-perfect spine alignment regardless of mud or sleet.
- **Dialogue Cadence**:
  - *Tone*: Monotone, clipped, bureaucratic, utterly devoid of petty malice or emotional inflection.
  - *Register*: Military administration, ballistics ballistics, logistics accounting, penal statutes, and public safety regulations.
  - *Deflection*: When confronted with weeping, flirting, or boasting, he responds with balance-sheet calculations, caloric ratios, and statutory subsections.
- **Contradiction**: An ascetic zealot of supreme order who brings peace, security, industrial safety, and roaring public stadium entertainment to a bandit-plagued wasteland—entirely through cold, clockwork totalitarian efficiency, where citizens are well-fed, well-amused cogs in an invincible machine.

### 2. Marek the Gilded

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Marek of High-Crest, "The Gilded Cleaver".
- **Archetype**: The quintessential wandering fantasy "Chad" adventurer. Towering, broad-shouldered, flowing chestnut locks, polished plate armor, wielding a five-foot greatsword named *Dawn-Sunderer*.
- **The Nature of His Crime**: Not merely being strong or attractive, but **conscious, parasitic betrayal**. He deliberately took over a party built on Lorenz’s blood, extracted resources without contribution, endangered the entire team through criminal sentry negligence, and committed armed sedition against state authorities.
- **Rehabilitation & Civic Utility**: Viktor does not starve Marek to death in a dungeon. In the Arena of the Black Sun, Marek's arrogance is publicly broken in front of five thousand cheering citizens through an engineered strength demonstration against an industrial steam-crane. Reclassified as a prime draught-engine, he is fed three thousand five hundred calories of hearty bread, lard, and salt broth in the deep coal mines, hauling anthracite to the blast-furnace gasifiers powering tank armor production and loading 105mm artillery shells, earning extra butter and tobacco rations whenever he exceeds his four-ton quota.

### 3. The Estranged Party (The Harem)

#### Clara of the Weeping Spring (The Childhood Friend / Cleric)
- **Role**: Novice healer of the Dawn-Light.
- **The Conscious Betrayal**: Clara’s crime is the most personally damning to Lorenz: **calculated emotional exploitation**. She did not act out of fear; she consciously discarded the boy who starved for three years to buy her holy focus the instant a wealthier, more glamorous man arrived. She weaponizes tears and femininity to evade accountability.
- **Pragmatic Redirection**: Assigned to the Field Hospital Munitions Trauma & Chemical Burn Ward. Issued clean linen caps, carbolic soap, and proper rubber gloves. Her cellular acceleration magic is strictly regulated to stabilize assembly-line burn victims and drill-press casualties, earning her bread through honest, humble service to factory laborers.

#### Isolde the Pyromancer
- **Role**: Hedge elementalist and spell-weaver.
- **The Conscious Betrayal**: Academic arrogance and callous mockery of the man who risked his life in fever-swamps for her sulfur reagents.
- **Pragmatic Redirection**: Converted into an energetic thermal catalyst for the synthetic fuel cracking stills and cordite drying tunnels. Given safety rotations, asbestos gear, and adequate calories, her flames are harnessed to crack coal tar into high-octane aviation spirit (*Flugbenzin*).

#### Brigit the Wood-Stalker (The Ranger)
- **Role**: Scout, trapper, and skirmisher.
- **The Contrast (The Carrot at Work)**: Unlike Clara and Isolde, Brigit possesses cold, utilitarian honesty. When captured, she does not whine or invent lies; she accepts the reality of her defeat. Because she works diligently and possesses acute spatial judgment, Viktor promotes her to the Ballistics & Optical Directorate, where she calibrates periscope prisms for *Sturmwagen* tanks and dive-bomber reticles.

### 4. Key Frontier Subordinates

#### Sergeant Major Kester (Garrison Veteran of Karst-Hold)
- **Transformation**: Transformed from a drunken, hopeless deserter into a proud, immaculately disciplined senior non-commissioned officer commanding the 1st Mechanized Infantry Cohort. He is loyal to Viktor not just out of fear, but because Viktor gave him real bread, restored his self-respect, and made him the master of ceremonies for the Arena's military parades and live-fire proving grounds.

#### Director Weidmann (Civil Registrar & Secret Police Chief)
- **Role**: Former provincial notary, now chief of the *Black Cloak Security Directorate*.
- **Personality**: Detail-obsessed, finding supreme bureaucratic bliss in cataloging every citizen's caloric intake, travel passes, denunciation slips, and Arena ticket subscriptions.
