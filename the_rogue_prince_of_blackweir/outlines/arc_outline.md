# Arc Outline: The Rogue Prince of Blackweir

Ten-chapter structural outline written in the **First-Person POV of Prince Daemon Targaryen**, detailing the trajectory from bitter tavern betrayal to sovereign conquest, peaking at the Chapter 8 arena catharsis.

---

## The Master 10-Chapter Beat Sheet

### Chapter 1: The Silver Smirk
- **Scene**: Corner booth of *The Salt & Grackle* in Blackweir during an autumn gale.
- **Conflict**: Lysander kicks Caspar out of the Dawn Vanguard as dead weight; Briony and Morwenna mock his non-magical logistics; Althea callously reveals her affair with Lysander on watch nights.
- **Disaster**: Caspar’s mortal heart breaks from grief and humiliation.
- **The Turn**: The soul of Prince Daemon Targaryen reawakens from the Gods Eye, commandeering Caspar's shell. Daemon mocks Lysander's divine vanity, reclaims his maps and ledgers, and walks out into the rain.

### Chapter 2: Flea Bottom on the Chasm
- **Scene**: The rain-slick alleys of the Lower Sump beneath the Cormorant Chasm.
- **Action**: Daemon assesses his new body and liquidates party intelligence. Lysander sends three tavern brutes to break Caspar's knees and retrieve the maps.
- **Combat**: Daemon executes a masterclass in Flea Bottom infighting—dislocating joints, severing arteries, and claiming the brutes' purse and steel.

### Chapter 3: The King of the Stepstones
- **Part I (Briony POV)**: Asked by Lysander to check on the three hired brutes in Gallow-Gate Lane, Briony discovers a horrific slaughterhouse: two dead with surgical wounds, and Joram barely clinging to life with a pulverized jaw and inverted knee. Joram babbles in terror of a demon in Caspar's skin. Briony tries to warn Lysander, but is dismissed with arrogant disdain.
- **Part II (Daemon POV)**: Daemon recruits Master Korgan and crippled scout Orrin Pike with stolen coin in the Sump. He observes Lysander receiving the Great Writ at the High Guildhall, noticing Briony's pale, trembling demeanor as fear takes root.

### Chapter 4: The Rot in the Gold
- **Scene**: Freezing sleet in the Wyrm-Tooth Crags vs. the sulfur lip of the Cinder-Gorge.
- **Conflict**: Lysander's camp frays; rations rot; Morwenna's mana is depleted; Althea is humiliated by Lysander and his new noble mistress.
- **Sovereign Claim**: Daemon files a perpetual freehold writ for the Cinder-Gorge, recognizing the volcanic heat as the cradle of star-iron.

### Chapter 5: Fire and Crucible
- **Scene**: The boiling depths of the Cinder-Gorge caldera.
- **Tactical Action**: Daemon clears the chasm-scorpions using trench warfare and mineral pitch. He slays the brood-matron with a levered iron bar.
- **The Forge**: Daemon and Korgan smelt star-iron over the volcanic flues, forging the dark bastard blade *Bloodthorn*.

### Chapter 6: The Broken Vanguard
- **Scene**: The high basalt ravine of Wyrm-Tooth Crags.
- **The Rout**: Lysander activates his blinding holy aura, unaware the blind behemoth hunts by heat and acoustic resonance. The beast decimates the company; Briony’s arm is pulverized; Morwenna’s focus explodes; Lysander flees on horseback.
- **The Aftermath**: The crippled survivors return to Blackweir to discover Caspar Thorne has been granted the title of High Warden of the Gorge.

### Chapter 7: The Scraps of Althea
- **Part I (Briony POV)**: Inside their squalid, leaking garret in Blackweir, Briony lies burning with fever from her shattered arm while bailiffs hammer on the doors below. Lysander hysterically orders Althea to put on a lavender-scented gown and seduce Caspar into signing over the star-iron deed. Briony warns them that Caspar is an unsparing butcher who slaughtered three men in Gallow-Gate Lane, but Lysander kicks her pallet and Althea arrogantly dismisses her warnings.
- **Part II (Daemon POV)**: Daemon receives Althea in the Great Hearth of his volcanic citadel. He exposes Lysander's secret indenture contract pledging her body to debt-slavers, treats her with icy aristocratic contempt, and drops a single copper coin. Althea flees screaming in humiliation, driving Lysander to issue his arena duel challenge.

### Chapter 8: The Gods Eye in the Arena (The Climax / Catharsis)
- **Scene**: The Grand Amphitheater of Blackweir before Duke Aldous and 5,000 spectators.
- **The Duel**: Lysander charges with blinding divine radiance. Daemon shields his eyes with his bascinet, steps inside the blow, shatters Lysander's elbow, kicks in his chest-plate, snaps his gilded sword, and systematically pulverizes his handsome face into red pulp with iron gauntlets.
- **The Humiliation**: The Goddess remains silent; Lysander soils himself and weeps for mercy, trading away his companions. Total, unsparing catharsis.

### Chapter 9: The Banishment of the Vain
- **Scene**: The marble steps of the High Keep in the rain.
- **The Settlement**: Duke Aldous grants Daemon sovereign autonomy and tariff-free food quotas.
- **The Reckoning**: Lysander is reduced to a toothless gutter beggar. Briony and Morwenna are assigned harsh manual labor in the outer quarries. Daemon flicks Althea’s copper coin into the sewer drain, abandoning her to her choices.

### Chapter 10: The Sovereign of the Ash
- **Scene**: Battlements of the Citadel of the Red Kilns six months later.
- **The Domain**: A booming industrial redoubt of star-iron plate and disciplined legions. Althea scrubs salt in the lye tanneries, mourning her folly.
- **The Future**: As a royal army approaches to claim the mines, Daemon unsheathes *Bloodthorn*—the Rogue Prince prepares for war.
