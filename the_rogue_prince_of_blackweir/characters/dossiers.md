# Character Dossiers: The Rogue Prince of Blackweir

Profiles and behavioral registers for the cast of *The Rogue Prince of Blackweir*.

---

## 1. Protagonist: Prince Daemon Targaryen / Caspar Thorne

![Prince Daemon Targaryen in Black Star-Iron Armor, Wielding Bloodthorn](../assets/daemon_caspar_black_plate.jpg)

- **Vessel**: Caspar Thorne, former field quartermaster of the Dawn Vanguard.
- **Transmigrated Soul**: Prince Daemon Targaryen, the Rogue Prince, former Commander of the City Watch of King's Landing and King of the Stepstones.
- **Physical Demeanor**: Lean, wiry frame hardened over volcanic anvils; hooded violet-tinged eyes; razor-sharp, languid movements with zero wasted motion.
- **Concrete Desire**: To forge an unconquerable fortress-realm in the Cinder-Gorge, build disciplined legions out of discarded men, and rule without bowing to church, crown, or petty guild oligarchs.
- **Core Conviction**: Power is not given by smiling gods or noble bloodline patents; it is seized, hammered over iron, and paid for in fire and blood.
- **Tactile Mannerism**: Resting his thumb along the pommel of *Bloodthorn*, or tapping the flat of his blade against his thigh when amused.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Low, clipped, aristocratic cadence; soft-spoken yet carrying absolute, lethal finality; dry, sardonic mockery toward arrogance.
- **Morbid Satisfaction (Soft Spot for the Dregs)**: Beneath his imperious cruelty and aristocratic arrogance, Daemon harbors a perverse affinity for those ground under the boot of society—the outcasts, the maimed, the gutter rats. He finds immense, wicked satisfaction in taking the discarded, arming them with steel and pride, and watching them ruthlessly trample the very highborn who once spat on them.

## 2. Antagonist: Lysander of House Morn
- **Epithet**: "The Blessed Son" / "The Sun-Favored".
- **Concept**: A vainglorious aristocrat granted raw divine radiance by the Luminescent Mother purely because of his angelic facial symmetry. Incapable of understanding tactics, creature biology, or logistical realities.
- **Concrete Desire**: Total public adulation, wealth, and effortless heroic glory without enduring hardship or discipline.
- **Core Misbelief**: Believes divine light and beauty elevate him above all consequences and that lesser men exist solely to reflect his magnificence.
- **Tactile Mannerism**: Preening his golden curls with ringed fingers; resting a manicured palm on his gilded broadsword hilt.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Theatrical, boastful, melodramatic; rapidly devolves into shrill, petulant whining when faced with real pain or opposition.
- **Fate**: In Chapter 8, his elbow is shattered, chest caved in, and handsome face pulverized into red pulp; reduced to a toothless, disfigured beggar in Blackweir's gutters.

---

## 3. The Turncoat: Althea Reed
- **Background**: Marksman from Briar-by-the-Fen. Childhood companion of Caspar Thorne, who financed her first recurve bow.
- **Betrayal**: Seduced by Lysander's aristocratic glamour and promises of capital nobility; callously betrayed Caspar and openly mocked his devotion.
- **Concrete Desire**: Security, aristocratic status, and freedom from peasant poverty.
- **Core Misbelief**: Believed loyalty was a sucker's game and that trading up to a handsome nobleman guaranteed safety.
- **Tactile Mannerism**: Clasping her hands to her collarbone; nervous fiddling with bowstrings.
- **Fate**: Rejected by Daemon with a single copper coin; abandoned by Lysander's bankrupt estate; spends her life scraping sheep-skins in the freezing lye-tanneries of Blackweir.

---

## 4. Former Party Members: Briony & Morwenna
- **Briony the Iron-Shouldered**:
  - *Role*: Heavy vanguard berserker.
  - *Flaw*: Blindly worshipped Lysander's aura; dismissed Caspar as a useless pack-mule.
  - *Fate*: Left arm crushed by the Gore-Horn Behemoth; granted harsh manual labor in Daemon's outer slag quarries where she hauls ore with her right arm.
- **Morwenna of the Silt**:
  - *Role*: Water and aether weaver.
  - *Flaw*: Arrogant, intellectual contempt for non-magical strategy.
  - *Fate*: Blinded in one eye by focus backblast when her depleted shields collapsed; works the sulfur distillation vats under Tobias.

---

## 5. Loyal Retainers of the Cinder-Gorge
- **Master Korgan**:
  - *Role*: Master Armorer of the Great Hearth. Disgraced dwarven smith stripped of his guild seal for refusing to forge decorative ceremonial tin for nobles.
  - *Trait*: Half-blind, scarred, master of high-temperature star-iron folding.
- **Orrin Pike**:
  - *Role*: Marshal of the Scout Cadre. Veteran legionary with a scarred throat who knows the deep gorge trails and commands heavy siege-arbalests.
- **Tobias the Bitter**:
  - *Role*: Head Apothecary and Alchemist. Brews military-grade mineral pitch, sulfur brine, and caustic distillates.
