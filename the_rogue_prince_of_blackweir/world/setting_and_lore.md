# Setting & Lore Codex: The Rogue Prince of Blackweir

Geographical, political, and material worldbuilding for the frontier marches of Blackweir.

---

## 1. Geography & Settlements

### The City of Blackweir
- **Location**: Built along the sheer limestone cliffs of the **Cormorant Chasm**, where the churning waters of the Silt River drop into the lower cataracts.
- **Architecture & Tiers**:
  - *The High Crescent*: White-sandstone terraces, guild halls, the Cathedral of the Luminescent Mother, and noble estates.
  - *The Middle Flumes*: Merchant warehouses, grain silos, and mercenary guild-lodges.
  - *The Sump*: The soot-stained subterranean lower tier along the canal gates. Choked with timber tenements, charcoal kilns, and tanneries.
- **Government**: Governed by Duke Aldous and a Council of Guild Masters who prioritize economic commerce over knightly honor.

### The Cinder-Gorge & Citadel of the Red Kilns
- **Topography**: A three-mile volcanic rift south of Blackweir. Walls of sheer black basalt and razor-sharp obsidian shale, perforated with thermal fumaroles and sulfur fissures.
- **The Red Kilns**: Fortified basalt redoubt engineered by Daemon Targaryen, tapping natural thermal chimneys to smelt high-grade meteoric iron without wood or coal.
- **Strategic Value**: Flanked by unscalable cliffs, immune to conventional siege engines, and housing the largest untapped vein of deep-earth star-iron in the province.

### The Wyrm-Tooth Crags
- **Terrain**: A jagged labyrinth of basalt needles, cold scree, and freezing rime sixty leagues north-east of Blackweir.
- **Danger**: Saturated with copper-slag runoff and acid springs; natural hunting territory of the Gore-Horn Behemoth.

## 2. Regional Fauna & Ecological Threats

### 1. Chasm-Scorpions
- **Habitat**: Lower volcanic terraces and sulfur vents of the Cinder-Gorge.
- **Anatomy**: Segmented chitinous predators measuring 8 to 12 feet long. Breathe via ventral abdominal spiracles.
- **Sensory Ecology**: Completely blind; detect vibrations in obsidian shale and thermal signatures.
- **Toxicity**: Neurotoxic amber bile secreted from barbed caudal stingers that curdle blood on contact.

### 2. The Gore-Horn Behemoth
- **Habitat**: Deep subterranean defiles of the Wyrm-Tooth Crags.
- **Anatomy**: Massive quadrupedal juggernaut covered in slate-gray hide. Head crowned by six hollow, flute-like horns.
- **Behavior**: Blind behemoth tracking prey solely through acoustic resonance and heat currents. When provoked, its hollow horns whistle a deafening frequency while spraying pressurized caustic bile.

## 3. Metallurgy & Armaments

### Star-Iron (Crucible Meteoric Ore)
- Extracted from deep hydrothermal seams within the Cinder-Gorge.
- Smelted using volcanic flue temperatures exceeding standard charcoal forges.
- Quenched in sulfur brine and mineral pitch, resulting in an oil-dark, non-reflective matte finish that resists corrosion and shatters standard guild steel.

### *Bloodthorn*
- Thirty-eight-inch double-edged bastard blade forged by Master Korgan and Daemon Targaryen.
- Folded star-iron core showing smoky dark ripples and faint crimson veins.
- Balanced three fingers above the crossguard, combining the speed of a rapier with the shearing mass of a war-sword.
