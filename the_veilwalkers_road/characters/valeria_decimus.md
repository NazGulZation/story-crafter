# Character Profile: Captain Valeria Decimus

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Captain Valeria Decimus, the Shield of Chorrol
- **Race & Demonym**: Imperial of western Cyrodiil (Colovian Highlands stock)
- **Age**: Twenty-nine winters
- **Physical Appearance**: Tall, athletic, and muscular with an imposing military bearing. Sun-bronzed olive skin weathered by Highland winds and mountain camps. Raven-black hair pulled back into a severe, tight legionnaire's braid secured with brass wire. Piercing amber-gold eyes beneath straight, stern brows; a sharp aquiline nose and chiseled jawline. An Imperial sunburst brand of Stendarr marks her left shoulderblade; faint crosshatched dueling scars run across her collarbone and upper ribs from her days in the Cyrodilic skirmish cohorts.
- **Armor & Arms**: Flawlessly maintained steel plate cuirass embossed with the Imperial crest over blackened chainmail and boiled leather chausses. Draped in a heavy crimson wool mantle fastened at the right shoulder by a bronze lion-head brooch. Wields an etched Colovian broadsword and a reinforced steel heater shield bearing the sigil of the Eighth Legion.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Born to an impoverished patrician family in Chorrol with a long lineage of military officers. Enlisted in the Imperial Legion as an auxiliary skirmisher at seventeen, earning her commission through sheer battlefield discipline and tactical tenacity during border counter-insurgencies in southern Cyrodiil. Reassigned north to Skyrim to oversee supply corridors along the Rift border, she found herself disgusted by Riften's corrupt jarls, Maven Black-Briar's syndicate tentacles, and the local guards' complicity in extortion. She views her posting as a sacred mission to restore Imperial law and order to a crumbling province.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Seize verifiable evidence of Maven Black-Briar's treasonous skooma and weapons smuggling, using Imperial martial law to arrest the syndicate leaders and secure the Rift highway for the Empire.

- **Core Misbelief**: Believes that personal desire is a fatal weakness and that steel discipline, devotion to Stendarr, and adherence to military regulations are the only barriers preventing her world from descending into chaos.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Overconfidence in her conscious mental willpower. Because she considers herself immune to base trickery, she fails to recognize how vulnerable her sensory nervous system is to subtle, pleasure-laced Illusion magic until the weave is already rooted in her blood.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: The terror of losing control; the overwhelming shame that accompanies intense, unbridled sexual arousal when her stoic military defenses are bypassed by magical euphoria.
- **Tactile Tic**: Pressing her thumb against the cold silver Stendarr amulet resting against the hollow of her throat beneath her steel gorget whenever confronted with moral hesitation.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Precise, commanding, and formal. Uses military terminology and Cyrodilic Latinate cadence; speaks with the clipped authority of an officer accustomed to unquestioned obedience, which fractures into breathless, ragged entreaties when overwhelmed by passion.
- **Internal Contradiction**: Enforces severe military discipline on her troops with an unyielding glare, yet spends hours alone in her tent privately agonizing over each casualty and quietly nursing an intense, secret hunger for intimacy that terrifies her waking mind.

Starts defensive and resistant, fighting to maintain her commanding posture, before dissolving under targeted sensory stimulation into desperate, vocal submission.
