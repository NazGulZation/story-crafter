# Character Profile: Nelvyne Sarethi

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Nelvyne Sarethi, "The Gilded Adder"
- **Race & Demonym**: Dunmer (Dark Elf) of House Sarethi / Morrowind expatriate
- **Age**: Ninety-four winters (young adulthood by Mer standards)
- **Physical Appearance**: Lean, sinewy build with angular jawline and high cheekbones. Ash-grey skin with faint indigo undertones. Vivid crimson eyes that catch candlelight like polished rubies. Coarse raven-black hair cropped sharp at the jaw to stay clear of collars and daggers. Calloused, nimble fingers stained faintly at the cuticles with alchemical testing acid and ink.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Born in the trading wards of Mournhold prior to the eruption of Red Mountain. Fled western Morrowind with her elder brother during the cataclysm, losing family holdings to Redoran martial consolidation and Argonian incursions. Rebuilt her livelihood in Skyrim through sheer cunning, operating as an independent fence, appraiser, and document broker along the Riften canals. Carries a thin, jagged burn scar across her left shoulder blade from a boiler rupture aboard an East Empire trading cog.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Secure the stolen Dwemer resonance cube from the Ratway and extract enough gold or arcane leverage to buy complete operational independence from Maven Black-Briar and the Thieves Guild.
  - *Internal Need*: Establish an unshakeable, secure home and loyal companions who will never abandon her to famine, displacement, or ash.
- **Core Misbelief**: Believes intimacy and vulnerability are financial liabilities; anyone without a contract or shared skin in the game will sell you out at the first drop of blood.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Arrogant reliance on cynical calculation; underestimates people whose loyalty is rooted in affection or shared grief rather than gold.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: Memories of her family suffocating under falling ash in Morrowind; deep terror of confinement in lightless, stagnant places.
- **Tactile Tic**: Rolls a smooth, oval garnet cabochon between the thumb and forefinger of her right hand when evaluating the terms of a deal or estimating risk.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Razor-sharp, sardonic, and rhythmic. Speaks with the clipped, haughty cadence of old Dunmeri merchant houses, laced with cynical gutter slang picked up in Riften's underworld.
- **Internal Contradiction**: Ruthless in her pricing and quick to draw a concealed glass dagger, yet pays the medicine tab for two sickly Dunmer weaver children living in Riften's Beggar's Row without ever taking credit.
- **Sexual Dynamic**: Provocative, verbal, and demanding. Initiates through sharp wit, mock disdain, and lingering physical inspection. Loves being observed, directing positions, and hearing her partner lose control. Cool-skinned with scalding inner heat; vocal during release, crying out in guttural Dunmeri dialects.
