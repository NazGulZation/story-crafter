# Character Profile: Sigrid Iron-Braid

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Sigrid Iron-Braid, formerly of the Windhelm Vanguard
- **Race & Demonym**: Nord of eastern Skyrim (Falkreath border descent)
- **Age**: Twenty-eight winters
- **Physical Appearance**: Tall, powerfully built, broad-shouldered with thick thighs and sculpted arms honed by a decade of mail and steel. Fair, sun-freckled skin that runs furnace-warm to the touch. Heavy mane of honey-blonde hair gathered into intricate warrior's braids pinned tight to her scalp. Grey-blue eyes under straight brows. A four-inch pale, puckered scar curves across her right collarbone where a barbed Reachman arrow gouged through boiled leather.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Enlisted as a young skirmisher in Ulfric Stormcloak's host during the civil unrest. Marched with the vanguard into Markarth in 4E 176 to crush the Reachman uprising. Witnessed the indiscriminate slaughter of unarmed Reach civilians, elders, and shopkeepers in the market squares after the gates fell. Broken by the horror, she threw her company badge into the Karth River at night, deserted east across the mountain ridges, and surfaced in the Rift as a hired sellsword and enforcer. Nelvyne hired her to break a debtor's knee three winters ago; they have shared a hearth, bed, and ledger ever since.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Amass three hundred gold septims to buy passage on an ocean caravel out of Dawnstar to Hammerfell or Cyrodiil, leaving Skyrim's endless factional bloodshed behind.
  - *Internal Need*: Absolution for the slaughter she witnessed at Markarth; finding an anchor strong enough to convince her of her humanity beyond combat and steel.
- **Core Misbelief**: Convinced her soul is permanently stained by the blood of Markarth; believes she is only good for guarding thresholds and cracking skulls.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Suppressed rage; when cornered or reminded of military commanders betraying their sworn men, she risks tactical suicide in favor of blunt physical violence.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: The sight of wounded youths or weeping non-combatants; loud metallic crashes that echo the battering of Markarth's bronze gates.
- **Tactile Tic**: Methodically adjusts the waxed calfskin wrapping around the leather grip of her arming sword with her thumb whenever she feels tension rising in a room.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Low, gravelly, and unhurried. Uses few words, preferring direct nouns and active verbs. Stripped of boastful Nord swagger; speaks like a veteran who knows what steel does to flesh.
- **Internal Contradiction**: Capable of snapping an adversary's forearm over her knee without blinking, yet tenderly combs Nelvyne's hair after nightmares and nurses stray, mangy hunting dogs behind the stable stalls.
- **Sexual Dynamic**: Grounded, athletic, and fiercely protective. Prefers heavy, deliberate physical contact—wrapping muscular legs and arms around her partner, pulling their weight firmly down against her pelvis. Quiet during foreplay, breathing through her nose in steady, furnace-hot gusts; holds with bruising strength at climax, shuddering from deep in her core while maintaining intense, unbroken eye contact.
