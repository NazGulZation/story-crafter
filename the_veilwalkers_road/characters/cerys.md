# Character Profile: Cerys of the Red Redoubt

- **Full Name & Epithet**: Cerys of the Red Redoubt, "The Briar-Bough"
- **Race & Demonym**: Reachwoman (Breton-Nedic ancestral bloodline native to the Reach)
- **Age**: Twenty-four winters
- **Physical Appearance**: Slender, lean, and whipcord-tough with long, wiry dark brown hair braided with bone beads and dried sprigs of wild juniper. Olive skin patterned with dark indigo ritual woad tattoos that swirl from her wrists, up across her shoulders, and converge over her sternum in the emblem of the Old Gods. Piercing, predatory moss-green eyes that study rooms with feral vigilance. Hard, calloused palms marked with fine kitchen burns from scrubbing pots and handling roasting spits.
- **Lived Background & Formative Scars**: Raised in a hidden redoubt along the canyon walls of the Karth River. Her father was an herbalist who traded poultices to hill clans; her younger brother, Brennan, was dragged off in iron chains during a Silver-Blood raid on Karthwasten two autumns ago and condemned to the depths of Cidhna Mine. Infiltrated Markarth under the guise of an impoverished scullery maid at the Silver-Blood Inn, gathering troop rotations, supply bills, and mining rosters for the resistance in the crags.
- **Concrete External Want vs. Deep Internal Need**:
  - *External Want*: Break the iron locks of Cidhna Mine, liberate her brother Brennan and the imprisoned clan-chiefs, and drive the Silver-Blood mercenaries from the stone valley.
  - *Internal Need*: Reconcile her fierce devotion to her people's ancient freedom with her mounting revulsion toward the bloody, mutilating sacrifices demanded by the elder Hagravens.
- **Core Misbelief**: Believes every outsider—Nord, Imperial, or high-born Breton—regards Reachmen as animals fit only for the lash, and that mercy shown to a foreigner is suicide.
- **Fatal Blind Spot**: Fanatical dedication to blood kin; will walk directly into an obvious tactical trap if she believes it buys her brother Brennan an hour of life.
- **Vulnerability & Breaking Point**: The smell of burning blood or boiling pitch; watching youth subjected to the Hagraven's briar-heart extraction ritual.
- **Tactile Tic**: Traces the jagged blue woad spiral circling her left wrist with her index fingernail whenever forced to flatter or serve a Nord noble.
- **Dialogue Cadence**: Sharp, hushed, and defiant. Speaks the common tongue with hard consonants and rapid, clipped sentences, occasionally dropping into the guttural, archaic idioms of the ancient Reach language.
- **Internal Contradiction**: Ready to slit an imperial courier's throat in a back alley for a pouch of mining schematics, yet risks her life smuggling winter fever bark to the Nord beggars huddled in the freezing gutters of the Warrens.
- **Sexual Dynamic**: Fierce, dominant, and ceremonial. Views physical intimacy as a solemn covenant of flesh, blood, and shared intent. Uses cold ritual paint, commanding touch, and intense physical containment—pinning wrists, biting skin, setting a relentless, demanding cadence. Softens into desperate, quivering vulnerability only when certainty shatters and authentic trust is earned.
